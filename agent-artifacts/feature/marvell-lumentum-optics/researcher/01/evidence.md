# Evidence: Marvell to acquire Lumentum (researcher 01)

The filings confirm every headline term in the commission: 4.500 Marvell shares per Lumentum share, an implied enterprise value of $115 billion, about $1,132 per Lumentum share, a 67/33 ownership split, more than $1 billion of run-rate cost synergies "primarily COGS", and a $550 billion combined addressable market. They also support the price-against-revenue computation: Lumentum's reported revenue (FY2026 $3,014.0 million; Q1 FY2027 $1,262.6 million; Q2 guidance $1.575 to $1.625 billion) and its own March 2026 target model ($8 billion annualized at $2 billion a quarter) are all in primary documents. Marvell's own drawing of which parts in a pluggable module and in co-packaged optics are Marvell's and which are Lumentum's is in the deal presentation (slides 13 and 14), and it matches each company's 10-K product descriptions. Two things shape the story. First, the "$100 billion" in Lumentum's employee FAQ against the release's "$115 billion" is reproducible from the filings' share counts (see Contradictions), and it also exposes that the dollar price floats with Marvell's stock because the ratio is fixed. Second, the $550 billion is not a sum of anything the filings show: Marvell's $400 billion includes $235 billion of custom silicon, Lumentum's $90 billion-plus is total industry optical AI revenue from third-party forecasts, and Marvell's CEO says on the call that $60 billion of the $550 billion is incremental. The evidence is thin on: Marvell's electro-optics or interconnect revenue in dollars (not disclosed), the Lumentum 10-Q for the September 2026 quarter (not yet filed), any named independent analyst view of the price, exact closing prices after announcement (only secondary quote boxes), the Inphi comparison Marvell's CEO invokes, and the contents of the regulatory-approval schedule (Parent Disclosure Letter section 7.1(c), not public).

Conventions used below. All Lumentum fiscal years end near 30 June (FY2026 ended 27 June 2026; Q1 FY2027 ended 26 September 2026). Marvell's fiscal year ends near 31 January (Q2 FY2027 ended 1 August 2026). SEC addresses are written in their canonical form without leading zeros in the CIK. The zero-padded form in the brief returns a 301 redirect to the same document. SEC fetches needed a descriptive User-Agent header. No source was gated beyond that. Fetches of company web pages and news pages (marked "read via summarizing fetch") returned a model-written summary, not the verbatim page, so any wording from those must be re-opened before it is quoted.

## Contradictions

1. **$115 billion against "approximately $100 billion".** The release says "implied enterprise value of $115 billion ... approximately $1,132 per share". The Lumentum employee FAQ says "valued at approximately $100 billion". Neither document defines its measure. What the filings let a reader compute (my arithmetic from the Merger Agreement Section 4.1(c)(i) as of 29 October 2026 and the 26 September 2026 balance sheet):
   - Lumentum common shares only: 90,684,914 times $1,132 = $102.7 billion. This is close to "approximately $100 billion" and is the likeliest source of the FAQ figure, but nothing in the FAQ says so.
   - Common plus Series A preferred (1:1 into common) = 93.56 million shares, $105.9 billion.
   - Adding unvested awards (203,017 options; 1,565,041 RSUs; 693,004 PSUs at target) = 96.02 million shares, $108.7 billion.
   - Adding the 7,271,887 shares issuable on conversion of Lumentum's convertible notes = 103.29 million shares, $116.9 billion.
   - $116.9 billion less $1,979.6 million of cash and short-term investments = $114.9 billion, matching $115 billion. That treats the notes as converted, so their carrying value drops out of net debt. Treating them as debt instead gives $116.3 billion. The press release gives no bridge, so this match is a reconstruction.
   - Cross-check: 103.29 million times 4.5 = 464.8 million Marvell shares. Marvell's fully diluted count excluding warrants from its Section 4.2(c)(i) is 925.7 million (877,987,882 common; 2,000,000 preferred at 10.8890 = 21.8 million; 2.8 million options, 19.3 million RSUs, 3.8 million PSUs). 464.8 / (464.8 + 925.7) = 33.4%, matching "approximately 33%". Without the convertible-note shares the split is 31.8%. So the 33% corresponds to the fully diluted count including the notes.
   Neither document says which count or which Marvell price the FAQ's "$100 billion" uses. The common-share arithmetic ($102.7 billion) and a post-leak Marvell price (see 2) are both consistent with it, and I cannot tell them apart. The writer should report the two figures, say the release's is an enterprise value, and not claim a reason for the FAQ's number.
2. **The dollar price moved before it was announced.** The $1,132 equals 4.5 times about $251.56, and slide 9 of the deal deck labels Marvell's "undisturbed share price" on 28 October as "~$252". Slide 21 values the combined enterprise at "over ~$275B ... Based on October 30, 2026". At the 28 October price, Marvell's equity alone (925.7 million shares times $251.56) is about $233 billion and the deal is $115 billion, which sums to well above $275 billion. The two can only be reconciled if Marvell's price had fallen sharply by 30 October. Secondary reports say it fell about 12% to 14% on 29 October on the Bloomberg report, and a Yahoo quote box shows MRVL near $171 on 2 November. At $171.41 the exchange ratio is worth 4.5 times $171.41 = $771.35 per Lumentum share, or $79.7 billion of fully diluted equity value (about $77.7 billion after cash). The same Yahoo quote box shows LITE at $684.40, about 11% below the $771.35. The filings do not report either closing price. The writer must treat "$115 billion" as the value at one date's Marvell price, which the release itself dates to 28 October.
3. **Lumentum's "TAM" and Marvell's are different things, and the $550 billion is not their sum.** Slide 11 shows $400B (Marvell) next to $90B+ (Lumentum) and $550B combined. $400B plus $90B is $490B, and Murphy fills the gap on the call: "incremental of $60 billion". The Marvell $400 billion includes $235 billion of custom compute (Investor Day, page 31), which is not Lumentum's market. Lumentum's $90 billion-plus is "industry revenue" for "AI datacom transceivers as defined by LightCounting" plus OCS (OFC deck, slide 30), meaning the whole module's revenue including the DSP, driver and TIA content that Marvell sells and counts in its own $65 billion interconnect TAM. No document says whether the two figures overlap. I did not find a statement that they are additive, other than the slide-11 arrow. That overlap is an inference of mine, not an established fact. Hurlston's email also says Lumentum will "more than quadruple" its TAM, which holds ($90B to $550B is 6.1 times).
4. **Lumentum's reported pace against the March target model.** The OFC deck (slide 38, 17 March 2026) lists "$1.25B Quarterly Revenue" as a target case with 33 to 37% operating margin. Lumentum reported $1.2626 billion with a 42.3% non-GAAP operating margin in Q1 FY2027, ahead of its own target case, and Q2 guidance ($1.6 billion midpoint, 45.5% operating margin) is between the $5B-annualized and $8B-annualized cases. This does not break the angle, but a writer who describes the price as "requiring" the $8 billion case should note that Lumentum had already passed the first case.
5. **Counter-evidence to a "peak of shortage" reading.** Arya's question puts the case that the price assumes shortage-driven pricing persists. Murphy replies the shortage is industry-wide and the volume is design-win driven. Lumentum's own documents say the shortage is real: the 10-K says demand is "outpacing our current supply", and the OFC deck lists backlog of more than 2 years for EML and CW laser chips. The 10-K also shows Customer A at 26.6% and Customer B at 15.0% of FY2026 revenue, so about 42% of revenue sat with two unnamed customers. Nothing I read says the shortage ends; nothing proves it persists.
6. **Marvell's pro forma figure is not a trailing-revenue figure.** Slide 20 takes Marvell's FQ3'27 guidance midpoint annualized ($12.6B) and Lumentum's Q1 FY2027 annualized ($5.1B), and the footnote says pro forma uses "Marvell's guidance provided on August 27, 2026". Murphy's "over $21 billion" uses Lumentum's Q2 guidance and Marvell "consensus". Both are run-rate figures from one quarter, annualized. A trailing-four-quarter revenue figure for Lumentum is $3,742.8 million, 26% below the $5.05 billion run rate.
7. **"$5 billion win": commitments or revenue.** Lumentum's release says "purchase commitments exceeding $5 billion". Hurlston on the call says "we expect to generate over $5 billion in revenue". These are different claims, and neither names the customer or the period.
