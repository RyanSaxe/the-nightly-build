# Commission: feature/lonvo-z-hereditary-angioedema

Series: feature, open, template `article`, strict. 600 to 1,200 words, two to
four flex sections, at least five sources. A five-minute read built around
visuals.

## Subject

Intellia Therapeutics' lonvoguran ziclumeran (lonvo-z, formerly NTLA-2002), a
single-infusion in vivo CRISPR therapy that knocks out the KLKB1 gene in the
liver to lower plasma kallikrein in hereditary angioedema (HAE). The news hook:
on 12 to 13 November 2026 Intellia presented further HAELO phase 3 and phase
1/2 data at ACAAI 2026, including an 81% reduction against patients' prior
long-term prophylaxis and an 89% attack-rate reduction across 93 patients
followed for up to four years.

## The question

How much of what a one-time edit promises can the published data show? The
piece sets lonvo-z's attack reductions beside the chronic prophylaxis that
patients already take on the same pathway (lanadelumab, berotralstat,
garadacimab, sebetralstat on demand, as the record supports), and asks what the
trial design can and cannot establish: placebo-controlled duration versus the
lifelong claim, the comparison against prior prophylaxis being within-patient
and unblinded, and the permanence and safety questions that come with an
irreversible edit. Intellia's other in vivo program, nex-z for ATTR, had a
clinical hold after a liver-related death; the researcher establishes exactly
what happened and when, from Intellia's filings and FDA statements, and whether
any HAELO safety finding bears on it.

## Required contribution

Do something the press releases do not: put the HAELO endpoints, the ACAAI
analyses and the comparator trials' own primary endpoints on one comparable
footing, with each figure's denominator and period stated, and show where the
comparison stops being like for like.

## Orchestrator decisions after research (researcher/01)

The angle stands. Corrections to the commission's premises, which the
evidence record governs:

- Nex-z: FDA's clinical hold (29 October 2025) came before the death
  (5 November 2025). The investigator-reported cause was septic shock from a
  perforated duodenal ulcer, in a course that included acute liver injury; no
  document attributes the death to the liver injury. Both holds are lifted.
  Report the sequence exactly and do not imply causation the filings do not
  state. Keep it to the share of the piece it earns: it bears on the safety
  question of an irreversible edit, and HAELO itself shows no liver signal.
- Timing: the release is dated 12 November, posters were 13 November, and two
  oral sessions are scheduled for 14 November. Do not report the content of
  the 14 November sessions.
- 87% (primary, all attacks, weeks 5 to 28), 91% (moderate or severe) and the
  ACAAI 89% (within-patient, 93 patients, two trials, no comparator) are
  different endpoints. Every percentage in the article carries its endpoint.
- The four-year claim rests on a shrinking uncontrolled cohort; show the
  at-risk numbers wherever durability is claimed.
- The source assets in the SEC-filed deck may be captured; the pathway is an
  original drawing from the label mechanism text.
