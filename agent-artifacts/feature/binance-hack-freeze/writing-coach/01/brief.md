Domain and genre: an explanatory feature on a crypto-security incident and the
on-chain mechanism that decides what can be recovered, for readers with CS/math
degrees and production engineering experience. The register is a clear, exact
explainer who respects a technical reader: it states the finding first, walks the
mechanism, and lets a comparison or a number carry the point without flourish. It
can be dry-witted but never decorative. Find at least three exemplars among writers
the field rates for explaining financial plumbing, security incidents, or on-chain
mechanics with rigor and a human voice. Study them with web tools; do not use this
repository or its prior articles as a voice source.

Take care with any exemplar writing about this very hack or about crypto hacks in
general: quote it for how it writes (how it opens on a finding, how it explains a
mechanism, how it handles an unproven attribution fairly), not for its claims about
this incident, since the writer inherits whatever is in this guide. Prefer a piece
on a different incident or mechanism where the voice is just as visible. Verify
every quotation against the raw page word for word and record where you checked it.
Write the "How this piece should sound" direction last, pinned to the passages you
chose, in this subject's own vocabulary (freeze, blocklist, issuer, native asset,
tracing).
