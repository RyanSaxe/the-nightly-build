# Revision 01: rewritten after the owner rejected the first version

The first version's headline, that a generic ReAct loop with GPT-5.5 matched Asta v0
at under half the cost, compared a model from April 2026 with an agent run in August
2025. The piece conceded this after its second figure, and its same-period comparison
pointed the other way: Asta v0 scored 7.6 points higher at 11 times the cost. It also
never said what AstaBench tests or what a score means. A table, a section and half the
dek rested on points per dollar, a ranking the piece itself said favours cheap agents.
Its chart legend still said "dominated" where its text said tie.

The rewrite keeps the subject and changes the argument. Across seven models run under
two general-purpose loops, a model swap moves the overall score about twice as far as
a loop swap, and the model accounts for 89% of the variance. Asta v0's lead over ReAct
on GPT-5 comes mostly from End-to-End Discovery, at many times the reported cost. Token
price and cost per problem are compared by plotting tokens against effective price.
Limits now sit beside the claims they qualify: the seven models span tiers, the two top
models differ by about as much as a loop swap, only three designs have an overall cost,
and Princeton's Holistic Agent Leaderboard agrees on one benchmark and disagrees on
another.

All four figures are new and drawn as inline SVG, so they follow the light and dark
themes and fit a phone. `chart-1.png` and `chart-1.py` are removed because the page no
longer uses them.

Verification: all 24 costed rows were rebuilt from Ai2's raw result files and match the
leaderboard at category level. The editor recomputed the headline ratio under other
averages and with any one model dropped, and it held at 1.8 to 3.0. It falls among the
top two models and varies by category, and the page says so. "More steps or loops"
stays the paper's explanation, attributed to the paper, because the result files hold
no step counts and the logs are gated. Every figure was viewed at desktop and phone
width in both themes.
