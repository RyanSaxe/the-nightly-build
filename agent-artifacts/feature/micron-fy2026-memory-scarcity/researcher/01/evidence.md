# Evidence record

Micron's September 30 report establishes extraordinary Q4 results driven chiefly by higher memory prices, modest bit growth and a large fixed manufacturing cost base. Customer agreements make part of future sales less exposed to a price collapse, but they do not establish permanent margins across the business. Deposits, remaining performance obligations and recognized revenue are separate accounting quantities. The prepared remarks support management's forecast of continued scarcity; they do not independently prove industry demand through 2028. Full Q&A and individual contracts were not obtained. Eight useful primary documents were opened and checked; secondary reporting was used to find the primary claims, not to own figures.

## Contradictions

- The prior article's idea that SCA revenue is booked before a spot-market turn is incorrect if interpreted as accounting recognition. Note14 of the Q3 filing explicitly places customer deposits in liabilities before recognition. FY25 accounting recognizes primarily on control transfer. Q4 deposits are financing cash flow. Say sales are contracted in advance; do not say recognized in advance.
- Scarcity affects conventional DRAM and NAND, not only HBM. In Q4 conventional DRAM margins were higher than HBM; increased HBM mix offset higher cloud-unit pricing. A pure premium-product explanation contradicts management's own decomposition.
- Stronger contracts do not make all projected sales fixed-price. A quarter of estimated SCA revenue remains periodically market-priced; defined bands also cap upside. Over35% is estimated revenue coverage, leaving most anticipated revenue outside SCAs. Do not call every agreement fixed-price or every future dollar protected.
- $32bn commitments, $150bn RPO and $12.7bn deposits received are different values with different definitions. Adding them would double count and confuse liquidity with contracted sales.
- Q3 filing RPO ~$5bn on May28 cannot be compared unqualified with June-call ~$100bn or September-call ~$150bn. The later snapshot includes agreements signed after the balance-sheet date. The $100bn June-call baseline was not established from an opened primary full prepared document in this invocation; omit it unless another agent verifies that exact source.
- Q4 has fourteen weeks. A simple quarter-over-quarter revenue growth comparison overstates the increase in weekly sales. Year-over-year also has an extra week. The magnitudes remain very large after normalizing.
- PC/mobile volume demand is weak: management warns of potential double-digit calendar2026 unit declines. Q4 mobile/client revenue growth came despite lower bit shipments. Higher revenue is not proof that all end markets are buying more devices.
- Q1 FY27 adjusted gross-margin guidance falls to86.25% from Q4's87.0%. Management attributes the dip to manufacturing incentive compensation absorbed in Q4 inventory and sold in Q1, then forecasts later recovery. This is an explanation/forecast, not an accomplished result.
- SCA margin floors above old cycle peaks are management's assertion; no actual floor prices or contract cost structures are disclosed. Historical annual DRAM price changes went as low as high40% declines; NAND low50%. Past price cycles remain a real risk for uncovered production.

## Limits

- Individual SCA agreements, customer identities, floor/ceiling prices, termination clauses, counterparty default protections and cancellation remedies are not disclosed in sources read. Take-or-pay improves enforceability; it does not let us claim impossibility of renegotiation/default/cancellation.
- No full Q&A transcript was obtained. Full prepared primary remarks were obtained and read; do not fabricate analyst questions or call answers.
- Q4 slides link on the IR home page was exposed but web click failed; guessed filename returned404 and is not a proposed citation. All required call topics are present in the complete prepared remarks.
- No audited FY26 10-K was established at publication-time access. Use the furnished unaudited release and freshest available quarter/annual policies with dates stated.
- No exact Q4 HBM revenue, realized SCA average price, cost per bit or future margin floor was disclosed. Avoid charts implying these values.
- Long-term AI/server demand and scarcity through2028 are company forecasts, not independent demand measurements.
- The useful eight-source set is all primary, mostly Micron; two technical studies are vendor-authored, one with Meta coauthors. This cannot independently validate management's industry forecasts.
- Web source word limits are200 each for all eight sources; enforce cumulative limits per document, not per open/ref ID. Primary numeric facts and independently calculated relationships should be stated in original prose. Avoid lengthy source-derived narrative or quotes.
