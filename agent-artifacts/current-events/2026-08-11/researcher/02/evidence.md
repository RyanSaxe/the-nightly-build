## Contradictions

- **The ruling is dated 7 August, not 10–11 August.** McElroy signed the Memorandum
  and Order on Friday, 7 August 2026; independent reporting landed on 10 August. Like
  the first pass's ballroom and Cook items, this moved earlier in the week and is a
  build-on/continuing story for an 11 August brief, not a same-day development. The
  editor should decide whether it belongs, and the item must carry the true date.
- **"97,000 people" is a plaintiffs' allegation, not a court finding.** The order
  records the figure as the plaintiffs' estimate (p.4, citing ECF No. 30-1 at 9); the
  court did not adopt or verify it, and expressly declined to reach the merits of the
  harm because it decided the case on the procedural (notice-and-comment) defect. Any
  use of 97,000 must attribute it to the plaintiffs.
- **A vacatur, not an injunction — HUD can try again.** The court denied a permanent
  injunction and said HUD "may attempt to issue yet another NOFO that contains these
  conditions after undergoing the requisite notice-and-comment process." The block is
  procedural and reversible, not a substantive bar on the policy; a headline calling
  it a defeat of the overhaul overstates it.
- **The court did not rule on the Housing First policy itself, or on the OMB/Vought
  funding conditions.** It decided only that HUD skipped required procedure and
  declined to address the substantive and constitutional challenges "at this time."
- **This is a second, distinct ruling.** It is separate from the June 29, 2026 order
  that vacated HUD's 2025 (November/December) NOFOs in the related 25-cv-626/636
  cases; the first pass's discarded California AG source documented that earlier line.
  Do not conflate the two.
- **Lisa Cook lead did not clear.** As of 10–11 August the Cook matter is unchanged
  from the first pass: a 5 August notice that Trump was "considering" removal, with a
  window to 26 August. No removal, filing, or response moved on 10–11 August, and I
  found no openable owning primary (the letter). It is not added as a new item.
