Focus: A selective US news brief (4-6 items, shortread) for a well-read reader
who already knows the day's headlines. Study exemplars of sharp, non-partisan
news-brief writing where each item's headline states the development and the

sentence items that add the consequence, not a recap. Record the banned dek
