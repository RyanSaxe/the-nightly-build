# Researcher brief · 01

## Inputs

- `editorial-direction.md`
- `commission.md`
- `writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md`
- The eight selected source URLs named in `commission.md`.

## Output

Write `evidence.md` in this directory. Record the source title, publisher,
stable URL, primary/secondary kind, date, and the exact facts each source can
support. Separate reported fact from synthesis. Flag any claim that must be
attributed or any uncertainty the writer must retain.

## Proof required

Open every source before recording it. For each of the four items, establish
one primary record and one independent account. Confirm the procedural posture
of both court items and the BLS dates and units. Do not use a forecast or an
unverified battlefield claim unless it is in the approved sources and marked
as such.
