## Guidance to set

Define a compact voice guide for this four-item morning brief. It should keep
the prose wire-like but analytical: concrete development first, one clean
consequence, and a caveat where the source is a party's or agency's account.
Set paragraph length, attribution, treatment of interim court orders, number
style, and a firm ban on generic transitions, unearned stakes, and newsroom
self-reference. Do not invent a recurring sign-off or copy an older opener.
