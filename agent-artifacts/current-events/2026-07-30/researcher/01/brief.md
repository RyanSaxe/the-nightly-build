# Researcher brief — current-events/2026-07-30 (brief template)

## Task
Establish, from live sources, the most consequential US developments on/around
2026-07-29 and the overnight into 2026-07-30, for a 4-6 item news brief. For
every item the writer may use, supply **exactly one primary** (the ruling,
filing, dataset, official statement, or a party's statement about itself) and
**at least one independent secondary** account. Prefer a reputable US newsroom
for the secondary; use the primary record regardless of country.

## Constraints
- min_sources 5 overall. Read each source; verify every number and quote against
  the primary that owns it. Record primary/secondary + a locator per citation.
- A 403/paywall is gated, not dead — never record an unverified URL. Every URL
  the writer cites must resolve.
- The GDP Q2 advance estimate releases 8:30am ET July 30 — after this run's
  clock (~04:11 ET). Treat it as not yet released; do not use a number for it.
- Non-repetition: do not re-run the 07-28 SCOTUS voter-list item, the 07-27
  Rogoff firing suit, or the 07-26 Iran-escalation "officials say" item. A
  genuine new development in an ongoing story is fine.

## Angle to test / break
Is a Middle East war now bleeding into the US economy, and is Washington
simultaneously shifting costs onto households? Find the contradictions: is the
Fed's hold dovish or a hawkish hold; does "confidence fell" overstate a small
move; does the CMS change actually raise premiums or merely could.

## Output
Write only `evidence.md` in this directory: one summary paragraph, then Sources,
Contradictions, Numbers, Source assets, Discarded.
