# Writing-coach brief — current-events/2026-07-30 (brief template)

## What this is
A nightly US news **brief**: 4-6 short items, each a *judgment about why a
development matters*, written for a reader who already saw the headline. Wire-
service register. Not recaps, not essays.

## Domain and genre to study
News-brief / wire "judgment" writing: the daily or weekly roundup where a
skilled desk compresses a development into a few sentences that carry the number
and the caveat the headline dropped. Study writers the field itself rates for
this exact move (turning a filing, a rate decision, or an official statement
into a paragraph that says what it means), not opinion columnists at length.

## What the writer needs from you
A short, practical voice guide that will change sentences in *this* piece:
- how to open an item on the development, not on scene-setting;
- how to state a judgment (why it matters) without self-grading or handing the
  point back to the reader;
- how to carry a number with a comparison the reader already holds;
- how to keep four short items feeling like one paper, not four styles.

## Hard constraints (from the house floor and template identity)
- Each item stands alone; the reader never needs a longer article.
- No manufactured punchlines, no "the real story is", no closing line that
  lectures the reader, no "X is the whole Y" mold.
- One name per thing, reused exactly. Period is the default mark.
- Do not coin catchphrases or reusable lines; extract transferable craft only.

## Recently used, do not reuse (from the commission)
The last three current-events briefs led on Trump-vs-court legal filings
(SCOTUS voter-list 07-28, Rogoff firing suit 07-27) and an Iran-escalation
"officials say" item (07-26). Avoid the "officials say / according to a person
familiar" hedged-attribution opener and the emergency-application explainer
shape those pieces used. Vary item openers and per-item shape.

## Output
Write only `voice-guide.md` in this directory. Cite >=3 real, respected brief/
wire/judgment writers with real piece URLs, each line beginning `Source: https://`.
