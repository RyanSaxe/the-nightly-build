## Contradictions

- **Oath Keepers — number of defendants.** Accounts disagree on how many
  defendants the 2026-08-04 order covered. NPR: Rhodes "and five other" members
  (six). U.S. News: Rhodes "and eight other defendants" (nine). CNN frames it as
  the final Jan. 6 case, dismissing charges against nine people. The primary
  docket order (1:22-cr-00015) resolves the exact count and named defendants; the
  writer should cite the order's own list, not a secondary tally. Do not state a
  count the order does not.
- **Oath Keepers — "dismiss" vs "vacate."** Secondary accounts variously say the
  DOJ moved to dismiss the indictment and to vacate the convictions. The
  operative posture: sentences were commuted in January 2025, and the 2026 order
  ends the case (dismissal). The writer should track the order's own verb.
- **Burke Law Group — immigration expertise.** NOTUS reports no staff attorney
  lists immigration as a specialty; NPR/ABC report the firm has "only two"
  attorneys who specialize in immigration and asylum. These are not identical
  claims. Attribute each to its source; do not assert a single count as fact.
- **Burke contract — "$150M" scope.** Reported as a $150M contract; at least one
  account frames it as $150M "per year" over a one-year base period (2026-08-15
  to 2027-08-14). The Federal Register notice owns the exact ceiling and period;
  cite it, and avoid "$150M per year" unless the notice says so.
- **Children covered.** Secondary reporting states the Burke contract covers
  "about 1,800 children" currently in ORR shelters, versus Acacia's 20,000-plus.
  The 1,800 figure is secondary (not yet found in the primary notice); mark it as
  reported, not verified against the primary, or omit.
- **Somalia TPS — population affected.** Some coverage cites roughly 1,100 Somali
  TPS holders; broader pieces cite 1.3 million TPS holders across all 17
  designated countries. These are different denominators. Use the Somalia-specific
  figure only if confirmed against USCIS/court filings; the ~1,100 figure is
  secondary here.
- **Michigan primary — figures are unofficial.** The 48.5%/47.5% split is a
  near-final network tally (~98.8% reported), not the canvassed state result.
  Present as "near-final" and cite the SOS portal for the official count.
- **Silica rule enforcement dates.** Accounts give April 14, 2025 (original coal
  compliance date), an April 2025 appellate stay, and an interim delay to August
  2025. Use the Federal Register documents for exact dates; secondary timelines
  vary.
