around Friday, 14 August 2026: events that change law, public policy, public
institutions, or people's material conditions. Bring more candidates than the
brief will use (aim for eight to ten), so the writer can select the strongest

- At least one independent account from a reputable newsroom; prefer a US

  continuation of a thread the recent front pages already carried (the commission

a single short candidate item, flagged, since the unbiased desk owns it tonight;
and route field-level technology news to tech-news, keeping here only stories
whose public/legal/policy consequences are the news.

Confirm every URL resolves to the source's own page. Record in Contradictions any
candidate where the primary and the reporting disagree on a material fact, so the
writer can weigh it. The brief needs at least five sources total across the items
it ultimately runs, with each item carrying its own primary and independent
account.
