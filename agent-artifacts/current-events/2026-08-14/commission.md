# Commission: current-events/2026-08-14

## The brief

The US-focused general-news front page for Friday, 14 August 2026. Four to six
items, selective, each carrying its primary record and at least one independent
account. Favor developments that change law, public policy, public institutions,
or people's material conditions. No topic quota, no routine political theater, no
story that is merely popular. Include an international item only if leaving it
out would make the brief misleading, and size it to its importance.

Item selection is the day's editorial judgment: the researcher surfaces the most
consequential real developments dated on or freshly advancing around 14 August
2026, with sourcing for each, and the writer selects and orders the four to six
that matter most. This commission sets the frame and the coherence constraints;
it does not pick the items.

## Boundaries

- Brief template: 4–6 items, per-item primary [1,1] and secondary [1,null],
  minimum 5 sources total. Every item explains why it matters; no item is a
  bare headline.
- Prefer a reputable US newsroom for independent reporting when quality is
  comparable; use the primary record regardless of country; use non-US reporting
  when it holds the original reporting or is closer to the event.
- Each item's display text (its heading/claim) meets `spec/headlines.md`: state
  the finding with its actors named, not a topic label.

## Recent habits not to inherit

Recent leads are single specific claims, which is right; the failure to avoid is
a house pattern in how items open or close. Vary item construction, keep each
item's first sentence carrying its own facts (no dangling referents for a reader
who arrives from a link), and check headings and the lead against the recent
front pages so the brief does not read as the same template refilled.
