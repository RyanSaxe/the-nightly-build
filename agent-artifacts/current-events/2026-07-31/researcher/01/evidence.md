### Item 3 — Q2 2026 GDP advance estimate

8. **U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, "GDP (Advance Estimate), 2nd Quarter
   2026," Report BEA 26-35** (`https://www.bea.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/gdp2q26-adv.pdf`),
   embargoed until 8:30 a.m. EDT, Thursday, July 30, 2026. **Primary** — BEA's
   own release, full 6-page PDF read directly. Establishes exact figures (all
   seasonally adjusted annual rates, % change from preceding quarter unless
   noted): **real GDP +1.5% in Q2 2026, down from +2.1% in Q1 2026** (0.4% at a
   non-annualized quarterly rate). Contributors to the increase: consumer
   spending, investment, exports; partly offset by a decrease in government
   spending; imports (a subtraction in GDP math) increased. Real final sales to
   private domestic purchasers (consumer spending + private fixed investment):
   **+3.9% in Q2, vs. +1.7% in Q1**. Price index for gross domestic purchases:
   **+5.7% in Q2, vs. +3.6% in Q1**. PCE price index: **+5.1% in Q2, vs. +4.6%
   in Q1**. Core PCE price index (ex food/energy): **+3.4% in Q2, vs. +4.4% in
   Q1** — core decelerated even as the broader PCE and gross-domestic-purchases
   price indexes accelerated. Current-dollar GDP: **+7.9%**. Technical notes:
   consumer-spending growth was led by prescription drugs, new light trucks, and
   furniture; investment growth was led by equipment and software/R&D, partly
   offset by falling inventory investment and nonresidential structures; the
   decline in government spending was driven by federal nondefense spending,
   specifically Strategic Petroleum Reserve crude-oil sales (an accounting
   effect: SPR sales are deducted from government consumption expenditure but
   show up elsewhere in GDP, so this particular federal-spending "decrease" has
   no net effect on the topline number — a caveat a naive read of the headline
   would miss). Next release: August 26, 2026 (second estimate). Locator: pages
   1-2 (headline figures), page 4-5 (technical notes on sources of change).

9. **Fox Business, "US economy Q2 2026 advance"**
   (`https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/us-economy-q2-2026-advance`).
   **Secondary** — opened and read. Confirms the 1.5% figure against a 2.1%
   consensus forecast (LSEG poll) and 2.1% Q1 print; first-half 2026 growth
   averaged about 1.8%. Named-economist quotes: **Gregory Daco**, Chief

   "caveat the headline dropped": a below-forecast headline number driven partly
   by temporary inventory and import effects, not a uniform slowdown.
