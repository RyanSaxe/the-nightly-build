# Evidence: current-events/2026-08-15 (researcher 01)

The record supports five strong candidates with a primary or near-primary
record plus at least one independent account each, and two softer candidates
the writer should treat cautiously. The strongest items by institutional
weight are the second dismissal of Nevada's fake-elector prosecution, the
federal ruling clearing border-wall construction across the Tohono O'odham
Nation's reservation, and Virginia's new 2-mg THC hemp limit taking effect
today with a federal judge's same-week refusal to pause it — all three carry
a state or federal document behind them (a state agency notice for the hemp
item; judicial holdings, quoted verbatim, for the other two). Mangione's
federal guilty plea and the Kennedy Center board's vote to close its main
building are each solid on one reputable independent account (NPR) carrying
on-the-record government or congressional quotes, but I could not open the
underlying federal press release or Kennedy Center statement myself — both
government pages returned 401/403 to every fetch attempt, a gating pattern
consistent across DOJ, CourtListener, and kennedy-center.org for this
session, not evidence the documents don't exist. The evidence is thin on two
fronts the commission flagged: I found no dated August 15 action on the
measles elimination review (only a "mid-August" meeting window, unconfirmed
to any single day) and no fresh August 15 fact on the carrier rotation beyond
what NPR reported August 14, which the commission has already marked
covered. Do not use those two threads as-is. The hack-back memo produced
nothing new on August 15; I did not pursue it further per the coordination
