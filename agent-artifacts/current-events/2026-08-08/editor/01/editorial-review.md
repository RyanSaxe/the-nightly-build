## Cut

One direct cut, in item 1. The revision sentence closed on "turning a spring
that had looked solid into a stall," a metaphor that echoed the item's opener
("The American jobs engine stalled in July") and restated the point already
made by "The steeper damage sat in the revisions." The clause carried no fact
the number and its framing did not already deliver, so it failed the
earns-its-place test. Removed; the material meaning survives intact.

Worst tell surveyed: none blocking. "The American jobs engine stalled" is a
mild worn metaphor but functions as the licensed consequence-first lead and
earns its slot. One repeated cadence exists — items 2 and 3 both end on a
"..., [Source] reported" attribution tag — but each is a correct,
voice-guide-licensed attribution of its secondary, and reshaping either would
be new prose that risks blurring the sourcing the guide prizes; left as is,
noted, not required. Grammar and syntax in prose, display text, and the stat
strip are clean. No prompt leakage: no planning labels, selection rules,
self-reference, or assignment-fulfilled claims survive into the copy.
