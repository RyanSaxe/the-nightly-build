# Writing-coach brief: current-events/2026-08-02 (01)

## Your job
Produce `voice-guide.md` here for a `brief`: a 4-6 item US news roundup where
each item is a compact judgment about why a development matters. Wire-service
discipline, house-paper analysis.

## Begin with these inputs
- This brief; `../../commission.md`; `../../editorial-direction.md` (register,
  brief identity, headline standard). Do not restate their rules.

## Study the best
Study at least three exemplars of the form the field rates: the compressed
analytical news item that adds the number and the caveat the headline dropped
(e.g. the best of a serious paper's briefing/leader-note writing, or a
respected newsletter that files short items with a real judgment each). Prefer
primary pieces. Do not study prior Nightly Build briefs for voice.

## Settle for this writer
- How to write an item that gives a development *and* why it matters in a few
  sentences without recap, hedging, or a handoff-to-the-reader closer.
- Item headline discipline (subject-verb-surprise; no colon-subtitle tell; no
  Betteridge question).
- How to advance a running story ("when a story you have covered develops, say
  so and build on it") without re-explaining it.
- Diction: name the actor, give the figure, drop the throat-clearing.
- How to vary item shape across 4-6 items so the brief does not read stamped.

## Constraints
How to write, not what to say. No catchphrases or reusable kickers. End with
`Recently used, do not reuse:` listing habits to avoid (macro/Fed lead by
reflex, formulaic item kickers, hedged-contrast deks, closers that hand the
point to the reader).

## Output
Write only `voice-guide.md`. Return `DONE writing-coach <path>`.
