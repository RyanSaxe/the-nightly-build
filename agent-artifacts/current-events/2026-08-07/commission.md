## Assignment
The US-focused general-news front page for Friday, 2026-08-07. Rolling brief on
the `brief` template. Select the day's 4-6 most consequential US developments
by the series test: events that change law, public policy, public institutions,
or people's material conditions. No topic quota. Routine political theater and
merely-popular stories do not qualify. Include an international item only if
omitting it would make the brief misleading, sized to its importance.

research in tech-news.
