## Contradictions

- **Author count (commission vs. primaries).** The commission cites
  "~370,000 authors." No opened primary states an author count. The settlement
  administrator and Authors Guild quantify the class by works (482,460 eligible;
  440,490 claimed) and by notice reach ("nearly 595,000 potential class
  members" contacted). Do not publish "370,000 authors" as fact; write in works,
  or attribute an author estimate to a named source that owns it.
- **Works count.** Commission and some coverage (TechCrunch) say ~500,000 works;
  the owning figure is 482,460 eligible works (Authors Guild, from the
  administrator). Use 482,460 (or 440,490 claimed) with the source.
- **Per-work payment.** Reported as ~$2,931.62 base before interest (Authors
  Guild, closest to the administrator's Plan of Allocation), ~$3,000 (rounded,
  settlement site and TechCrunch), and ~$3,100 (some coverage). The base figure
  is an estimate that rises with accrued interest and falls with costs; state it
  as "about $3,000 per work" and cite the base figure, not a false-precision
  number.
- **Settlement freshness.** Final approval and judgment were 2026-07-20, not
  2026-08-07. The genuine 08-07 hook is the move into distribution / imminent
  initial payments (FAQ estimated ~2026-08-10, contingent on the judgment
  becoming effective and appeals resolving). Frame it as the payout phase
  beginning, not as the approval — the approval is two weeks stale.
- **July jobs report is unverified.** At research time the BLS release URL still
  served June data; only the consensus forecast (~80–85k, 4.2%) and the June
  baseline (+57k, with April/May revised down a combined 74k) are established.
  If the actual July figures cannot be verified from the BLS release at publish,
  do not run specific July numbers.
- **Two distinct wind rulings in the news.** Judge Immergut's 2026-08-06 order
  concerns the Pentagon's freeze on wind-project *reviews* (onshore, nationwide,
  APA deadline violation). Separate coverage references a "Revolution Wind"
  (offshore) matter. Do not conflate them; this item is the Immergut
  review-freeze injunction only.
- **Boundary check (do not cross into unbiased/tech-news).** This wind item is a
  district-court APA injunction on permitting *reviews*; it is NOT the DC
  Circuit clean-energy-*funding* termination ruling reserved to the unbiased
  piece. The Anthropic item is a law/business story, not a tech-field
  development. Both sit correctly in this lane, but the orchestrator should
  confirm the wind item does not overlap the unbiased impoundment/funding angle.
