## Authorized work

## What this brief is
The US-focused general-news front page for 2026-08-04: 4-6 selected items, each
explaining why it matters, every item carrying at least one primary record and at
least one independent account. This is a *rolling news* piece, so the subject is
the day's actual developments — selection happens in research against live
reporting, not from a pre-chosen topic. The researcher assembles the candidate
day's-news set from reputable US reporting and the primary records behind it; the
writer selects and orders the final 4-6.

## Selection standard (from the series prompt)
- Favor developments that change law, public policy, public institutions, or
  people's material conditions. No topic quota. Routine political theater and
  merely-popular stories do not qualify.
- Include an international story only when leaving it out would make the brief
  misleading, sized to its importance.
- Put technology here only when its *public consequences* are the news;
  developments in the field itself belong to Tech News (see non-overlap below).
- Independent reporting: prefer a reputable US newsroom of comparable quality;
  use the primary record regardless of country; use non-US reporting when it
  holds important original reporting or is closer to the event.

## Sourcing
Brief template: 4-6 items; per item primary exactly 1, secondary 1+. Every number
verified against the primary that owns it; every name/title/role exact. Accusations
need two independent confirmations. Confirm every URL resolves to the source's own
page.

## Template and policy
- Template: `brief` (fixed). `min_sources: 5` overall; per-item primary [1,1],

- Production policy (balanced): editor required, high effort, model inherit.

## Boundaries — do not repeat, and non-overlap with this edition
- Recent current-events slugs are dated (2026-07-26 … 2026-08-03). Do not re-file
  a story already covered as new; carry a genuinely new development or a new,
  sourced turn in a running story.
- **Non-overlap with tech-news (same run):** a pure AI/technology development
  (new model, chip, security research) belongs in tech-news, not here. It appears
  here only if its public/policy consequences are the news.
- **Non-overlap with unbiased/iran-war-powers (same run):** if the Iran conflict
  is a lead item, report *what happened* (facts, primary records). Do NOT argue
  the war-powers legality question — that is the unbiased desk's piece this
  edition. A one-line factual note that the authorization question is contested is
  the ceiling here.
- Headlines/deks: state the finding with actors named; avoid colon subtitles, the
  comma-triad dek, and scaffolding subheads. Check recent deks before settling.
