# Evidence: current-events/2026-08-04 (01)

This record assembles seven candidate items for the 2026-08-04 US general-news
brief so the writer can select the final 4-6. The evidence is strongest on four
items whose primary records I read directly and whose figures I verified against
the owning source: the Blanche attorney-general nomination (DOJ order plus Senate
Judiciary schedule), the mail-in-voting emergency appeal (Supreme Court docket
26A124), the multistate Cyclospora outbreak (CDC outbreak pages), and the
California DROP data-broker deletion obligations that began 1 August (CPPA). Two
items carry usable but softer news hooks: the Section 232 pharmaceutical tariff

## Contradictions

- **Cyclospora case count (material).** CDC (primary, both outbreak pages,
  updated 2026-08-03) says at least ~1,947 laboratory-confirmed cases in 9
  states with 98 hospitalizations. Several secondary summaries (and one search
  index of US News / mappr / Newsweek) report 18,000+ "confirmed or suspected"
  cases across 45 states, 423 hospitalizations, and a Michigan-alone figure of
  ~11,234. These cannot both be true: a national confirmed count of ~1,900 in 9
  states is incompatible with 11,234 in Michigan alone. CDC owns the national
  count. Treat 1,947 / 9 states / 98 hospitalizations / 2 deaths as the verified
  figures and do NOT file the 18,000/45-state numbers unless a state health
  department primary (e.g., Michigan MDHHS) is opened and reconciled. The two
  deaths are consistent across sources.
- **Source attribution for the outbreak.** CDC names iceberg lettuce from Taylor
  Farms de Mexico (central Mexico). One secondary summary mentioned distribution
  "through Taco Bell locations"; CDC's own text I read names the grower/lettuce,
  not a restaurant chain. Use CDC's attribution; do not assert a named restaurant
  without CDC confirming it.
- **Iran, "are they talking."** Trump says negotiations are underway / imminent;
  Iran's Foreign Ministry (Baghaei) says it is talking only to Oman about the
  strait, not to the US. This is a live factual dispute; report both attributions,
  do not adjudicate.
- **FIRE numbers.** The "13.2% red light" figure (Washington Times) is not on the
  fire.org rankings page I read; it belongs to FIRE's written-policy ratings, a
  different product with a different denominator (~493 institutions vs. 257
  surveyed schools). A writer using the red-light figure must cite the correct
  FIRE primary, not the student-rankings page.
- **Blanche fund "termination."** The order rescinds the fund, but NPR and CNN
  note the written agreement does not prevent a future order from reviving it,
  and Blanche's audit-protection concession was limited to parties of the
  settlement. Report the rescission as done; note the durability caveat rather
  than implying permanence.
