## Contradictions (this round)

- **May 18 vs. May 19 orders — do not conflate.** The order rescinded here is
  the **May 18, 2026** order that *established the fund*. The DOJ press statement
  in the same PDF separately discusses a **May 19, 2026** order "regarding a
  mutual release of claims" that "applies by its terms only retroactively" and
  only to "the named parties in the lawsuit." These are two different orders on
  consecutive dates. The rescission quote and the "May 18, 2026" date belong to
  the fund-establishing order; the release/immunity-scope discussion (the Cornyn
  concern) attaches to the May 19 order. The writer should keep them distinct.
- **"Deliberate" vs. "deliberative."** The prior draft's quoted word
  ("deliberate") is not what the application says ("deliberative"). Corrected
  above.
- Round 01's contradictions (Cyclospora case count, outbreak source attribution,
  Iran "are they talking," FIRE red-light figure, fund durability caveat) stand
