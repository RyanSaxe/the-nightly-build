## Contradictions

- **Flooding death toll (evolving).** NPR (Aug 15) reported "at least 3" dead; by Aug 16
  AP-sourced accounts (NBC, CBS) reported "at least 7," while U.S. News and rollingout.com said
  "at least 6." No source disputes the others; the toll rose across the window. Use "at least 6
  to 7 as of Aug 16" and attribute to the Aug 16 reports.
- **Emergency vs. major disaster (do not conflate).** The Aug 16 action is an *emergency*
  declaration (CBS). A referenced state PDF carries number DR-4882-IN, which is a *major
  disaster* series number. These are distinct FEMA instruments; if the writer cites a
  declaration number, confirm which instrument it belongs to.
- **Gaza item has no owning US primary.** The meeting is sourced to a regional official and a
  Hamas official, both anonymous (NPR). Treat it as reported by parties in a position to know,
  corroborated across NPR, WaPo, AP and Times of Israel, not as an official-record fact.
- **"Monday" meeting date rendering.** Multiple accounts say the Netanyahu meeting is "Monday,"
  which is Aug 17, 2026. One automated read of the NPR page rendered the date as "August 19,"
  which is a Wednesday and therefore inconsistent with "Monday." The day of week ("Monday" =
  Aug 17) is the reliable anchor; the writer should state Monday, Aug 17.
- **Egg Class I upgrade — date-of-week tension.** The Time piece is dated Aug 15 and says the
  upgrade came "Wednesday," and separately "three weeks after" the July 22 recall (≈ Aug 12,
  a Wednesday). Aug 15, 2026 is a Saturday. Record the upgrade as mid-August (on or about
  Aug 12-13), reported Aug 15; do not assert "Aug 15" as the upgrade date.
- **Was mid-August data weak or strong?** One search summary (Bloomberg, Aug 16) framed the
  period as the dollar falling "after weak US economic data." A separate market wire (fxstreet,
  Aug 15) reported July retail sales up 1% (vs. 0.3% expected) and the dollar *rising*. The
  July CPI (Aug 12) is the cleaner, primary-owned data point; the retail-sales/dollar direction
  is contested and should not be asserted without the BLS/Census releases in hand.
