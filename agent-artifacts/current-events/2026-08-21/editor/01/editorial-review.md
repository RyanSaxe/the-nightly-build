# Editorial review — current-events/2026-08-21 (editor 01)

Decision: **APPROVED.** Five items, each on its primary record plus one
independent account, all within the template's 4-6 band and selected on
consequence rather than a quota.

## Fixes made in place

1. **Census item, factual reversal (item 5).** The first paragraph said the
   method "counts anyone the match can't clear as a citizen." That is the
   opposite of what the report does and of the item's own second paragraph:
   an unconfirmed record defaults into the noncitizen count. Changed to
   "counts anyone whose citizenship it can't confirm as a noncitizen," which
   also removes the earlier ambiguity in the "clear" phrasing. This was the
   only error that touched what the item claims a source found, so it was the
   one worth catching.

2. **Debt item, stakes-announcement (item 1).** "The pace is the story:"
   graded the argument instead of making it — the "here's the kicker" family.
   Replaced with a claim in the item's own nouns ("The pace outruns the round
   number"), immediately proved by the March 17 figure that follows.

3. **Planned Parenthood item, stamped closer (item 4).** Trimmed the negative-
   parallelism tail "not settled law" from the last sentence. "A fracture
   already on the record" carries the point without the reflex the commission
   flagged in recent closers.

## Held after review

- The dek breaks the recent "and … on a day that also" / "ruling that" tail
  mold: one main clause with an "as" subordinate, anchored to the debt item's
  own figures.
- Headlines vary in construction (number-led, contrast noun phrase, actor-
  verb-object, "what X couldn't do," bare noun phrase); none restates the dek,
  and only one is the actor-verb-object form recent issues overused.
- "The government lost this case less on the facts than on its own silence"
  reads as negative parallelism but is earned: the misconception (a loss on
  the merits) is real and the correction (a waived compelling-interest
  defense) is the item's substance, named in the next sentence.
- No overlap with Tech News' field angle or Unbiased's data-broker framing.
  The Census item is elections/voter-file, not the Maryland ALPR complaint.
- The Fifth Circuit procedural note in the ghost-gun item and the February
  tariff-ruling reference in the debt item are accurate background the
