# Evidence record: current-events/2026-08-16 (01)

This record supports a US-focused front page for the Saturday-into-Sunday of
15–16 August 2026. It carries eight candidate developments, more than the four to
six the writer will select. Two clear the bar most decisively and are flagged as
leads: the federal government's emergency application to the Supreme Court to
resume White House ballroom construction (a separation-of-powers fight over who
controls the Executive Residence), and a Massachusetts judge lifting her block on
ending Somali Temporary Protected Status (roughly 700 people lose protection from
removal, and the reason is a June Supreme Court precedent that stripped courts of
the power to review such terminations). The record is strongest on the ballroom
docket, the Indiana flooding declaration, and the egg-recall public-health
numbers, where the primary lives at a resolvable public page I read directly. It
is thinner on three items where the owning primary is a court order or an agency
release I could reach only through secondary reporting or a fetch-gated address:
the Somali TPS order (D. Mass.), the IDF/Lebanese Health Ministry figures on the
Lebanon strikes, and the National Firearms Act injunction. Those gaps are named
per item so the writer can prefer the cleanly-sourced candidates or pull the
primary before citing. Several muddled results for a Chicago National Guard
immigration deployment could not be dated to this window and are discarded rather
than surfaced.

## Contradictions

- **Number of Somali TPS holders.** Accounts converge on "roughly 700" nationally
  (Just the News; a separate summary put Minnesota's TPS share at "less than 1%"
  of the state's Somali population). One earlier search summary loosely said
  "roughly 700 Somali nationals live in the U.S.," which conflates the resident
  population with TPS holders and is wrong on its face. Cite the TPS-holder figure
  from the order, not the resident population.
- **Lebanon toll composition.** Times of Israel gives seven dead in Ansar
  (three children, two women) and four in Deir al-Zahrani; some accounts headline
  "three children" without the two women. Total of 11 is consistent across US News
  (AP), ABC, Times of Israel, and Washington Times. The IDF's claim that a Radwan
  battalion commander was among the dead is Israel's attribution; Lebanon's prime
  minister disputed that the dead were military targets. Keep the two attributions
  separate.
- **Ballroom, what is enjoined.** The injunction blocks above-ground ballroom
  construction while allowing below-ground work; a flat "construction halted" would
  overstate it.
- **Egg onset range vs. recall dates.** CDC's illness-onset range (21 Nov 2025 –
  30 Jun 2026) is far wider than the recalled eggs' production window (6 Jun –
  3 Jul 2026), and the FDA states Midwest Poultry Services "does not account for
  all the illnesses in this outbreak." The recall does not explain every case; do
  not imply it does.
