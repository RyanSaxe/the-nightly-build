  Exact language: "The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is amending its regulations to

## Contradictions

- **Charge count for Colin Gray**: AJC reports "29 counts"; ABC News, Fox News's own count
  breakdown, and the arithmetic of the charge categories (2+2+18+5) all support **27 counts**.
  Treat 27 as the reliable figure; if AJC's number is used, flag it.
- **Number injured in the Apalachee shooting**: most contemporaneous reporting on the
  September 4, 2024 shooting (repeated in this week's coverage) says "four dead, seven
  injured" (e.g., ABC News, DA Smith's own quote: "four people are dead, seven are injured").
  A search result also produced "wounded nine others" (in a search-summary description of
  Colt Gray's July 28 sentencing). The DA's own on-record courtroom figure — seven injured —
  should be treated as authoritative over an unattributed aggregator phrasing.
  Denominator/context: four fatalities (two 14-year-old students, two teachers) plus wounded
  is the count that matters for describing the shooting's toll.
- **Whether the Gaza "agreement" is actually agreed**: the Board of Peace/White House frames
  July 30 as a completed, historic agreement for Hamas's "complete disarmament." Israel's own
  government, per Jerusalem Post's diplomatic source, says the 15-point roadmap does not meet
  its demand for "full demilitarization," and Netanyahu's office is reported (Al Jazeera) to
  have separately said the proposal "did not meet Israeli demands." Hamas official Ghazi Hamad
  separately conditions actual disarmament on Israel first halting strikes, withdrawing, and
  increasing aid. All three parties to the "agreement" are on record qualifying or disputing
  it the same week it was announced — this is not a settled fact and any item built on it
  should say so plainly, not narrate it as done.
- No contradiction found between the Fed's own release and Fox Business's account of it, or
  between BEA's release and Fox Business's account of it — both secondary sources track their
  primaries exactly on the figures I checked.
