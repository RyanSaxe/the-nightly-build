# Researcher brief — current-events/2026-08-01 (01)

## Your job
Select and verify the day's 4–6 most consequential US developments (window:
roughly 2026-07-30 through 2026-08-01), then write the evidence record the
writer uses. You do the selection; the writer writes. Ground everything in
sources you actually open and read.

## Exact inputs (start here)
- `agent-artifacts/current-events/2026-08-01/commission.md`
- `agent-artifacts/current-events/2026-08-01/editorial-direction.md`

## Selection standard (from the commission)
Developments that change law, public policy, public institutions, or people's
material conditions. Skip routine political theater and merely-popular stories.
International only if omitting it would mislead. Technology only when its public
consequences are the news. **Do NOT center the mail-in voting executive order** —
Opinion owns it tonight; pick other developments (or, at most, a different
factual facet, briefly).

## Per item, capture
- The **primary** source: the ruling / filing / enacted text / agency or party
  statement / dataset. Exact title, publisher, resolvable URL, date, and the
  specific figure or passage the item will rest on (quote it).
- At least **one independent secondary**: a reputable newsroom's original
  reporting, with URL and the specific corroborating detail. Prefer US newsrooms
  when quality is comparable; use the primary regardless of country.
- One-line "why it matters" (the judgment the item will carry).
- primary/secondary classification with reason for each source.

## Deliverable
`agent-artifacts/current-events/2026-08-01/researcher/01/evidence.md`:
- 4–6 candidate items, each as above, strongest first. Give the writer at least
  one more item than the minimum if the day supports it, so the writer can drop
  the weakest.
- A short note on what you deliberately left out and why (e.g., theater/popular).
- Contradictions/uncertainties (disputed figures, developing stories).
- Confirm every URL resolves; a paywall/403 is gated — find the primary another
  way; never record an unverified URL.

## Constraints
- Verify dates carefully; this brief is dated 2026-08-01 and must reflect real
  developments in the window. Cite only what you read.
- Begin with the named inputs; focused web research only, no repo/archive tour.
- Missing context: `REQUEST researcher <one-sentence need>`.
