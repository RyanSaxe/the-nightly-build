# Evidence: current-events/2026-08-09 (01)

This record supports a Sunday, 9 August 2026 US front page built from developments
dated on or immediately before the date. The strongest, best-sourced items are the
Senate's 50-49 confirmation of Todd Blanche as Attorney General (8 Aug, primary:
Senate roll call), the divided D.C. Circuit ruling upholding the injunction against
White House ballroom construction (7 Aug, primary: the panel opinion), a federal
judge's order enforcing an injunction that keeps the VA's union contract alive on the
day it was set to lapse (7 Aug, primary: the D.R.I. order), and the July Employment
Situation showing a payroll contraction (7 Aug, primary: BLS). Two further candidates
are verified but qualified: a pair of birthright-citizenship executive orders (6 Aug,
primary: White House) sits three days before the date, and the FDA's approval of a
first-in-class narcolepsy drug (5 Aug, primary: FDA) is a science-field development
that belongs to Tech News under the commission's boundary. Where noted, the record is
thin on exact opinion length (secondary accounts give 136 pages and 100 pages) and on
the primary-sourced count of VA employees covered; both are flagged below. The
commission explicitly warns against leading on the jobs print because recent editions
did; that is an editorial caution, not an evidentiary weakness, and the item itself is
well-owned by BLS.

## Contradictions

- Ballroom opinion length: NPR states the opinion runs 136 pages; Reason/Volokh says
  the majority opinion is 100 pages. Likely 100-page majority plus dissent totaling
  ~136; treat as unresolved and avoid printing a hard page count without the opinion
  in hand. The primary (the opinion itself) governs; do not assert a figure secondary
  accounts disagree on.
- Jobs revisions: NBC's account describes May as "cut by 66,000 to 129,000 total jobs
  added." The BLS primary shows the reverse: May was revised down from +129,000 to
  +63,000 (a 66,000 cut). Print the primary's post-revision figures: May +63,000,
  June +20,000. This is a secondary transcription error, not a real dispute.
- Blanche prior role: coverage says he "had been serving as acting attorney general"
  and "replaces Pam Bondi"; his 2025 confirmation was as Deputy Attorney General. The
  8 Aug vote is the Attorney General confirmation. Do not conflate the two votes; a
  2025 "confirmed as Deputy Attorney General" headline is a different event.
- Vote timestamp: the Senate page records 04:17 AM (8 Aug); some accounts say ~4:31
  a.m. The Senate record governs. Absent member: coverage identifies McConnell as the
  one "Not Voting."
