Inputs:

- ../../commission.md — the selection rule, the source floor, and the Tech News boundary

Output: evidence.md

Find the genuinely consequential US developments of Sunday, 9 August 2026, and
build the record for the four to six the edition will run. Work from the primary
record outward:

- Identify candidate events dated on or immediately before 9 August 2026 that
  change law, policy, public institutions, or people's material conditions. For
  each, locate the primary that owns it: the court order or filing, the agency
  or White House release, the data print, the company or agency statement, the
  transcript. Read the primary, not a headline about it.
- For each event the edition is likely to run, secure at least one independent
  account from a newsroom outside the authoring party, and verify every figure,
  date, name, and title against the primary. Where primary and secondary numbers
  conflict, the primary governs and you record the discrepancy.
- Note where a story is really a technology-field development rather than a
  public-policy event, so the orchestrator can keep it in Tech News and out of
  this brief.
- Confirm every recorded URL resolves to the document's own page. A paywall or
  403 is gated, not dead; record the source's real address.

Bring more verified candidates than the edition needs (six to eight), each with
its primary and independent account, so selection has room. Source floor is 5
overall, with one primary and at least one independent secondary per item.
Classify each source primary or secondary and say why. In Numbers, give each
figure the edition may print its exact reading, owner, and scope. In Source
assets, name an official visual (a chart from a data release, a document image)
only where it would carry an item better than prose.
