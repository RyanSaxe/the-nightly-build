The genre is a compressed daily news brief: four to six items, each a tight lede
that states what happened, names the actors, and carries the figure that shows
its size, for a well-read reader who wants the day's consequence without the
recap. Study the writers and desks who do short, verified news compression well:
wire-service and quality-newsroom brief formats, and daily digests that state a
development in one or two sentences and stand behind a number. What the writer
needs from the exemplars is how a lede lands the consequence first, how a figure
rides inside the sentence rather than trailing it, and how compression stays
concrete instead of turning into abstraction.

Prefer exemplars covering other days and other stories, not 9 August 2026, so no
framing of today's events arrives in the guide as material.
