  Only the scheduled CPI release ([s1]) falls on Wednesday, 12 August, and it had not
  posted a figure ([s2]). No reported development lands on Wednesday. Flagged to the
  orchestrator for the 12 August dating decision.
- Gilman (resolved). researcher/01 said "arrived Wednesday" and "military hospital in
  Texas." The cited source [s7] says back Tuesday night, expected at Andrews AFB, no
  Texas, no hospital named. Record corrected; Texas/Wednesday removed.
- EO mechanism (resolved). The AP secondary described a "federal eligibility list"; the
  EO primary [s8] shows states submit a state-specific eligible-voter list and USPS
  refuses to transmit ballots to anyone not on it. Primary governs; recorded from [s8].
- Epstein scope (open). Outlets frame the unsealed material two ways: documents from
  Giuffre's 2015 civil suit ([s4] Bloomberg/AP) versus grand jury materials produced by
  Boies Schiller Flexner (Courthouse News/PBS). Boies Schiller was Giuffre's civil firm,
  so these may be facets of one omnibus 11 Aug order or closely related orders. The
  common, cross-confirmed holding is that Preska cleared release of sealed
  Epstein-investigation records over Maxwell's objection under the Transparency Act. The
  exact document set and whether the order sits on the civil docket or a grand-jury
  docket are unresolved because the primary [s5] could not be retrieved.
- Conflation traps avoided: an NBC URL (rcna248191) resolves to a December 2025
  Engelmayer grand-jury ruling; a Fox URL on "unsealing Epstein grand jury records"
  resolves to a 5 December 2025 Florida ruling by Judge Rodney Smith. Neither is the
  11 August 2026 Preska ruling. Recorded so they are not cited as this day's news.
- CPI (resolved). researcher/01's downstream draft asserted the July release "happened
  this morning" while citing the June release. The release event is owned by the schedule
  [s1]; the June data by [s2]; no July figure exists. Sourced accordingly.
