# evidence: current-events/2026-08-12 (01)

This record supports a US general-news brief for Wednesday, 12 August 2026, but with
one finding the writer and editor must confront first: the available web record for
12 August itself is nearly empty. The most consequential, verifiable US developments
in this window are dated Tuesday, 11 August 2026 (a federal Epstein-files disclosure
ruling, the release of a former Marine held in Russia, and a second injunction against
the mail-voting order). The one hard event scheduled for 12 August, the Bureau of Labor
Statistics release of the July Consumer Price Index at 8:30 a.m. ET, had not posted to
the BLS primary at research time: the agency's live release still carried June data, so
the July figure that the item would turn on could not be verified against its owner. I
verified every date below against article datelines rather than search summaries, which
repeatedly mislabeled Tuesday's rulings as 12 August. Each lead item has a resolving
secondary; the underlying court orders are the primaries and I could not retrieve their
document pages directly (403/JS-shell), a gap recorded per item. The record is thin on
anything that happened strictly on Wednesday, 12 August: the strongest candidates are a
day old, and the CPI figure is unresolved. The orchestrator should decide whether this
edition runs as an 11-August-dominated brief or the date is reconsidered.

## Contradictions

- Date labeling. Search-engine summaries repeatedly attached "August 12" to events that
  the underlying articles date to Tuesday, 11 August 2026 (Aug 12, 2026 is a Wednesday;
  Aug 11 is the Tuesday). The Preska, Gilman, and Talwani items are all 11 August by
  their own datelines and publication timestamps. Only Gilman's US arrival carries into
  12 August. I found no US law/policy/institutional development datelined Wednesday, 12
  August 2026 in the available record; an explicit "Wednesday, August 12, 2026" search
  surfaced only Philippine school-closure notices and an "Asia economic calendar nearly
  empty" note.
- CPI: forecast vs. fact. Previews converge on ~3.4% headline / ~2.5% core for July, but
  that is consensus, not the release. The BLS primary still owned only June (+3.5%) at
  research time. The figure the CPI item turns on is unresolved.
- Epstein ruling scope. The AP/Bloomberg framing describes documents from Giuffre's 2015
  civil suit; other outlets (Courthouse News, NBC) framed an adjacent unsealing of
  Maxwell-related grand jury subpoenas produced by Boies Schiller Flexner. These may be
  the same 11 August ruling described from different angles or closely related orders; I
  verified only the Giuffre-2015-documents framing through a resolving source. Note this
  is distinct from the December 2025 Maxwell grand-jury ruling by Judge Paul Engelmayer
  (an NBC URL surfaced in search resolved to that older December 2025 story).
- Gilman detention length is consistent across outlets: "more than four years," detained
  since 2022, reported by Fox as 1,666 days; sentence 3.5 years extended to ~10 years.
