# Commission: current-events/2026-08-12

## Assignment

The US-focused general-news brief for Wednesday, 12 August 2026. Template `brief`
(shortread; 4 to 6 items; each item one primary record and at least one
independent secondary account). Authorized scheduled work from `nb duty`: rolling
mode, the selected UTC date is unpublished, one article.

## What the brief covers

Select the day's most consequential US developments: events that change law,
public policy, public institutions, or people's material conditions. Do not fill
a topic quota, and do not run routine political theater or a story that is merely
popular. Include an international story only where leaving it out would make the
brief misleading, and size it to its importance. A development whose news is its
public consequence belongs here; a development in a technical field itself belongs
to Tech News and is out of scope for this brief.

The item set and the day's through-line are the researcher's and writer's to
determine from what actually happened on 12 August 2026. This commission fixes the
frame, the boundaries, and the coverage this edition has already spent, not the
items.

## Boundaries with the rest of tonight's edition

The Opinion desk is publishing a piece today on the executive order narrowing the
childhood vaccine schedule. This brief already reported that order on 11 August.
Do not re-lead it or re-run it as an item unless 12 August brought a genuinely new
development in it (a court filing, a formal agency action, a named institution's
response), and if so, report only that new development and leave the argument to
Opinion. Tech News owns developments inside technology, AI, and research; keep
those out of this brief unless the news is their public or political consequence.

## What the brief must do

Each item states what happened and the immediate context, drawn from a primary
record, with the headline carrying the development and the prose carrying the
detail. Report; do not editorialize. The lead item and the one-sentence dek
capture the day's real through-line, not a manufactured theme. Where a single
figure carries an item (a vote count, a dollar total, a casualty number), verify
it against the primary that owns it.

## Sources

Template floor: `min_sources` 5. Per item: exactly one primary record, and at
least one independent secondary account (prefer a reputable US newsroom of
comparable quality; use the primary regardless of country, and use non-US
reporting when it is closer to the event or holds the original reporting). Every
recorded URL resolves to the source's own page.

## Required contribution

A reader finishes the brief knowing the day's few developments that will still
matter next month, why each matters, and the figures that size them, with each
claim traceable to a record they can reopen.
