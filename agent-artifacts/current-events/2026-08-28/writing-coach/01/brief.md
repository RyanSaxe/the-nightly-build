The genre is a wire-service news brief: several short, self-standing items, each
a judgment about what moved for a reader who already saw the headline. Study
writers who make a tight news item carry a number and a caveat without padding
(the best newsroom brief writers, an Economist "Espresso"-style item, a sharp
policy explainer). The register is the paper's calm, precise, first-principles
baseline applied to fast news.

This round's focus: how an item lands its one dropped detail and stops, without
a closing line that hands the point back to the reader. The brief template bars
that closer, so the guide should show what a clean, self-terminating item sounds
like.
