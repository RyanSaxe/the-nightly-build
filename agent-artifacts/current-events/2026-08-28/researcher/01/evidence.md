## Contradictions

- **Warsh, the commission's lead, has no primary record yet.** The Fed speech
  index shows no August 28 Warsh entry and all coverage is pre-speech (latest
  update 5:52pm UTC, August 27). The writer cannot attribute anything Warsh
  "said." Two honest paths: lead on the setup as a scheduled event, or hold the
  item until the transcript posts at federalreserve.gov. Do not source speech
  content to previews.

- **Which inflation number.** BEA owns July inflation at 3.7% headline / 3.3%
  core PCE. Fed-preview coverage repeatedly cites "inflation at 3.4%," a
  different series (CPI or a rounded core reading), not the PCE headline. If the
  brief pairs the Fed and the price data, cite the BEA PCE figure and do not let
  the "3.4%" from previews stand in for it.

- **Is the strait open.** CENTCOM says the internationally recognized lanes are
  mine-free and shipping is moving; Iran says it will not permit a reopening
  until its four conditions are met, and the Joint Maritime Information Center
  still rates the threat SEVERE with uncharted mines outside the cleared lanes.
  Earlier same-week reporting put actual traffic at roughly 20-30 tankers a
  night, about half the pre-war level. "Open" is a US claim about the lanes, not
  an agreed commercial status.

- **ICE "worst of the worst."** The federal framing (thousands of dangerous
  criminals taken off the streets) is contradicted by the Star Tribune's own
  review: about half were already in custody, and the DHS list carried
  duplicates, freed individuals, and at least one misstated conviction (Mariama
  Sia Kanu). Federal officials acknowledged to CNN the site had errors. Any item
  built on the enforcement pattern must carry this, not the DHS count alone.

- **Boundary — do not build.** A federal judge (Indira Talwani) issued a 14-day
  TRO on August 27, 2026 blocking the USPS rule implementing Trump's mail-voting
  order, days after the August 24 Trump v. California order. This is the
  mail-in-voting thread the commission assigns to the unbiased desk. Noted here
  only so the writer does not pick it up as a current-events item.
