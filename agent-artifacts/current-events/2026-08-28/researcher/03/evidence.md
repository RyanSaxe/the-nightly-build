# Evidence record: current-events/2026-08-28 (03)

This record corrects one load-bearing detail on the Hormuz item (item 3) and
preserves all other round-02 work unchanged. The correction: gCaptain (s6), the
recorded URL, places the residual/uncharted mine risk **in and near the cleared
lanes (the TSS)** — not "outside the cleared corridor," as rounds 01 and 02
paraphrased it and as the article body prints it. Re-read at the recorded URL, the
source says the risk sits inside and adjacent to the very lanes CENTCOM calls
cleared, and that authorities are "not yet treating the broader waterway as
mine-free." This strengthens the contested framing: the US "lanes open" claim is
disputed even for the lanes themselves.

Exact gCaptain wording (verbatim, s6):
- "The Joint Maritime Information Center said Thursday that there remains a risk
  from drifting or uncharted mines in and near the TSS, with mine danger areas
  still active."
- "The warning suggests that while U.S. forces may have cleared known mines from
  the established shipping lanes, maritime authorities are not yet treating the
  broader waterway as mine-free."
- "JMIC also said clearance and mine-surveying operations are continuing
  throughout the Strait and maintained its overall threat assessment at SEVERE."

Corrected phrasing for the writer to replace item-3's "with uncharted mines a
risk outside the cleared corridor": say the risk sits **in and near the cleared
lanes themselves** — e.g., "with the Joint Maritime Information Center reporting
drifting or uncharted mines still a risk in and near the cleared lanes, mine
danger areas still active, and its threat assessment held at SEVERE." Do not write
"outside the cleared corridor"; that reverses the source and understates the
dispute.

Two carry-forward cautions still bind. Nothing here is sourced to Warsh's Jackson
Hole speech: within the research window it was undelivered, the Fed's 2026 speech
index showed no August 28 entry, and no primary records what he said. The
mail-in-voting thread (the August 24 Trump v. California order and Judge
Talwani's August 27 USPS TRO) is left to the unbiased desk.

## Contradictions

- **Is the strait open — and are even the lanes clean.** CENTCOM (item 3 primary)
  says the internationally recognized lanes are mine-free and shipping is moving.
  gCaptain (s6), re-read, undercuts the claim at its center: the Joint Maritime
  Information Center reports drifting or uncharted mines still a risk **in and near
  the TSS — the cleared lanes themselves** — with mine danger areas still active,
  says authorities are "not yet treating the broader waterway as mine-free," and
  holds the threat at SEVERE with clearance operations continuing. Iran separately
  says it will not permit a reopening until its four conditions are met. Earlier
  same-week reporting put actual traffic at roughly 20-30 tankers a night, about
  half the pre-war level. "Cleared/open" is a US claim contested even for the
  lanes, not an agreed status for the waterway. This is the corrected framing;
  the earlier "risk outside the cleared corridor" was wrong and reversed the
  source.

- **Which inflation number.** BEA owns July inflation at 3.7% headline / 3.3%
  core PCE. Fed-preview coverage repeatedly cited "inflation at 3.4%," a
  different series (CPI or a rounded core reading), not the PCE headline. If the
  brief pairs price data with the Fed, cite the BEA PCE figure and do not let the
  preview "3.4%" stand in for it.

- **Ballroom staleness.** The order is a week old and the Court has taken no
  further action on the docket within the window. If the writer keeps item 4, the
  news peg is the ongoing construction under a temporary stay, not a fresh ruling.

- **Boundary — do not build.** The August 24 Trump v. California order and Judge
  Talwani's August 27 USPS TRO (mail-in voting) belong to the unbiased desk.
  Noted only so the writer does not pick them up.
