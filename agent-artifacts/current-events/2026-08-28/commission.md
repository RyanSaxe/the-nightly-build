# Commission: current-events/2026-08-28

## Assignment

The US general-news brief for Friday, 2026-08-28. Select the four to six most
consequential US developments dated on or immediately around this date: events
that change law, public policy, public institutions, or people's material
conditions. Each item is a judgment about what moved, for a reader who already
saw the headline. Include an international story only where leaving it out would
mislead, and size it to its importance.

The specific items are research-driven within this beat. The researcher surveys
the day and documents qualifying developments with their primary records; the
writer selects the final four to six and orders them by consequence. Do not fill
a topic quota, and do not manufacture an item to reach the count.

Candidate leads to verify (not a required set, and not exhaustive): the Federal
Reserve under Chair Kevin Warsh and the Jackson Hole rate signal; ICE
enforcement patterns after Operation Metro Surge (arrests at or near
correctional facilities); the SCOTUS order letting White House ballroom
construction proceed; developments in the Iran-strikes aftermath with domestic
US consequences. Any strong, better-sourced development of the day supersedes
these.

## Boundaries with this edition

- **Unbiased owns the mail-in-voting story this edition.** The unbiased desk is
  running a full two-position piece on the Supreme Court's August 24 order
  (Trump v. California) letting the President's executive order restricting
  mail-in voting proceed for the November 3 midterms. Do not make that order a
  current-events item and do not reproduce its analysis. current-events already
  carried the procedural run-up on 2026-08-14; leave the merits to unbiased.
- **Field developments belong to Tech News.** Put a technology story here only
  when its public consequences are the news. The AI-industry and research
  developments of the day go to the tech-news brief running alongside this one.
- Do not re-lead on the national debt: current-events led on the $40T debt
  figure on 2026-08-21.

## Sources

Template floor (brief): at least 5 sources total. Per item: exactly 1 primary
(the original announcement, filing, ruling, or record) and at least 1
independent secondary account. Prefer a reputable US newsroom for independent
reporting where quality is comparable; use the primary record regardless of
origin.

## Recent habits to break

From the last two weeks of this series (openers, deks, headings the editor will
compare against):

- Deks have been building a lead clause and then appending the other items with
  "on a day that also X, and Y" (2026-08-17). That comma-triad append is a
  banned dek mold. Let the dek add one identifying detail the headline dropped,
  not a run of the day's other items.
- Headlines have settled into "concrete event + its institutional consequence"
  (federal debt arrives early; flooding puts Indiana under a federal emergency;
  Somali TPS ends after a ruling). Keep the specificity; vary the construction so
  the front page does not read stamped.

## Required contribution

A reader finishes the brief understanding what actually moved in the country
today and why each item earns its minute, with every claim traceable to a
primary record. The brief is analysis, not a recap: assume the headlines and add
the number or caveat they dropped.
