dated Monday 2026-08-03 and Tuesday 2026-08-04. It is strongest on four items
with a clean primary record: the record June trade deficit (BEA/Census, released
Aug 4), the cyclosporiasis outbreak's first deaths and record case count (CDC
surveillance, data through Aug 3), the 25-state Section 301 tariff lawsuit
(New York AG filing, Aug 3), and the Spokane arson charge (Spokane County

the NDNY immigration-mask injunction, and the 24-state TANF-data suit): each is

The one international candidate (Iran / Strait of Hormuz) could not be pinned to
a primary and is recorded as unverified. A structural caution runs through the
selection: four of the seven domestic candidates are Democratic-state-versus-Trump
legal actions all filed or decided on Monday Aug 3, so a naive selection would
publish four versions of one story. No candidate is a pure AI/tech field
development, and no US AI-policy action landed on Aug 3-4, so there is no genuine
overlap with tech-news or with the opinion desk's EU-AI-Act argument.

## Contradictions

- **Cyclosporiasis counts moved during the day.** Earlier Aug 3-4 secondary
  reporting (CNN, Deseret, Today) cited 6,707 confirmed / ~18,000 estimated /
  423 hospitalized / 45 states, and located both deaths in Michigan. The CDC
  surveillance page as of Aug 4 (data through Aug 3) owns the higher current
  figures: 10,468 confirmed, 517 hospitalized, 2 deaths, 47 states. Use the CDC
  numbers; the Michigan death location rests on secondary reporting only (CNN
  was geo-blocked and not opened), so attribute it as reported, not as CDC-stated.
- **Spokane evacuation figure.** NPR reports ~65,000 evacuated; US News reports
  ~67,000. Both trace to state/local officials mid-event; the count was moving.
  Record it as "roughly 65,000-67,000."
- **Voter-registration ruling date.** Democracy Docket dates Judge Abelson's
  order Monday Aug 3; Votebeat, the Maryland Daily Record, and the Washington
  Times date their stories Aug 4. Most likely the order issued Aug 3 and was
  widely reported Aug 4. Confirm against the D. Md. docket before printing a date.
- **Tariff suit venue.** Some wire copy implies a "district court"; the NY AG's
  own release specifies the US Court of International Trade. The AG release controls.
- **Section 301 country count.** Secondary copy says "more than 80 countries";
  the NY AG filing says 59 countries and the EU. The filing controls for the
  suit's scope; the "80+" figure may reflect an earlier/broader tariff tranche.
- **Iran / Strait of Hormuz (international candidate).** Reporting itself is
  contradictory: Trump said US-Iran talks were scheduled "within a day or two,"
  while the Iranian government said no talks were underway. The underlying
  primary could not be opened (CNN and Reuters were geo-blocked/unfetchable), so
  this item is unverified and should not run without a primary or a US-based
  independent account that resolves the dispute.
