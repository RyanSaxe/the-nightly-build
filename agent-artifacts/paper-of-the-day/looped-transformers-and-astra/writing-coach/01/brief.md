# Writing coach brief

Set a calm, technically literate voice for a reader with mathematics and
computer-science training. Explain the first principles before using the term:
define recurrence over depth, then show what the loop stores and what it costs.
Use the focal paper’s concrete computer construction as the narrative spine.

The Astra portion must be visibly conditional. Use “OpenAI’s public materials
do not specify” and “secondary reporting says” where appropriate. Do not turn an
unnamed-source report into a model specification. Avoid generic significance
claims, hype, and claims that looping alone hides chain-of-thought.
