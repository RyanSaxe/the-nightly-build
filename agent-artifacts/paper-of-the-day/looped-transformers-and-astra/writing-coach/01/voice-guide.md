# Voice guide

- Calm, precise, and first-principles driven.
- Write for an ML engineer who can read equations but wants the architectural
  consequence stated plainly.
- Prefer concrete nouns: block, pass, state, instruction, memory, scratchpad,
  parameter, FLOP, cache.
- Explain each new abstraction at the moment it is needed.
- Make the paper own its result. Use later papers only when they change the
  interpretation of the focal paper.
- Keep reported fact, synthesis, and uncertainty distinct.
- Let citations carry exact claims. Do not imply private knowledge about Astra.
- End with the strongest evidence-backed boundary, not a generic lesson.
