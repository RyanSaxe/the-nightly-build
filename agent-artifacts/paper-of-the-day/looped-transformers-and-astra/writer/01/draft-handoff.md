# Draft handoff

The draft uses the title “Looped transformers turn depth into a dial.” It names
Giannou et al. as the focal ICML 2023 paper and presents Universal Transformer
as the early predecessor. It explains FLEQ/SUBLEQ, address pointers, branches,
function calls, the constant-depth/program-length distinction, and the
hard-coded-weight limitation.

The later sections use Yang et al. to show why input injection and training
across loop outputs matter, then use Geiping et al. to show a scalable
prelude–core–coda language model with variable test-time recurrence. The draft
includes the reported numerical comparisons only where they clarify the
resource trade. Saunshi et al. and Mixture-of-Recursions supply the latent-
reasoning and adaptive-token extensions.

The Astra section distinguishes official OpenAI sources from The Verge’s
unnamed-source report. It explicitly says the public record does not establish
the architecture or any causal link to Astra’s performance or monitorability.
