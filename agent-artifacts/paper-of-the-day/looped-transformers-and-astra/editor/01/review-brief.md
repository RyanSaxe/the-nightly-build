# Editorial review brief

Audit the paper against the paper-template contract and the source record.
Check the abstract card, source order, inline locators, figure captions and
alt text, equation annotation, word band, and section-level citation density.
Pay special attention to the Astra attribution: official OpenAI materials must
remain separate from the unconfirmed secondary report. Check that “same
weights” is never presented as “free compute,” and that the focal construction’s
hard-coded nature is visible.
