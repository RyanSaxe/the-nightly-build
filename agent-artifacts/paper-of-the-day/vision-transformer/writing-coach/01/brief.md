- editorial-direction.md (house standard, slop, headlines, press voice, paper
  template identity, series prompt) — at the artifact root
- commission.md (the paper, the claim to rebuild, the after-record to weigh it
  against, and habits not to inherit) — at the artifact root

Output: writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md

Focus: a research-paper reconstruction (1800-3400 words) that sets real notation
and reads figures, for a machine-learning engineer. Study writers who explain a
technical result by building each idea before the sentence that needs it, in the
patient register of Chris Olah or Lilian Weng, and who weigh a claim like a
reviewer. Prefer exemplars on machine learning or adjacent quantitative fields.
Do not study prior Nightly Build paper pieces for voice. Do not put the Vision
Transformer's results or the article's verdict in the guide.
