  direction (rebuild one paper's claim and weigh it)

Output: writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md

Study writers who explain a mathematical machine-learning idea by building it up
concept by concept and setting the math honestly: the register the press points
at is Chris Olah and Lilian Weng, with Mitchell Hashimoto's plainness. Choose
exemplars who teach a derivation (the ELBO, a reparameterized estimator) without
slop and without hiding the math behind metaphor. Prefer passages that show the
writing rather than restate VAE findings, since the writer inherits whatever the
guide quotes.
