# Writing-coach brief: paper-of-the-day/generative-adversarial-networks (01)

## Your job
Produce `voice-guide.md` here for a `paper` article (1800-3400 words) that
rebuilds the GAN paper's minimax claim and its two theoretical results, then
weighs them against the follow-on record (WGAN, Lucic et al., diffusion).

## Begin with these inputs
- This brief; `../../commission.md`; `../../editorial-direction.md` (press voice:
  Hashimoto's calm first-principles register; Olah/Weng patience where it teaches).

## Study the best
Study at least three exemplars the field rates for rebuilding a technical result
for a fluent reader: writers who derive an idea cleanly and weigh it honestly
(e.g. distill.pub-style exposition, Lilian Weng, Chris Olah, or a respected
researcher's paper walkthrough). Prefer primary pieces. Do not study prior
Nightly Build paper pieces for voice.

## Settle for this writer
- How to present an equation and a short derivation (the D* result; the JS-
  divergence reduction) in prose that carries the reader through the algebra
  without hand-waving or drowning them — building each symbol before the line
  that needs it.
- How to move from "what the paper proves" to "what practice did instead"
  without a stock revelation frame, keeping reported proof, and empirical
  follow-on, and the writer's synthesis distinct.
- Register for a reviewer's verdict that commits without overclaiming.
- Diction: one name per concept (discriminator, generator, value function),
  concrete over abstract.

## Constraints
How to write, not what to say. No catchphrases, no reusable closers, no named-
persona imitation. End with `Recently used, do not reuse:` (the one-line-paradox
headline + "N follow-ups disagree" dek; the "proved X for a setting practice
never occupied" opener-as-formula; closing on a reading list).

Write only `voice-guide.md`. Return `DONE writing-coach <path>`.
