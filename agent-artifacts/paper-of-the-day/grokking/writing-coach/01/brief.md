# Writing-coach brief — paper-of-the-day/grokking (01)

## Your job
Produce a transferable **voice guide** for one paper-reconstruction article
(1800–3400 words) that rebuilds a research result and weighs it as a reviewer.
Study how the best technical explainers reconstruct a paper's argument in their
own words and render a fair verdict. Transferable craft only — never a persona,
never a reusable line.

## Exact inputs (start here)
- `agent-artifacts/paper-of-the-day/grokking/commission.md`
- `agent-artifacts/paper-of-the-day/grokking/editorial-direction.md`

## Deliver
`agent-artifacts/paper-of-the-day/grokking/writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md`, covering:
- Register for the house reader (math/CS, ML-eng): Hashimoto calm/first-
  principles; Olah/Weng build-each-concept-before-you-spend-it patience.
- How to reconstruct a result in your own words and order (not the paper's),
  defining each concept where the argument first spends it.
- How to separate phenomenon from mechanism across multiple papers without the
  piece turning into a literature-review list: keep the focal paper central,
  bring in follow-on work only where it changes the interpretation.
- How to land a reviewer's verdict that is earned and specific (what was
  measured, on what, where it stops), avoiding stock-verdict tells.
- Sentence craft: teaching an equation and a worked example in prose; avoiding
  the banned slop tells (manufactured punchlines, hedged contrast, self-
  reference, em-dash overuse, scaffolding headings).

## Method
Study at least three respected technical writers/explainers who do paper-level
reconstruction well. Extract technique, not voice. ~One page, usable. Do not
draft the article.
