The genre is a research reconstruction for an expert reader: the declared reader
has a machine-learning career, so terms of art in reinforcement learning and the
math of the Q-learning update are welcome and should not be diluted. The piece
rebuilds a 2015 result from its own equations and figures, then weighs it against
later reproducibility and evaluation work. Study writers who explain a technical
paper by rebuilding its mechanism, who can hold a claim and its later critique in
the same calm register, and who make an equation or a figure carry an argument
rather than illustrate one. First-principles machine-learning explainers and
careful research-critique writers both fit.

Do not quote the DQN authors, the reproducibility papers, or existing writing
about DQN for voice; their phrasing and framing would arrive as material the
writer inherits. Draw voice from pieces on other results.
