# Researcher brief

## Inputs

- Read `commission.md`.
- Read the generated `editorial-direction.md`.
- Read the focal paper at https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01595 and its PDF at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.01595.
- Read the sources named in the commission that are used in the article. Open each source directly. Do not rely on snippets or uncited memory.

## Output

Write `evidence.md` in this directory with these exact top-level sections: `## Sources`, `## Contradictions`, `## Numbers`, `## Source assets`, and `## Discarded`.

For every source used, record: URL, Kind (`primary` or `secondary`), what it establishes, a faithful paraphrase, a locator such as page/section/figure or a stable page heading, and one short exact quote only when it earns use in the article. Mark the focal paper as the owner of each theorem and model claim. In `## Numbers`, list every numerical result proposed for the article and its source locator. In `## Source assets`, identify the exact focal-paper figures and crop limits, or state why no additional asset is appropriate. In `## Contradictions`, record disagreements or scope limits among the sources, especially the difference between in-context experiments and claims about real deployed systems. In `## Discarded`, record read sources or claims that were excluded and why.

## Research questions

1. What does the focal paper mean by a type, a verification order, partial implementation, and a double deviation?
2. What exactly does the revelation principle establish, and what does it leave open?
3. How do the nested action sets in Figure 1 produce directional sandbagging?
4. What are the assumptions and precise results behind the monotone cap, ironing, and the optimal cap `1 - sqrt(mean bias^2 + variance)`?
5. What does Figure 8 actually show about technology choice, and which quantities are illustrative rather than empirical?
6. What do the focal paper’s multi-agent examples assume about higher-order beliefs, rewards, and monitoring?
7. Which external evidence supports the motivation, and what limitations prevent it from being treated as validation of the model?

## Proof

- Use at least eight read primary sources.
- Verify every number and every URL.
- Report exact figure/page locators for both planned focal-paper assets.
- Do not browse the repository or the Git history as part of research.
- State a research limitation if a source is inaccessible or only available as an abstract.
