# Writing coach brief

## Inputs

- Read `commission.md`.
- Read the generated `editorial-direction.md`.
- Read the initialized paper template and its furniture guidance.
- Read at least three complete public-domain or openly accessible technical pieces that exemplify calm, first-principles explanation. Prefer one Mitchell Hashimoto technical essay and one each from Chris Olah or Lilian Weng, but choose replacements if a source is unavailable.

## Output

Write `voice-guide.md` in this directory. Begin with `## How this piece should sound`. Give concrete guidance for sentence rhythm, how to introduce mechanism-design terms to an ML reader, how to move from the paper’s type tuple to the sandbagging example, how to handle equations and figures, and how to close with a conditional verdict. Include 2–3 short, exact, verified quotations from each exemplar, with source URLs and a one-sentence note about the technique each quotation demonstrates. Do not write generic praise or restate the commission.

## Proof

- Each exemplar was opened and read in full enough to verify the quotation.
- The guide distinguishes transferable technique from topic or form.
- The guide warns against generic safety prose, overclaiming empirical evidence, headline formula, and ornamental transitions.
- The file is self-contained for the writer while referring to the commission and editorial direction for article facts.
