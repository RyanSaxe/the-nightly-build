## Recent patterns to break

- Recent brief headlines: "Friday's 3.7% drop takes the S&P 500 into a
  correction", "The World Court finds ...", "The NSA's classified network goes
  offline ...", "The White House announces ...". Do not open with "The" plus an
  institution, and do not lead with a day name plus a figure.
- Recent deks pair one figure with a second figure or an enforcement clause
  ("..., with Nvidia down 14% and Broadcom down more than 14%"; "... with
  enforcement left to ..."). Build the dek on a different detail: who, or what
  happens next.
- Item headings recently ended in counts ("48 to 51", "nine votes to six") or
  "X releases Y under licence". Avoid both forms.
- Word stories: the last one walked a source chain (dictionary credits X,
  Y traces it to Z, dates disagree). Tell this word's story in its own order.
