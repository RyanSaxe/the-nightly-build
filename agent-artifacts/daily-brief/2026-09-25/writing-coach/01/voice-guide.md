# Voice guide

## How this piece should sound

Write six small reports, not one tour of the day. The headline and dek may establish the date and the range of the brief, but they should not inventory all six subjects or use yesterday's “Today’s items cover” construction. Each item should begin with its own useful fact. Treat *mandamus* as a word a reader can use: give its legal meaning, pronunciation and history without turning it into a wink at any of the three court or diplomatic stories.

The legal items should have Lindsay Whitehurst's control over procedural scope. Her first passage says what a ruling permits while preserving the word “potentially”; her second separates a legal order from the administration's next choice. For each order, name the court, the action required or allowed, and whether the order resolves the merits. A return of access or ballots is an operative consequence, not proof that every underlying claim has been finally decided.

Use Hyung-Jin Kim's discipline when the material comes from governments. A readout establishes what its issuing government says occurred. A commitment belongs to both sides only when the sources show a joint agreement. Ceremony, cordial language and an extension of an existing pause do not establish a durable settlement. If the American and Chinese accounts differ, place the attributable claims beside each other and leave the difference visible.

For the technology items, follow Stephen Nellis's sequence: define the unfamiliar mechanism in ordinary language, then attach the claim to a physical result or bounded test. Keep manufacturer figures attributed. Mass production does not by itself prove yield, market share or parity with a competitor; a flight test does not establish operational deployment or autonomous combat use.

Every item should stop on information, not cadence. The Word item may close on the exact construction a reader would encounter, such as a petition for or writ of *mandamus*. Each News item should end with the next supported procedural step, the operative duration of an agreement, or one specifically unresolved issue. Each Tech item should end with the test boundary or unverified production claim that limits what can yet be concluded. Do not append a lesson, forecast, “what it means,” or generic sentence about broader stakes.

## Hyung-Jin Kim, “South Korea’s Lee tells AP he wants step-by-step approach to North Korea’s nuclear dismantlement”

Source: https://apnews.com/article/koreas-lee-trump-kim-un-nuclear-2c12c8b88ca37e9dab861af584efa574

> “Whatever approach is taken, it’s far from certain whether North Korea will return to talks anytime soon.”

Checked: https://apnews.com/article/koreas-lee-trump-kim-un-nuclear-2c12c8b88ca37e9dab861af584efa574, retrieved 2026-09-25

Kim states the uncertainty as a fact about participation, not as a vague warning that diplomacy is difficult. The plain phrase “return to talks” keeps the limit legible after several paragraphs of proposals.

> “Lee acknowledged that Trump’s decision came ‘somewhat unexpectedly.’”

Checked: https://apnews.com/article/koreas-lee-trump-kim-un-nuclear-2c12c8b88ca37e9dab861af584efa574, retrieved 2026-09-25

The attribution preserves both the speaker and his restrained wording. Kim lets the modest phrase carry the diplomatic tension instead of upgrading it into a rupture the source did not claim.

## Stephen Nellis, “With new Macs, Apple aims to take on Microsoft, Nvidia in a rush to lower AI costs”

Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/with-new-macs-apple-aims-take-microsoft-nvidia-rush-lower-ai-costs-2026-09-22/

> “They are cheaper than renting data centers.”

Checked: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/with-new-macs-apple-aims-take-microsoft-nvidia-rush-lower-ai-costs-2026-09-22/, retrieved 2026-09-25

Nellis presents the company's unusual pitch in the concrete terms of a buying decision. The sentence is memorable because the comparison is specific, while the surrounding attribution prevents the pitch from becoming the reporter's verdict.

> “Such tasks usually require a data center, but the stack of Macs runs off one wall outlet.”

Checked: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/with-new-macs-apple-aims-take-microsoft-nvidia-rush-lower-ai-costs-2026-09-22/, retrieved 2026-09-25

The contrast turns an abstract hardware claim into two objects a reader can picture and compare. “One wall outlet” supplies scale without promotional adjectives, and Nellis's presence is visible in the choice of the telling physical detail.
