# Commission

## Assignment

- Produce the September 25, 2026 Daily Brief in the fixed order: Word, three News items, two Tech items.
- Word: `mandamus`, treated as a useful legal term rather than as commentary on any news item.
- News: the federal order restoring White House access to CNN, MS NOW, and Politico; the California Supreme Court order requiring return of roughly 500,000 seized ballots; and the September 24 Trump–Xi talks in Washington.
- Tech: CXMT's fifth-generation memory platform entering mass production and the Rafale flight tests of two onboard AI algorithms.

## Reporting boundaries

- Each item gets exactly one primary source and one secondary source, except the Word item may use two authoritative lexical sources.
- State what each order, readout, test, or product announcement establishes and what remains unresolved. Do not infer motives or outcomes from ceremony, litigation posture, or vendor claims.
- Keep every item concise and independent. Do not reuse yesterday's “Today’s items cover” dek mold or its topics.

## Form and quality bar

- Preserve the generated Brief template and metadata. Use compact, specific headings and source labels.
- Make the news items useful to a U.S. reader while preserving legal and diplomatic uncertainty.
- Define technical terms at first use. Resolved policy: researcher capable/high, writer capable/medium, editor inherit/high.
