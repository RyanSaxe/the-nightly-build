## Contradictions

- **Newsom: "commutes" against "plans".** Headlines (KQED, Fox, CNN) say he commutes 548 sentences. The governor's own release says he "plans to change" them, and the explanatory statement is a request for the state Supreme Court's recommendation on 382. The primary does not say whether any commutation has taken effect. I did not find a signed commutation order. Write "announced he will".

The Death Penalty Information Center (read through WebFetch, not raw) says about one-third take effect immediately, which fits the 166 left over (548 minus 382; the arithmetic is mine, and the statement does not give 166). The primary figure is 382.

- **Arch order timing.** NPR says "just before midnight Friday", which cannot be right given its own 12:05 AM Friday publication. NBC says "late Thursday". Write "late on Thursday 5 November" or "just before midnight Eastern", not a minute.
- **Ecdysiast and Gypsy Rose Lee.** Some musical reviews say Mencken created it for her. Quinion says it was made in reply to Georgia Sothern, and Lee mocked it. Use Sothern.
