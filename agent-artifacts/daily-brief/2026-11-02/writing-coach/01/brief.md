Domain and genre: a very short daily news brief for a technical, widely-read
audience: six items, each two or three sentences, that say what happened, who did
it, and what follows, with no filler, plus one word-of-the-day card with a
documented origin told in three or four sentences. Two registers to cover: terse,
precise wire-style news writing that leads with the concrete development; and a
compact, enjoyable word-origin note. Find at least three exemplars among writers
and outlets the field rates for exactly this — spare, high-information news
writing, and good short etymology/word-history writing. Study them with web tools;
do not use this repository or its prior briefs as a voice source.

The whole brief is about a three-minute read, so the guide should be able to be
acted on in two or three directions per register, not a long list. Prefer passages
that show how to pack a development into two sentences without hedging, and how to
tell a word's origin story with a light touch. Verify every quotation against the
raw page word for word and record where you checked it. Write the "How this piece
should sound" direction last, pinned to the passages you chose.
