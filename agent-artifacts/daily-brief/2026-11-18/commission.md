## The assignment

Six items, in this order: one Word of the Day, three news items, two technology items.

- **Word of the Day.** A word with a documented origin story, ideally a coinage with a known inventor
  and a datable first printing. It must not repeat any word the paper has already run: search
  `nb history` (the brief's word-of-the-day items and the paused word-of-the-day series) before
  choosing. Avoid a word from logic or philosophy of induction, since 17 November ran "grue".
- **News, three items.** Chosen by importance of what happened, not volume of coverage. Favor the
  United States. Report only what happened between 16 and 18 November 2026, or what changed in that
  window on a running story. A New York-region or international story displaces a national story only
  when it matters more.
- **Technology, two items.** A release, capability, research result or other change from roughly
  12 to 18 November 2026.

## Sourcing

One strong source per item. The underlying record (order, filing, release notes, paper, official
statement) where it can be opened; a reputable report where it cannot. A second source only where the
first is disputed or cannot settle the claim. Attribute unverified claims to who makes them.

## Lead

a fresh present-tense verb. The dek adds the detail the headline left out and does not restate it.
