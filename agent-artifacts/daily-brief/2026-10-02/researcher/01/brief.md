Your job is to establish, from documents you open and read, the material for six
items for Friday 2 October 2026: one Word of the Day, three news items, two
technology developments. Resolve more candidate items than six so the writer can
choose the strongest, but make clear which you recommend and why.

Required of the evidence:

  development against the document that owns the claim (the agency release, the

  source, with exact locators (section, page, paragraph, or timestamp). The
  template needs twelve sources minimum across the six items.
- Mark, for each item, exactly what the evidence establishes and what it does
  not. The brief's discipline is the honest status line; give the writer the
  facts to write it.

  speech and pronunciation, and an independent word history (for example a
  reputable etymology reference) for the origin and one documented moment. Do
  not propose "quiddity" (used 1 October).

Check the leads in commission.md; keep the ones that hold up with firsthand
sourcing and discard the rest with a one-line reason. If a strong, better-sourced

and the status of any unverified claim. Note any item where you could not reach
a primary source, so the writer does not build on a hole.
