# writing-coach brief: daily-brief/2026-10-02 (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (../../editorial-direction.md) — the binding standard, read it
- commission.md (../../commission.md) — the assignment and its boundaries

Output: voice-guide.md (in this folder, ./voice-guide.md)

Work from these inputs. Do not tour the repository, the Git history or the
archive for background. Where something you need is missing, ask me.

Genre: a daily news brief with a Word of the Day. Six short items. Each item is
one tight paragraph (the word card gets a definition line plus a short origin
paragraph) that reports what happened, names who is claiming it, and marks how
firm the evidence is. The audience reads widely and has strong math and CS
background, so the register is spare, exact, and unhurried, never breathless.

Find exemplars among writers the field rates for exactly this: the short,
high-density news item and the lively-but-precise word/etymology note. Think
disciplined newsletter and wire-brief writing, and the best etymology columnists
for the word card. Study how they pack a development and its caveat into a few
sentences without filler, and how a word note stays lively while short. Avoid
SEO roundups and influencer threads.

The voice guide should help the writer hold two registers in one page: the
even, careful news register and the slightly livelier word-card register. Say
what each takes from the passages you quote.
