# writing-coach brief: daily-brief/2026-10-03 (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md (../../editorial-direction.md) — the full standing standard.
- commission.md (../../commission.md) — the six items and the day's assignment.
Output: ./voice-guide.md

Work from these inputs. Do not tour the repository, the Git history or the archive for
background, and do not use prior articles as a source of voice. Where something you need
is missing, ask me.

Genre and audience: a compressed daily news-and-technology brief for a widely read
reader fluent in mathematics and computer science. Six short items in one issue: a
lively Word-of-the-Day card, three terse news reports, two technology notes. Each item
is a few sentences that must carry what happened, who claims it, and what the evidence
settles. Find exemplars of two things the brief needs: (a) excellent short-form news or
newsletter writing that is dense and specific without padding, and (b) lively, exact
etymology/word writing for the word card. Choose writers the field rates; quote passages
this compressed form can actually act on (not long-form flourishes a 3-sentence item has
no room for). The register is plain and precise, not chatty.
