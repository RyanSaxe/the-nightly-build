Work from these inputs. Do not tour the repository, the Git history or the archive
for background. Where something you need is missing, ask me.

Domain and genre: a daily news brief for a technically literate general audience —
six short items (a Word of the Day, three news items, two technology developments),
each a tight paragraph that says what happened and why it matters, attributing
unverified claims and marking the status of evidence. The register is wire-service
crispness with a human edge: every word carries, no filler, each item stands alone.

Find exemplars the field rates for exactly this compression: the best short-brief /
newsletter writing where a single paragraph delivers a development and its stakes
cleanly (for instance the tight item-writing of a well-edited daily newsletter, a
numbers-literate brief, or a sharp word/etymology column for the Word of the Day's
register). Prefer primary pieces; skip SEO/aggregators. Three+ exemplars, two or
three verified quotations each, from different parts of each piece. At least one
exemplar should model the lively-but-brief word/etymology card register, since item 1
is a Word of the Day.

Register target: each item opens on the concrete development (not a wind-up), states
the stakes in plain words, and attributes claims precisely. The `## How this piece
should sound` section must be unmistakably about short daily-brief item-writing —
compression, attribution, self-contained items — not generic news style.
