## Shortlist (ranked; ★ = recommended six)

### Word of the Day

1. ★ **grue** (Nelson Goodman, printed 1954). It is a coinage with a documented inventor and an exact defining sentence. Most well-read people have not met it, and this paper's ML readers will want to use it about generalisation. It is unlike the recent words in kind: a logician's made-up predicate, not a Carroll portmanteau or a Greek-root psychology term. It does not appear in `nb history`.

3. **panjandrum** (Samuel Foote). Only Merriam-Webster was checked. The Foote story is usually told with an unverified anecdote that he wrote it to test an actor's memory, so it needs more work before use.

## Contradictions

- **Measles deaths:** the CDC page lists 6 deaths in 2026 ("subject to change"), while NBC and ABC (14 Nov) report 10 from state health departments (ABC: 7 Pennsylvania, 1 New York, 2 Wisconsin). Give the count with its owner: "state health departments report 10; the CDC lists 6."
- **Measles status, stated as fact or not:** Just Security's Early Edition and Democracy Now! (both 16 Nov) state flatly that PAHO "revoked" the status, citing the Wall Street Journal. NBC says the official announcement is expected on Tuesday, and PAHO had published nothing by 06:25 UTC on 17 Nov. CNN carried a report dated 13 Nov (not opened; HTTP 451). Until PAHO speaks, the decision is reported, not announced.
- **Hormuz, mines or projectiles:** the IRGC navy describes tanker incidents as mine strikes on vessels using an "unauthorised" route (Al Jazeera, 15 Nov; AA briefing, 16 Nov: two tankers hit mines, one sank). UKMTO, as gCaptain reports it, describes an "unidentified projectile" and an "attack". An IBTimes headline carries an IRGC claim that six vessels are sinking, which no other source corroborates. Attribute each account to its party. Whether UKMTO's vessels are the ones Iran describes is not established.
- **Bahrain, damage claims:** for 13 Nov the IRGC claimed a radar destroyed and CENTCOM said two drones were shot down with no damage. For 15 Nov the IRGC claims six drones at Riffa, Bahrain says it intercepted them, and the US had not commented.
- **OpenAI's framing against the AG's:** the complaint calls the Hugging Face attack "entirely foreseeable" and the product of a culture prioritising growth. OpenAI says the suit singles it out over "a challenge that's been identified by every major AI lab". The "over 80 times" figure is the AG's account of what OpenAI told it. OpenAI's own statement of that number was not found.
- **Optimizely, money taken:** Report URI counts about $7,040 in attacker wallets by 19:25 UTC on 16 Nov, and StepSecurity (via Cyber Kendra) about $6,600. Report URI's count is the primary measurement.
- **Optimizely, scale:** an earlier social-media count of 186 infected snippets was superseded by Report URI's 188 (178 organisations). Optimizely's status page says only "some customer projects".
- **"grue" dating:** Wiktionary says Goodman coined it "in 1955" and quotes it first from 1965. Goodman's own 1954 introduction says the 1953 London lectures were "now printed for the first time" in the first edition, published in London by Athlone Press in 1954 and by Harvard in 1955. His 1946 "A Query on Confirmation" made the same argument with a different example, so "grue" first appears in print in 1954.
- **Qwen4-Max weights:** TNW reports the weights are closed and that smaller Qwen4 models will be open. The primary post does not say "closed". It says Max is available through Alibaba's services and that smaller models "are coming to open source". Write "available only through Alibaba's services", which the post supports, or attribute "closed weights" to TNW.
- **Commission angle:** the commission asks for a non-AI technology item, and Optimizely satisfies it. Nothing found breaks the lead choice. Measles is arguably as consequential, but its decisive document (PAHO's) does not exist yet, and the elimination story was already in circulation on 13 Nov.

## Limits

- PAHO had not published the measles decision by 06:25 UTC on 17 Nov. The item rests on NBC's unnamed sources until it does, and the orchestrator should recheck https://www.paho.org/en/news before drafting.
- Nvidia's third-quarter results were not released by 06:25 UTC (they are due after the US market close on 17 Nov). Nothing about them can be reported from this record.
- UKMTO's own incident pages could not be opened (Cloudflare block on fetch and on headless Chrome). The tanker times and places come through gCaptain. No vessel was named by any source.
- The US military's response to the 15 Nov Bahrain attack was not found. The reported arrival of a third aircraft carrier (USS Theodore Roosevelt), from ABC's live-page headline and the NYT via AA, was not confirmed from Navy or CENTCOM records, and the carrier count differs across outlets.
- OpenAI's own account of the "over 80" loss-of-control figure, and its July technical report on the Hugging Face incident, were not opened. The complaint's allegations are untested in court.
- Optimizely's statement does not say how the attacker got write access or how many customers were affected. Report URI says the entry point is unknown.
- The Qwen4-Max benchmark charts are images. The DeepSWE and Terminal-Bench figures come through TNW, not from the post's text. The pricing page was not opened.
- For "grue", no dictionary of record (OED or Merriam-Webster) was found with the philosophy sense. Part of speech and pronunciation come from Wiktionary. That "grue" blends "green" and "blue" is evident from the definition, but Goodman's text read here does not say so in words. Whether the word was spoken in the May 1953 lectures, as opposed to first printed in 1954, is not established.
- Microsoft's preliminary post-incident report was not opened directly (alternate item only).
