## Recent patterns to break

- Four of the last five brief headlines open with "The" plus an institution
  and a present-tense verb ("The World Court finds", "The NSA's classified
  network goes", "The White House announces"). Build this headline differently.
- Recent deks pack a vote count or dollar figure plus an enforcement or
  "ahead of" clause. Vary.
- Item headings have recurring "X does Y nine votes to six" count endings and
  "<Agency> patches two <component> bugs". Avoid both forms.
- Word stories have tended to open with a dated scene ("In August 1834 John
  Scott Russell saw ..."). Open the story differently if the word allows.
