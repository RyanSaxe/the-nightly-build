## Word of the day — palimpsest

**Supported card:** *palimpsest* (noun), Oxford American pronunciation /ˈpæləmpˌsɛst/ (roughly PAL-imp-sest); an old document whose earlier writing was erased or obscured and replaced, or by extension something with visible layers. The word passed through Latin from Greek *palimpsēstos*, from *palin* (“again”) and *psēstos* (“rubbed smooth”). The etymology explains reuse, not a claim that every palimpsest was fully erased. A vivid documented example is a tenth-century Syriac manuscript on reused pages that preserve two underlying Latin layers: a seventh-century grammar commentary and fragments of a fifth-century text of Granius Licinianus, a second-century historian otherwise known only through those pages. This avoids the more familiar Archimedes example.

- **Primary/reference, definition and pronunciation:** Merriam-Webster, dictionary page updated July 1, 2026 (page metadata); [definition and audio/pronunciation](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/palimpsest). It gives two pronunciations and both literal and figurative senses.
- **Reference, etymology and American pronunciation:** Oxford Advanced American Dictionary, publication/update date not displayed; checked Sep. 30, 2026. [Entry](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/american_english/palimpsest). Gives the Greek elements and pronunciation.
- **Primary collection record/curatorial account:** British Library, “Palimpsests: The art of medieval recycling,” page date not displayed (available in its Medieval Manuscripts series); [article](https://www.bl.uk/stories/blogs/posts/palimpsests-the-art-of-medieval-recycling). It identifies the tenth-century double palimpsest, both undertexts, and Licinianus fragments. This is the documented example, not a claim that the undertexts have all been fully reconstructed.

## News 3 — White House voluntary AI accord

**Event/document date:** Sep. 29, 2026. Six company leaders (Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI and Nvidia) signed the one-page “White House Accord on Super Intelligence — Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities,” alongside President Trump. Its four controls call for internal model monitoring, an internal team to check the controls and remedy issues, independent external evaluation, and an independent board committee to review reports. It also says participants will meet to develop practices. The text describes a voluntary commitment, with no stated enforcement process, penalty, public-reporting requirement or deadline. Its suggestion that measures might later be codified is not a law or current legal obligation. Do not conflate the accord with the separately issued White House executive order on terminology.

Lists signatories and commitments, and reports the document’s future-codification language. - **Independent secondary, The Guardian:** [“Trump announces vague ‘morally binding’ AI deal…”](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/sep/29/trump-ai-deal-tech-ceos-superintelligence), published Sep. 29, 2026. ## Technology 2 — WSL containers reach general availability

**Announcement date:** Sep. 29, 2026. Microsoft marked WSL containers GA. Users can update WSL and use `wslc.exe` (also aliased as `container.exe`) to build/run/deploy Linux containers, or call its API from native Windows apps. Microsoft lists new lifecycle commands, networking and observability features; the launch extends Intune policy controls and Defender for Endpoint visibility to container activity. VS Code dev containers and Aspire are listed as integrations. The company’s “up to 2x” Windows-file-access speed statement is a ceiling without test conditions in the launch post. Help Net Security reports Compose remains a requested gap; avoid suggesting every Docker Compose project works unchanged.

- **Primary, Microsoft announcement:** [“WSL containers is now generally available”](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsdeveloper/2026/09/29/wsl-containers-now-generally-available/), Windows Developer Blog, Sep. 29, 2026. Specifies `wsl --update`, CLI/API, new capabilities, Intune/MDE integration and editor/runtime integrations.
- **Primary, Microsoft technical documentation:** [WSL container documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/wsl/wsl-container), current documentation checked Sep. 30, 2026. Explains `wslc.exe`, API and NuGet package; useful for verifying that this is both a CLI and an application API.
- **Independent secondary, Phoronix:** [GA coverage](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Microsoft-WSLC-Linux-Containers), published Sep. 29, 2026. Describes the move from public preview and isolated storage/network architecture.
- **Independent secondary, Help Net Security:** [“WSL containers are generally available on Windows”](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/30/microsoft-wsl-containers-available/), published Sep. 30, 2026.
