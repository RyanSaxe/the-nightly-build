Story available to the writer: Tolkien needed an opposite of tragedy's catastrophe and built one from Greek eu-, "good". The word belongs to the fairy-story lecture he gave at St Andrews on 8 March 1939. It reached print in the 1947 memorial volume for Charles Williams. In a 1944 letter to his son Christopher he wrote that he had coined it, and the letter's date is disputed (see Contradictions). Tolkien called the Birth of Christ "the eucatastrophe of Man's history". The usage note follows the word's own definition: a sudden turn and not merely a happy ending. Tolkien's essay says it does not deny "sorrow and failure", and that it denies "universal final defeat". That phrasing comes from the highlights page and has not been checked against an edition.

## Contradictions

- **When Tolkien coined eucatastrophe.** Flieger and Wikipedia tie the word to the March 1939 lecture and the 1947 essay. Wiktionary dates the letter in which Tolkien wrote "I coined the word" to 28 October 1944. Wikipedia, citing Carpenter 2023 #89, dates the letter to Christopher to 7–8 November 1944. I could not open the 1939 lecture text, so I cannot say whether the word was already in it. The 2008 expanded edition prints the manuscripts. The safe wording: the word was coined for "On Fairy-Stories", which grew from the 1939 lecture and was published in 1947.
- **Hegseth vote timing.** On 6 November Massie said he would introduce articles "as soon as next week". ABC (8 Nov) says he will reintroduce the resolution "as early as Monday". Both point to the week of 9 November, and neither is a scheduled vote. Jeffries moved in two days from "I don't want to get out ahead of the caucus" (6 Nov, KPBS headline, not opened) to "hard yes". The Gateway Pundit placed the interview on NBC's Meet the Press; ABC's own page shows it aired on This Week.
- **Kraków injuries and mourning.** NBC's first-day report says at least 15 injured, and a later NBC rundown says 16 in the text and 15 in the video headline. The first-day report gives only city mourning through 11 November; the follow-up gives two days of national mourning. The time is given as both "around 3 p.m." and "midday".
- **Hormuz tanker count.** AFP via Gulf News reports the IRGC saying three tankers "exploded and caught fire". AFP via Manila Times reports the IRGC, through IRNA, saying two tankers hit mines. CENTCOM says all mines have been cleared. No account independently confirms any of it.
- **Eisenkot's deadline.** Times of Israel gives 28 days plus up to 14. One NBC report summarized in search results says "six weeks", which matches 28 + 14 = 42 days only if the extension is used.
- **Nvidia's earlier stance.** Search summaries say Nvidia had called the relationship routine and professional when the NYT and FT first reported it. I did not open that statement.
- **Inkling 2 Small's weights.** The launch page says "next week". I could not confirm whether the weights are posted as of 9 November, because the Hugging Face organization URL I tried returned 404.
- **The angle.** The brief commissions a shortlist and sets no angle to test. No source contradicted the selection.
