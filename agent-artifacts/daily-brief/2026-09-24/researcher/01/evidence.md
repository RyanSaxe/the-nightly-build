## 1. Word of the Day: concordat

### Primary

URL: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/concordat

Merriam-Webster's entry identifies `concordat` as a noun, gives the pronunciation `kən-ˈkȯr-ˌdat`, and defines it as a compact or covenant, specifically an agreement between a pope and a sovereign or government regulating ecclesiastical matters. Locator: entry lines showing pronunciation and definition.

### Secondary

URL: https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/concordat

Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary gives the pronunciation variants `/kɒnˈkɔːdæt/` and `/kənˈkɔːrdæt/`, defines the term as an agreement especially between the Roman Catholic Church and the state, and traces it to early-17th-century French or Latin `concordatum`, from `concordare`, “be of one mind.” Locator: entry and Word Origin sections.

Attribution boundary: keep the card to definition, pronunciation, part of speech, and documented origin. Do not claim that current U.S.–China talks are a concordat.

## 5. Technology: FAA SMART

### Primary
