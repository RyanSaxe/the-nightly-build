# Commission

## Assignment

- Complete the rolling Daily Brief for `2026-09-24`.
- Publish exactly six items in template order: Word of the Day, three News items, two Technology items.
- Keep each item self-contained, short, and useful to a reader who has not read another brief.
- No repeated topic or angle with the Feature or Technical article commissioned in this run.

## Editorial decisions

- Word of the Day: `concordat`; define the noun, give pronunciation and part of speech, and explain its French/Latin origin without turning the card into a lesson on church history.
- News 1: the California-led settlement with Paramount over the Warner Bros. merger. Report the pending court approval, the five-year production commitments, worker fund, and why the settlement is not approval of the merger.
- News 2: construction beginning on the Big Bend 1 border-wall project. State the 47-mile project, first-panel date, the wider five-project/roughly-500-mile plan, and the opposition. Keep Big Bend 1 distinct from the temporarily paused project inside Big Bend National Park.

- Technology 1: FAA deployment of SMART. Explain that it uses 200 data streams and predictive analysis, began in limited mode around Washington, D.C., and remains a staff-reviewed scheduling aid rather than aircraft control.

- Use a concrete, date-specific headline and a one-sentence dek.
- Keep the word card concise and the remaining items to the development, context, and known limits.
- Avoid generic transitions, prediction, inflated adjectives, vague attribution, rhetorical questions, and unsupported causal claims.
- Use only the supplied twelve source slots. Update metadata, source count, word count, reading time, date, title, and dek.
