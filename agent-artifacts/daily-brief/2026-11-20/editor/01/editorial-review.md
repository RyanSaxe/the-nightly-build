## Correct

Thesis, from the draft alone: on 19 November the International Court of Justice found that Myanmar, as a state, committed genocide against the Rohingya. The brief carries five shorter items with it, and each one pins a claim to the document that owns it.

Headline. "The World Court finds Myanmar itself committed genocide against the Rohingya." I reopened ASIL, which says the Court "ruled today that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya group in northern Rakhine State ... by a vote of nine to six", and The Conversation, which says it is "the first time since the adoption of the UN genocide convention in 1948 that the court has found a state responsible for committing genocide", and that in 2007 "Serbia itself was not responsible". "Itself" is The Conversation's contrast, and both reports carry the finding. The headline holds as a claim attributed to two reports. The Court's press release PDF still returns 403 to me, so I could not check the judgment's text.

ICJ item. Two breaks. "The dissenters, led by Vice-President Sebutinde" was not in the sources: ASIL says "Vice-President Sebutinde and five other judges", and nothing in it says she led them. "Its precedents" left a reader unsure whose precedents were meant, and ASIL says "the Court's precedent". I rewrote the sentence to ASIL's terms. "Failing to prevent and punish Srebrenica" now says what The Conversation says: failing to prevent the genocide and punish those responsible.

I dropped "first appeared": the record shows only that the word appears in the coining paper's title.
