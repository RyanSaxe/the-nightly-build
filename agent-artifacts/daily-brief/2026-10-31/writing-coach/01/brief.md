# writing-coach brief: daily-brief/2026-10-31 (01)

Inputs:
- `../../editorial-direction.md`
- `../../commission.md`
Output: `voice-guide.md` in this folder (`writing-coach/01/`)

Work from these inputs. Do not tour the repository, the Git history or the
archive for background. Where something you need is missing, ask me.

This is a six-item daily brief: a Word of the Day, three news items, two
technology items, each two or three sentences. Two distinct voices to show:
1. **The newswire item.** Dense, factual, one event per item, the consequence in
   the last clause, no editorializing. Find writers/desks the field rates for
   exactly this compression: a wire lead (Reuters, AP), The Economist's "The
   world in brief" / Espresso, Axios's clipped items, or a strong newsletter
   that states what happened and what follows in two sentences.
2. **The Word of the Day entry.** A short, vivid etymology told as a documented
   story. Find writers who do word histories well: the OED/Merriam-Webster
   "word history" notes, Anatoly Liberman, John McWhorter, or a lexicographer
   whose etymology prose is a pleasure and still factual.

Keep each voice to passages this genre can act on: a two-sentence news item
cannot carry a personal aside, and the word entry has room for a little delight
but not a joke at the reader's expense. The register overall is terse, exact,
and quietly confident. A short brief can act on only two or three directions per
voice, so keep the guide tight.
