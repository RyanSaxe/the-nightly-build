```text

Paraphrase:  Chemistry editor Austin M. Patterson wrote a slip meant to record "D" as an abbreviation for "density". The slip had spaced terms, and a later handler read the "or" as part of one word, underlined the four letters as a headword and made it a lowercase common noun, "dord". The entry had no etymology and no usage examples, and the editors' files had none either. The dictionary had about 250 editors and consultants. An editor found the problem in 1939 and wrote a slip marked "imperative! urgent!" asking for a correction in the next printing. The change was not made until 1947. Merriam-Webster calls dord its only ghost word. No one was fired.

Quote:       "imperative! urgent!" (the 1939 slip, as reported)
```

## Contradictions

- COP31 overrun. ABC says the deal was rejected 44 hours after the official close. The AFP report in the Korea Herald (https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10946205) says the gavel fell "more than 49 hours" after the scheduled end. UN News gives no hour. The writer should say "about two days" unless the editor settles it.
- COP31 electrification. ABC reports the failed text set a goal of 35% electricity in final energy by 2035. The AFP report says a co-host electrification goal was dropped with the deal. Another search summary I did not open (CASRAI, Yahoo) said more than 90 countries signed an electrification pledge. UN News has Stiell citing COP31 Action Agenda initiatives on electrification without a figure. I could not verify the 90-country figure. Do not use it.
- First-failure claim. ABC and Wikipedia say the first failure to adopt an agreed deal since Copenhagen in 2009. UN News does not make the claim. Wikipedia says "COP15".
- Measles deaths. The CDC page counts 6 deaths (as of 19 Nov). Al Jazeera counts Virginia, Pennsylvania (8), Wisconsin (2) and New York (1), which is 12, from state reports. The 12 is my sum of Al Jazeera's per-state figures; a business-standard headline in my search results also says "takes US death toll to 12", which I did not open.
- Virginia measles total. Al Jazeera gives 220 cases. A search snippet (12onyourside.com, 18 Nov, not opened) gives 186 cases. The VDH dashboard did not render for me. These may be different dates.
- Libya. AP (unnamed sources) says Ukrainian sea drones launched from a base in western Libya. Nobody has claimed the strike, and the eastern administration asks Kyiv for explanations. Al Jazeera's analyst (Tallha Abdulrazaq) judges Ukrainian intelligence most likely. Attribute, never state.
- Cairncross. Axios (19 Nov, via search) says he is leaving; the Anadolu digest of 21 Nov has Trump saying "I didn't think that person was good enough". Not used in the brief.
- Dord removal date. Merriam-Webster says the change was made in 1947. Other accounts say the 1940 printing. Merriam-Webster is the owner.
- BepiColombo software issue. Wikipedia attributes a shorter-than-planned burn ("shorter than the expected 37 minutes due to a software issue") to Reuters. I could not open Reuters. A search for it returned only 2024 reports of a power fault. Do not use the claim.
