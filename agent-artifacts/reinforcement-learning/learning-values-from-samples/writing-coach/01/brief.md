learning, with full math and runnable code, for readers with CS/math degrees and
production ML experience who are new to RL. The register is a patient, precise
teacher who shows a thing running rather than asserting it, carries one concrete

for the findings it states, since the writer inherits whatever is in this guide.

introduced, how a worked number is walked through) over passages that hand over
this lesson's own content. Verify every quotation against the raw page word for
word and record where you checked it. Write the "How this piece should sound"
direction last, pinned to the passages you chose, in this subject's own
