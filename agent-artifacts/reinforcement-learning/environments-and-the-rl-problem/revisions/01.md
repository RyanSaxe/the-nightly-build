# Revision 01: rewritten after the owner rejected the first version

The first version told the reader they already knew the bandit, because the course
prompt listed past study that should never have been treated as a prerequisite. The
course now assumes no prior reinforcement learning. Its Why this matters block was the
owner's largest complaint: a tour of applications, a numbered promise of outcomes and a
claim that everything later builds on it.

Three of its explanations were wrong, though its numbers were right. It said the
Bellman equations are coupled because a cell's value appears on both sides of its own
equation, when a self-loop solves by one division and cycles between different cells
are what force a joint solve. It said the discount made the return converge and the
system solvable, on a grid where γ = 1 works for that policy because every episode
ends. And it described a bandit as having a transition.

Its code could not run. The printed listings were excerpts with no imports and nothing
calling the functions, under captions saying `uv run` worked, and the files themselves
never reached the bundle, which accepts only images and chart files. Its visuals drew
the setup and not the ideas, one diagram's labels ran over its boxes, and it linked no
paper.

The rewrite uses a new environment, a slippery 4x4 grid with a goal and a pit, which
stays in use through the control lessons. Two complete files are printed on the page.
Eight new figures draw an episode, value as an average of returns, the backup for one
cell, why cycles force a joint solve, values spreading by sweep, the discount, a policy
that never ends, and the convergence rate. All are inline SVG, so they follow the light
and dark themes and fit a phone. `chart-1.png` and `chart-1.py` are removed because the
page no longer uses them.

Verification: the listings were copied out of the page and run with `uv run`, and their
output matches the output printed on the page. Changing the settings line to slip 0 and
to γ = 1 also runs. Each causal claim was tested by changing the code so the claimed
cause was absent, which caught and corrected several reasons in the draft. The
derivations were worked independently, which added the conditions the Markov step
needs and completed the γ = 1 argument. Every figure was viewed at desktop and phone
width in both themes.

The stamped reading time of 23 minutes counts code, printed output and figure labels.
The prose is about 3,300 words.
