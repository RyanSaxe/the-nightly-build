   / Federal Register). Identify the specific provisions at issue: the "state
   citizenship list" (USCIS + SSA) and the mail-ballot delivery limitation.

2. **The district-court order (June 2026).** Which court, which judge, what it

   the majority's reasoning and the dissent's. Quote both. Get the panel, date,
   and docket. Confirm the "23 states" figure from the filing/opinion.
4. **The DOJ Supreme Court application (~July 27 2026).** What relief the
   administration seeks and its core legal argument (its strongest form). Quote

   counter section. Get the docket/application.

   people/officials on record: the state AGs (name a few + quote their claim),
   the judges (their cited words), and named election-law scholars (e.g.,
   on-record commentary) arguing the president lacks this power. Provide exact
   quotes + URLs so the position card can name them.
6. **Named holders of the opposing view.** The DOJ/SG, and scholars/officials
   defending the order's legality — their actual argument, quoted, for a
   steelmanned counter.
