# Writing-coach brief — opinion/mail-in-voting-order (01)

## Your job
Produce a transferable **voice guide** for an argued opinion (900–2500 words) on
a constitutional/legal question, written for a serious general reader. Study how
the best argumentative essayists and legal commentators state a precise position,
build it from a record, and steelman the other side before answering it.
Transferable craft only — no persona, no reusable line, no partisanship-as-style.

## Deliver
`agent-artifacts/opinion/mail-in-voting-order/writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md`, covering:
- Register: calm, precise, argued from structure and the record — persuasion by
  reasoning, not adjectives or heat. Fit for the house.
- How to open an opinion whose stance is already disclosed on the position card
  (title/dek sell the question or consequence, not the verdict).
- How to build each argument section from cited authority (the advocate's job),
  and how to write a genuine steelmanned counter its own believers would sign.
- How to close on what the argument earned and name what would change the
  judgment, without a stock verdict.
- The banned slop tells, plus the specific risk here: caricature, unearned
  certainty, and treating a legal question as a partisan one.

## Method
Study at least three respected opinion/legal writers. Extract technique.
~One page. Do not draft the article.
