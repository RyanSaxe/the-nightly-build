theory, and named reactions on both sides as separate facts; this draft is the

guardrails' only defense against misattribution and escalation is a legal

Program cannot lawfully approve any operation until the 60-day operating
procedures exist) is evidence the government itself is unsure the line holds —
then holds the strongest capacity-based counter-case to that same untested
line and finds it doesn't answer the legal question at all.
