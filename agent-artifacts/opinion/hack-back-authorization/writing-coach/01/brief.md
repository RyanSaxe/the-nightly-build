# writing-coach brief: opinion/hack-back-authorization (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md — house standard, press voice, Opinion series direction
  (the paper takes a side and defends it)
- commission.md — the occasion, the stance, and the counter to answer

Output: writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md

Study the best argued opinion and legal-policy writers: those who state a
position plainly, build it from the public record, and give the opposing case its
own terms before answering. The subject runs through cyber policy and law, so
choose exemplars who argue technical-policy questions without hedging into a
both-sides summary and without a contrarian pose that skips the case.
