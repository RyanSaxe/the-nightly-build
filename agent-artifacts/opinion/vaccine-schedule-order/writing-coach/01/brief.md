Genre: an argued opinion column on a current executive action, for a
quantitatively literate, well-read audience. It pins a precise position, connects
it to the public record, gives the strongest opposing case its own terms before
answering it, and closes on the evidence that would change the paper's mind. The
register is a serious editorial voice arguing from first principles: it presses a
judgment hard, but every step is earned from cited evidence and it never
caricatures the other side. Study opinion and analytical writers the field rates
for arguing a contested public question with precision and intellectual honesty,
and for steelmanning an opponent before answering. Quote for that argued,
scrupulous register, not for this column's subject or facts, which the article must
establish from its own sources.
