Focus: an argued legal-policy opinion that discloses its position up front and
answers the strongest opposing case, for a well-read reader without assuming a
law degree. Study writers who argue a constitutional-law question with precision
and restraint — steelmanning the other side, tying each step to text and
precedent, committing to a verdict without polemic (strong legal essayists /
serious opinion writers the field respects). License only what changes sentences
here: how to state a precise legal position without hedging, how to write a
counter section that genuinely strengthens the opponent before answering, and
how to hold argumentative force at the paper's evidentiary bar. No catchphrases;
no house slogans; the guide must outlive the piece.
