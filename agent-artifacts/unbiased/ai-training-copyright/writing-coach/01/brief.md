The genre is the two-position explainer that presents a contested legal and
economic fight fairly, without tipping the paper's hand, for a technical reader
who wants the real structure of each side. Study writers who steelman opposing
positions rigorously and neutrally (the field rates certain long-form
explainer and legal-analysis writers for exactly this restraint). Quote for how
they hold neutrality while still being precise and readable, not for AI-copyright
content; a passage carrying either side's argument leaks material, so choose
passages that show even-handed craft on other disputes.
