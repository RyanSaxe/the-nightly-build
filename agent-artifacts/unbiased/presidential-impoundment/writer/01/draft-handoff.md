# Draft handoff: unbiased/presidential-impoundment (01)

## Original work

The piece prises the timely anchor apart from the durable question: it shows the
en banc D.C. Circuit blocked the clawback on the Inflation Reduction Act's own
mandatory appropriation, not the Impoundment Control Act, and then reframes the
whole fight around the seam both sides share — even the executive branch's 1969
Rehnquist OLC memo concedes an appropriation "permits but does not require"
spending — so the real contest narrows to whether a given appropriation commands
and whether any residual constitutional power to decline survives a clear one, a
question the fractured, repeal-entangled record leaves open.
