## Your job
Produce `voice-guide.md` here for a technology `brief`: 4-6 items, each a compact
judgment about why a development matters, for a technically fluent reader.

## Begin with these inputs
- This brief; `../../commission.md`; `../../editorial-direction.md` (register,
  brief identity, headline standard, press appetite for tech that changes what a
  field can do). Do not restate their rules.

## Study the best
Study at least three exemplars of excellent short technology writing the field
rates: writers who file a compact item with a real technical judgment and the
number the headline dropped (respected engineering/ML writers, a serious tech
desk's briefing notes). Prefer primary pieces. Do not study prior Nightly Build
briefs for voice.

## Settle for this writer
- Writing an item for a reader who can read a benchmark table: what to assume,
  what to state, how to add the caveat a vendor omitted without hedging.
- Item headline discipline (subject-verb-surprise; numbers only when they are
  the story; no colon-subtitle tell; no Betteridge question).
- Advancing a running story without re-explaining it.
- Varying item shape across 4-6 items so the brief does not read stamped.

## Constraints
How to write, not what to say. No catchphrases or reusable kickers. End with
`Recently used, do not reuse:` (AI-security lead by reflex, stacking AI-safety
items, formulaic kickers, hedged-contrast deks, reader-handoff closers).
