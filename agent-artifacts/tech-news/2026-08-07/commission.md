# Commission: tech-news/2026-08-07

## Assignment
The daily technology front page for Friday, 2026-08-07. Rolling brief on the
`brief` template. Select the day's 4-6 most consequential developments in the
technology field itself. AI is central but significance decides the mix.
Product promotion, incremental releases, and online attention do not qualify on
their own. Science and health belong when a result changes technical knowledge
or practice (treat the research as the development).

## Lane and cross-brief boundary (important)
Current-events runs the same night and takes the *public-consequence* stories.
- Developments in the field itself → **here**.
- The AI-authors copyright settlement is a legal/public-consequence story and
  runs in **current-events**; do NOT run it here.
- The DC Circuit clean-energy-funding ruling is tonight's **unbiased** anchor;
  not here.

## Candidate developments (researcher confirms, dates, and finalizes 4-6)
Prioritize genuine 2026-08-06/07 hooks with a primary record (the lab's paper,
the vendor's technical post, the journal article). Leads to check:
- Notable model releases / capability results around 08-06/07 (e.g. new Gemini
  or other frontier-model releases; verify what is technically new, not just
  the announcement). Primary = the model/technical report.
- A concrete AI-in-science deployment (e.g. FireSat / Earth Fire Alliance +
  Google Research wildfire-detection satellites) if a fresh, datable milestone
  lands. Primary = the program's own technical release.
- A science or health result (journal paper) that changes practice, if
  published on/around 08-07. Primary = the paper.
- Any datable infrastructure/hardware/open-source development of real technical
  consequence.
Each item: primary record + at least one independent account (per_item_sources:
primary [1,1], secondary [1,null]); min_sources 5 overall.

## Requirements
- brief template; 4-6 items. Significance, not category balance. Analyze what
  the development changes for the field; assume the reader saw the headline.
- The researcher owns finding and verifying the day's items and replacing any
  lead that does not hold up with a stronger documented one.

## Neighbors in this run
current-events brief (public-consequence news), unbiased (impoundment), plus
four non-news pieces. Coordinate only on the boundary above so the two briefs
do not print the same story.

## Habits not to inherit
- Avoid an all-LLM-vendor front page; mix in a genuine science/health or
  infrastructure result if the day supports it.
- Vary item dek shapes; do not stamp one mold across all items.
