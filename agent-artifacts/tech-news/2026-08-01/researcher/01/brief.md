# Researcher brief — tech-news/2026-08-01 (01)

## Your job
Select and verify the day's 4–6 most consequential technology developments
(window: ~2026-07-30 through 2026-08-01), then write the evidence record the
writer uses. You do the selection; the writer writes. Ground everything in
primaries you actually open and read.

## Exact inputs (start here)
- `agent-artifacts/tech-news/2026-08-01/commission.md`
- `agent-artifacts/tech-news/2026-08-01/editorial-direction.md`

## Selection standard (from the commission)
Significance decides, not topic or novelty. AI central but not exclusive; mix in
systems/hardware and at least one science/health result that changes practice.
Skip product promotion, point releases, and attention stories. A story whose
public/policy consequence is the real news belongs to Current Events — leave it.

## Per item, capture
- The **primary**: vendor model card / official announcement / arXiv paper /
  dataset / filing. Exact title, publisher, resolvable URL, date, and the
  specific figure/claim the item rests on (quote it). For a model: verify
  parameter count, license/open-weight status, modalities, context window, and
  any benchmark exactly as the primary states them — do not repeat aggregator
  numbers.
- At least **one independent secondary**: reputable original reporting, URL, and
  the corroborating detail.
- One-line "why it matters" judgment.
- primary/secondary classification with reason.

## Deliverable
`agent-artifacts/tech-news/2026-08-01/researcher/01/evidence.md`:
- 4–6 candidate items, strongest first, each as above; one spare if the day
  supports it so the writer can drop the weakest.
- What you left out and why (promotion/incremental/attention).
- Contradictions/uncertainties (unverified aggregator claims you could not
  confirm to a primary — flag or discard).
- Every URL resolves; a 403/paywall is gated — reach the primary another way;
  never record an unverified URL.

## Constraints
- Verify dates; this brief is dated 2026-08-01. Cite only what you read.
- Begin with the named inputs; focused web research only, no repo/archive tour.
- Missing context: `REQUEST researcher <one-sentence need>`.

## Report
End with: `DONE researcher agent-artifacts/tech-news/2026-08-01/researcher/01/evidence.md`
