# Commission — tech-news/2026-08-01

## Assignment
Tonight's technology brief on the `brief` template. 4–6 items. Each item is a
judgment about what moved in technology and why it matters. Date: 2026-08-01 (UTC).

## Selection standard
The day's (and last ~48 hours') most consequential technology developments. AI
is central but significance decides the mix, not topic. Product promotion,
incremental point releases, and online attention do not qualify on their own.
Science and health belong when a result changes technical knowledge or practice
enough to matter here (treat the research itself as the development). Field
developments live here; a technology story whose *public/policy consequences*
are the real news belongs to Current Events (which runs tonight — deconflict).

which must be verified against a **primary** (vendor model card / official
announcement / paper), with significance (not novelty) justifying inclusion:
- Moonshot AI **Kimi K3** — reported largest open-weight MoE (~2.8T params,
  multimodal, long context). Verify parameter count, license, modalities,
  context window against the official model card/repo; significance = open-weight

  capability change, not a version bump).
- At least one **science/health** result that changes technical practice, with a
  primary paper.
Drop any lead you cannot ground in a primary, and prefer significance over a
"models" monoculture: a strong brief mixes model, systems/hardware, and a
science result.

## Per-item source obligation
Each item: exactly **1 primary** (vendor announcement / model card / paper /
dataset / filing) + **≥1 independent secondary** (reputable original reporting).
Prefer US newsrooms when quality is comparable; use the primary regardless of
country. Headline links to the primary.

## Voice / contribution
Wire-service register for a technical reader: assume they saw the release; add
the number, the benchmark caveat, the architectural detail that makes it matter.
No hype, no benchmark-sheet regurgitation — a judgment about what actually moved.

## Habits not to inherit
Vary item shapes; no colon-subtitle headlines; no "faster, cheaper, smarter"
triads performing comprehensiveness; no item closing by handing the point back.
