Saturday-into-Sunday, 15-16 August 2026. Surface more than six candidates so the
writer can select. For each:
- The primary record that owns the development (the paper, model card, release
  notes, benchmark, official post, or filing), read at the passage that
  establishes the claim, with its URL resolving to that document's own page.
- At least one independent account from a reputable outlet reporting the same
  development.
- One or two lines on what the result or decision changes in technical knowledge
  or practice, so the writer can weigh significance over popularity.

  self-report or independently verified, and by whom.
Flag the one or two most consequential candidates.

Verify every number against the primary that owns it. Give each named system,
lab, or person the exact label the primary states, and do not inflate a
self-reported score into an established result. Record where accounts conflict.

and skip developments already covered unless there is a new, sourced turn (see
the commission's do-not-repeat list).
