consequential developments in technology, with artificial intelligence central

## Selection standard

Significance decides the mix. Product promotion, incremental releases, and online
attention do not qualify on their own. Science and health belong when a result
changes technical knowledge or practice enough to deserve attention here; treat
the research itself as the development rather than forcing it into a separate
category. Every item carries its primary record (the paper, the release notes, the
filing, the primary account) and at least one independent account.

## Boundaries and the split with current-events

Current-events runs the same night. A technology story whose news is its public
consequence (a ruling, a regulation, a trial, a breach's public fallout) belongs
there, not here; this brief covers developments in the field itself. The Made by
Google 2026 event (Pixel 11, Pixel Watch 5) on 2026-08-12 is product promotion and
does not qualify on its own unless a specific capability is the actual development.
Coordinate through the orchestrator if an item could sit in either brief.
