# researcher brief: tech-news/2026-08-14 (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, declared reader, series territory
- ../../commission.md — the frame, the no-repeat and coherence constraints, the per-item source rule

Output: ./evidence.md

Surface the most consequential real technology developments dated on or freshly
advancing around Friday, 14 August 2026: results and events that change technical
knowledge or practice. AI is central, but significance decides the mix; include
consequential science and health results where the research itself is the
development. Bring more candidates than the brief will use (aim for eight to
ten), so the writer can select the strongest four to six. For each candidate:
- The primary record (the paper, preprint, release notes, filing, or official
  statement), read for the actual result, with exact figures, dates, and
  locators verified against that primary. For a research result, the paper is the
  primary, not the press release.
- At least one independent account from a reputable newsroom; prefer a US
  newsroom when quality is comparable, and use the primary regardless of country.
- One line on why it matters technically, and whether it is genuinely new on 14
  August or a continuation of a thread the recent front pages already carried
  (the commission lists them).

Honor the coherence constraints: do not surface old research for reconstruction
(paper-of-the-day owns that), and treat any AI-training-copyright development as
at most a single short flagged item, since unbiased owns it tonight. Avoid making
another agent/harness security exploit the lead unless the day genuinely warrants
it.

Classify each source primary or secondary with the authorship-and-stake test.
Confirm every URL resolves to the source's own page. Record in Contradictions any
candidate where a self-reported benchmark or claim is unverified or disputed, so
the writer can weigh it honestly. The brief needs at least five sources total
across the items it runs, each item carrying its own primary and independent
account.
