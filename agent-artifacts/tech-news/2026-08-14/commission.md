## The brief

The daily technology front page for Friday, 14 August 2026. Four to six items,
selective, each with its primary record and at least one independent account.
Artificial intelligence is central, but significance decides the mix. Product
promotion, incremental releases, and online attention do not qualify on their
own. Science and health belong when a result changes technical knowledge or
practice enough to deserve attention here; treat the research itself as the

consequential real technology developments dated on or freshly advancing around
14 August 2026, with sourcing for each, and the writer selects and orders the
four to six that matter most. This commission sets the frame and coherence
constraints; it does not pick the items.

## Boundaries

- Brief template: 4–6 items, per-item primary [1,1] and secondary [1,null],
  minimum 5 sources total. Every item explains why it matters.
- For a research result, the paper or preprint is the primary; read the actual
  result, not a press release. Prefer a reputable US newsroom for independent
  reporting when quality is comparable; use the primary regardless of country.
- Each item's display text meets `spec/headlines.md`: the finding with its
  actors named, present tense for events, no triad-of-adjectives roundup line.
