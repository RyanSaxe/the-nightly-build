## The commission in one line
A 4-6 item technology brief (AI-central): each item a judgment about what moved,
carrying the technical number or caveat the headline dropped, standing alone, in
a calm serious register for a technical reader — not vendor copy, not a feed.

## What to study (at least three respected sources)
Study how the best technical news desks write a tight item without hype: the
compression of a strong research-newsletter brief, the skeptical precision of a
good independent-evaluation write-up (numbers over adjectives), and a
wire-service technology lede. Extract: how to state a capability claim and its
independent check in one item; how to strip vendor superlatives; how to add the
one technical caveat a reader needs; and how to vary item shape.
