# Commission: tech-news/2026-08-09

## What this edition covers

The daily technology front page for Sunday, 9 August 2026. Select the day's most
consequential developments in technology. Artificial intelligence is central,
but significance sets the mix; product promotion, an incremental release, and
online attention do not qualify on their own. Science and health belong when a
result changes technical knowledge or practice enough to deserve the space, and
the research itself is then the development. A Sunday runs lighter, so let
significance set the edition inside the four-to-six band rather than a count.

Current Events runs the same night and owns stories whose news is their public
policy or material consequence. This brief owns developments inside the field.
Where a story is both, decide which brief carries it and keep it out of the
other.

## Sourcing

- Template: `brief`. Required `nb-brief-item` per item, four to six items, and a
  `Sources` section. Source floor: 5 overall.
- Every item carries one primary record (the paper, the model card or technical
  report, the release notes, the advisory, the filing) and at least one
  independent account from outside the authoring party. A vendor's own post is
  primary for what the vendor did and never the independent confirmation of
  whether it holds up. Verify every figure, version, and benchmark against the
  primary.

## Template, tags

Tags follow the items selected; the writer sets them in `nb-meta`. Furniture
(`nb-brief-item`, and a `nb-table` or `nb-stat` where a comparison or figure has
a shape) carries evidence, never decoration.

## Distinct value and habits not to inherit

The recent run leans hard on AI-security incidents (agent exploits, prompt
injection, RCE disclosures) as the lead night after night. Where a real
disclosure is the day's most consequential development, run it; but do not reach
for the security beat by reflex, and let a peer-reviewed science or health result
lead when it is the more consequential development. The recent openers name a
finding as a located reversal ("...broke three coding agents at the harness, not
the model"); write the lead item's headline for its own news, not to that mold.
Avoid the banned three-clause comma-and-"and" dek. Lead with the single
development that matters most and say what happened to it.

## Boundaries

One edition, this date. Report the day's field developments; do not recap the
running AI storylines the paper has covered except where the day moves them.
Keep to four to six items, and keep public-policy stories in Current Events.
