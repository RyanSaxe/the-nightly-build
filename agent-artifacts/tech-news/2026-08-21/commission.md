# Commission: tech-news/2026-08-21

## Assignment
The technology front page for Friday, 2026-08-21. Four to six items, each a
development inside technology that changes what a field can do. Artificial
intelligence is central, but significance sets the mix. Product promotion,
incremental releases, and online attention do not qualify on their own. A
science or health result belongs when it changes technical knowledge or
practice enough to matter here; treat the research itself as the development.

## Boundaries
- Template `brief`: 4-6 items, per-item citation. Minimum 5 sources overall.
- Each item carries its primary record and at least one independent account
  (per-item: primary exactly 1, secondary 1 or more). Prefer a reputable US
  newsroom for the independent account; use the primary record whatever its
  country.

## Required contribution
Argue each item's significance from first principles. When a claim is a lab's
own benchmark, say so and weigh it as such, but do not let the skepticism become
the item's only content (see patterns below).

## Coordinate with tonight's edition
- Current Events (2026-08-21) runs the same night and covers public
  consequences of technology. Keep this brief to developments inside the field;
  leave public-policy fallout to Current Events. Do not share an item.

## Recent patterns to break (from the last two weeks' briefs)
- Dek: avoid the two-lab contrast mold ("X gates … while Y publishes …") and
  the standing skepticism qualifier ("unverified so far", "zero independently
  verified benchmark scores", "self-reports from the labs that produced them").
- Openers: avoid the release-plus-date sentence that pivots straight to the
  vendor's own benchmark claim.
- Closers: avoid the terse aphoristic contrast to end a section ("X, not Y";
  "where before …").
- Headlines: not every item should be an "[Actor] [verb]s [object]" news lede.
- Vendor-benchmark skepticism ("no independent evaluator", "the lab's own
  numbers", "own, unreproduced") is now a house tic. Use it once where it earns
  its place, not as every item's frame.
