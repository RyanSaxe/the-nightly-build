# Voice guide — tech-news/2026-08-21

## Sentence-level defaults
- Short, single-purpose sentences as the default; let one longer sentence
  carry a mechanism when the mechanism has several linked parts (the
  Marvell vesting structure, the diamond-to-fusion chain). Do not let a long
  sentence carry two unrelated ideas.
- Period over em-dash. Reach for the dash only for a real interruption —
  the isolated-vm item uses one to break in with the researcher's own
  correction ("V8's Isolate boundary held") because that is a real aside,
  not connective tissue.
- No semicolons planned in this draft. If one shows up in revision, it needs
  two independent clauses tight enough that a period would over-separate
  them — check before keeping it.

## Named things, kept named
- "Astra" always for OpenAI's next model, never "the new model" once named.
- "Isolated-vm" for the library, "ExternalCopy" for the specific mechanism —
  do not vary to "the tool" or "the function" for variety.
- "Flexoelectricity" is defined once, in the sentence it first appears, then
  used bare after that.
- Figures stay in the form the source gives them: "$206.58 per share," not
  "roughly $200"; "7,300 Kelvin," not "extremely high temperature."

## Attribution
Every named claim traces to a person or organization: Staicu (Endor Labs),
Iyengar and Ajayan (Rice, quoted), Millot and Eggert (Lawrence Livermore),
OpenAI's own statement (quoted once, flagged as self-reported), Hugging
Face's forensic account. No "researchers say" or "experts note" — the
paper or the press release always has a name attached, and every item here
does.

## The one place skepticism belongs
OpenAI's determination that Astra "cannot rule out Critical capability" is
the company's own internal evaluation, not an independently reproduced
result. Say so once, plainly, where that claim appears. Do not carry the
qualifier into any other item — the Marvell filing, the Hugging Face
forensic count, the two physics papers, and the Endor Labs writeup are not
self-reported marketing claims and do not need the hedge.

## What "teach, don't summarize" means for each item
- OpenAI/Hugging Face: the reader should finish understanding why an
  evaluation sandbox and a production system being unrelated is exactly
  what made this different from a red-team drill.
- Marvell/Google: the reader should finish understanding that the warrant's
  value is conditional on future purchasing, not a grant — the structure
  is the story, not the headline number.
- isolated-vm: the reader should finish understanding where the failure
  actually sat (the marshaling code, not the isolation primitive itself).
- Graphene: the reader should finish understanding that sharpness, not
  size, drove the effect, and why that is a fabrication-relevant distinction.
- Diamond/fusion: the reader should finish understanding the causal chain
  from a corrected melting curve to a specific claim about laser energy
  budget, not just told that the result "has implications."
