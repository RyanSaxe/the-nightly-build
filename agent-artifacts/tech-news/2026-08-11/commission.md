# Commission: tech-news/2026-08-11

## Assignment

The technology brief for Tuesday, 11 August 2026. Template `brief`: 4 to 6
itemized developments, each carrying its own primary record and at least one
independent account. Authorized scheduled work from `nb duty`.

## Territory and selection

Select the day's most consequential developments in technology, with artificial
intelligence central but significance deciding the mix. Product promotion,
incremental releases, and online attention do not qualify on their own. Science
and health belong when a result changes technical knowledge or practice enough
to deserve attention here; treat the research itself as the development.

Anchor selection to what moved on or into 11 August 2026. When a story this desk
already covered has developed, say so and build on it. Recent tech briefs have
covered: OpenAI's Astra/Lean 4 proof claims and the effort to verify them
(08-04, 08-10), superconductivity in pentalayer graphene surviving a high
in-plane field (08-09), the coding-agent harness RCE disclosed at Black Hat
(08-08), an AI agent smuggling malicious code caught by a maintainer plus the
UK AISI unauthorized-action counts (08-07), the anellovirus/long-COVID IMPACC
correlation (08-06), and the Qwen3.8-Max weightless release (08-05). Only carry
one of these forward on a genuine 11 August development.

  material-conditions news. Do not run the same underlying event both desks would
  cover. Tie-break: a development that changes technical knowledge or practice is

  current-events's.

  `unbiased` piece. Do not run an AI-copyright fair-use item here.

## Sources

Per item: exactly one primary (the paper, the model card or weights, the
security advisory, the official announcement, the dataset) and at least one
independent secondary, preferring a reputable US newsroom of comparable quality.
Use the primary record regardless of country. Every cited URL must resolve to the
source's own page. Verify every figure against the primary that owns it.

## Required contribution

Each item adds what the announcement dropped: the benchmark's fine print, the
caveat, the number that sizes the claim. The brief is a judgment about what
advanced, not a recap of press releases.

## Habits not to inherit

  stamp every item to one mold.
- Do not let AI crowd out a genuinely more consequential science or health
  result; significance decides the mix, not category.
- The tags array in nb-meta is the writer's to set from the items actually run.
