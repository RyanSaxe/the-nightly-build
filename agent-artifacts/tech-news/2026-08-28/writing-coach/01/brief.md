The genre is a technology wire brief for a machine-learning engineer: several
short, self-standing items, each a judgment about what actually moved. Study
writers who report a technical development precisely without hype and without a
tutorial (Simon Willison's news notes, the Economist's technology items, a
strong primary-source-first explainer). The register is the paper's calm,
first-principles baseline, skeptical of unverified claims.

This round's focus: how an item states what is confirmed and flags what is not,
in a technical register, and stops on the caveat rather than a line handed back
to the reader. The brief template bars that closer.
