# Commission: tech-news/2026-08-12

## Assignment

The daily technology brief for Wednesday, 12 August 2026. Template `brief`
(shortread; 4 to 6 items; each item one primary record and at least one
independent secondary account). Authorized scheduled work from `nb duty`: rolling
mode, the selected UTC date is unpublished, one article.

## What the brief covers

Select the day's most consequential developments in technology. Artificial
intelligence is central, but significance decides the mix, not a quota. Product
promotion, incremental releases, and online attention do not qualify on their own.
Science and health belong when a result changes technical knowledge or practice
enough to deserve attention here; treat the research itself as the development.
A story whose news is its public or political consequence belongs to Current
Events, not here.

The item set and the day's through-line are the researcher's and writer's to
determine from what actually happened on 12 August 2026. This commission fixes the
frame, the boundaries, and the coverage this edition has already spent.

## What the brief must do

Each item states the development and the immediate technical context from a
primary record, with the headline carrying the result. Where a claim is a lab's
self-report, say so and attribute it; do not launder a self-reported benchmark
into an established fact. Where a figure carries an item (a parameter count, a
benchmark score, a measured quantity), verify it against the primary that owns it
and give its scope.

## Sources

Template floor: `min_sources` 5. Per item: exactly one primary record, and at
least one independent secondary account. Prefer the primary (the paper, the model
card, the release, the filing) regardless of country. Every recorded URL resolves
to the source's own page.

## Required contribution

A reader finishes knowing the day's developments that actually change what a field
can do, which reported numbers are independently established and which are
self-reported, and the figures that size each result.
