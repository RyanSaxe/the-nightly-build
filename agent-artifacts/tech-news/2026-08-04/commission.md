# Commission: tech-news/2026-08-04

## Authorized work
Scheduled duty for UTC 2026-08-04 returned `tech-news` (rolling, brief template)
for the unpublished date 2026-08-04. Publish exactly one brief for that date.

## What this brief is
The daily technology front page for 2026-08-04: 4-6 selected items, each
explaining why it matters, every item carrying at least one primary record and at
least one independent account. Rolling news: the subject is the day's actual
technology developments, selected in research against live reporting. The
researcher assembles the candidate set and the primary records; the writer selects
and orders the final 4-6.

## Selection standard (from the series prompt)
- The day's most consequential developments in technology. AI is central, but
  significance decides the mix. Product promotion, incremental releases, and
  online attention do not qualify on their own.
- Science and health belong when a result changes technical knowledge or practice
  enough to deserve attention here; treat the research itself as the development.
- Independent reporting: prefer a reputable US newsroom of comparable quality; use
  the primary record regardless of country; use non-US reporting when it holds
  important original reporting or is closer to the event.

## Sourcing
Brief template: 4-6 items; per item primary exactly 1 (the paper, filing, release
notes, advisory, or benchmark that owns the claim), secondary 1+. Verify every
figure against its primary; for a research result, open the paper, not the
press release. Confirm every URL resolves to the source's own page (e.g., the
arXiv abstract page, not a fetch endpoint).

## Template and policy
- Template: `brief` (fixed). `min_sources: 5`; per-item primary [1,1], secondary
  [1, null].
- Production policy (balanced): editor required, high effort, model inherit.
  Actual harness: writing-coach = Sonnet; researcher, writer, editor = Opus.

## Boundaries — do not repeat, and non-overlap with this edition
- Recent tech-news slugs are dated (2026-07-27 … 2026-08-03) and have leaned
  heavily on: open-weight model releases, model-vs-model benchmark races,
  AI-security disclosures/CVEs, and Claude/Anthropic cryptography findings. Do not
  re-file a running story without a genuinely new, sourced development, and vary
  the mix so the brief is not four model-release items.
- **Non-overlap with current-events (same run):** a development whose news is its
  *public/policy consequence* belongs to current-events, not here. Keep the two
  briefs' item sets disjoint.
- **Non-overlap with paper-of-the-day (same run):** paper-of-the-day is a
  reconstruction of the 2015 ResNet paper. Do not let a tech-news item become a
  foundational-paper explainer; tech-news items are current developments.
- Headlines/deks: state the finding with actors named; avoid colon subtitles and
  the comma-triad dek; check recent deks before settling.
