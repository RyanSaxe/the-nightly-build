# Evidence record: company-analysis/palantir (01)

The evidence firmly supports the article's factual spine from primary sources: Palantir's Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026) total revenue of $1,935,464 thousand (+93% YoY), the U.S. commercial re-acceleration (revenue $764M, +149% YoY; a five-quarter series is verified quarter-by-quarter for a chart), GAAP operating margin of 47% and GAAP net margin of ~55%, stock-based compensation of $265,209 thousand with only trivial YoY dilution, remaining-deal-value and RPO figures, customer count, the full-year 2026 guidance raise to $8.150–8.158B, and a defensible forward EV/sales multiple computed from the 10-Q share count and a recorded market price. It is thin or contradictory in five places the writer must handle carefully: (1) the commission's stated report date "August 5, 2026" is wrong — results were announced August 3, 2026; (2) the commission's "triple-digit P/E" is not supported on a forward basis (forward P/E is ~85x, secondary; the company issues no EPS guidance, so no forward P/E is primary); (3) net dollar retention is disclosed in neither the 10-Q nor the earnings release, so no NDR figure can be cited; (4) GAAP net income exceeds operating income and adjusted net income because of a ~1.4% effective tax rate and non-operating gains, so GAAP net margin overstates operating profitability; and (5) a chart-quality peer valuation set at a single consistent as-of date could not be assembled from accessible sources — the peer multiples below are scattered across dates and sources and are directional only. One piece of contradictory context deserves prominence: the stock entered this print roughly 40% below its 52-week high (secondary), which complicates any simple "priced for perfection, one stumble away" framing.

## Contradictions

- **Report date.** The commission and brief say the report was "August 5, 2026." The primary record disagrees: the earnings release is dated **August 3, 2026**, the Form 8-K carrying it is dated 2026-08-03, and the Form 10-Q was filed 2026-08-04. Use August 3, 2026.
- **"Triple-digit P/E."** The commission asserts a "triple-digit P/E." On a **forward** basis this is not supported: the forward P/E is ~85x (secondary, analyst-estimate-derived; GuruFocus, 2026-08-03), and Palantir issues no net-income/EPS guidance, so there is no primary forward P/E to cite. A trailing GAAP P/E could plausibly be triple-digit, but it cannot be computed from the sources read here (only Q2 2026 and Q2 2025 net income are in hand, not the full trailing twelve months), so no trailing figure is asserted. The writer should say "high-double-digit forward P/E (~85x)" or compute a trailing P/E from a full TTM net-income series, not repeat "triple-digit" unqualified.
- **GAAP net income > operating income, and adjusted net income < GAAP net income.** GAAP net income ($1,065,962K) exceeds income from operations ($912,004K) because of interest income ($77,505K), other income ($91,836K), and a near-zero tax provision ($15,383K, a 1.42% effective rate). GAAP net margin (~55%) therefore overstates operating profitability (47% operating margin). The non-GAAP reconciliation runs the other way: adjusted net income to common ($1,047,001K) is **below** GAAP net income to common ($1,061,890K), because the release's "income tax effects and adjustments" of $(297,357)K (a normalized tax) outweigh the SBC and payroll-tax add-backs of $282,468K. Both GAAP and adjusted diluted EPS round to $0.41. Handle the tax rate honestly: the GAAP net-income headline is flattered by an unusually low effective tax rate.
- **RPO vs. RDV.** The 10-Q's remaining performance obligations are **$4.9 billion** (Note 3), materially smaller than the release-highlighted U.S. commercial **remaining deal value of $6.238 billion**. These are different metrics: RPO excludes cancelable and optional amounts (much government work), while RDV counts the total remaining value of all contracts. Do not conflate them or imply RDV is a GAAP backlog.
- **Net dollar retention is absent.** Neither the 10-Q nor the earnings release states a net dollar retention rate. Any NDR figure would have to come from the separate investor presentation/earnings-call deck (not a filed document and not read here). Do not cite an NDR number as verified.
- **The acceleration is U.S.-only; international lags.** "Rest of world" revenue grew from $271,105K (Q2 2025) to $362,417K (Q2 2026), ~+34% YoY, versus U.S. revenue +115%. International share of revenue fell from 27% to 19%. Any "broad-based global acceleration" reading is unsupported; the story is U.S. commercial.
- **Revenue vs. receivables concentration.** MD&A states no customer was more than 10% of total revenue, yet accounts-receivable concentration shows "Customer I" at 27% of receivables as of June 30, 2026. These measure different things (period revenue vs. balance-sheet receivables); do not present the 27% as a revenue-concentration figure.
- **Pre-print drawdown vs. "priced for perfection."** The stock closed $125.65 the day of the release, ~40% below its 52-week high (secondary), before jumping ~15% after hours. The market had already de-rated the name substantially, which cuts against a naive "extreme valuation, one stumble away" premise. Steelman both reads with this context in view.
