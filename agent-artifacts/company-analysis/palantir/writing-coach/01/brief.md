Focus: a chart-forward market-and-business analysis that teaches an unfamiliar
company as part of an argument, for a reader with strong quantitative background
but no assumed knowledge of Palantir. Study writers who analyze a company's
numbers to answer a market question with precision and without hype or tip-sheet
tone (serious equity/business analysts and the best financial explanatory
writing). License only what changes sentences here: how to interleave taught
business context with argument, how to state a figure with the comparison that
makes it mean something, and how to weigh a valuation question without issuing
advice. No catchphrases; the guide must outlive the piece.
