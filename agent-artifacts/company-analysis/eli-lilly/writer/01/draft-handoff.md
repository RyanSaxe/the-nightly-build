# draft-handoff: company-analysis/eli-lilly (writer 01)

## Original work
This piece separates Lilly's thrice-raised 2026 guidance into its volume and
realized-price legs across two quarters and three geographies, shows on one
chart that the only price line that looks like it is recovering (the US) is
flattered by rebate/discount estimate adjustments while every other line is
persistent or deepening, and converts "can volume outrun price" into a single
falsifiable test: whether the ex-US price line steps down again from −36% as
ex-US volume cools from +113%.

## Commission decisions applied
- Tariff framed as a contingent future risk Lilly is presently largely exempt
  from (10-Q exemption language, onshoring 20% and MFN 0%-until-2029 paths,
  $4.5B US expansion, generics/biosimilars excluded), not a live US-price
  overhang; corrected effective date to July 31, 2026 (Annex III). It gets one
  of three sub-points in the risks section, no more.
- Decomposition presented as Lilly's *own* reported volume-vs-price split, taken
  as reported and corroborated only by the per-product/geographic tables, with
  the unquantified FX/other residual stated (60−13=47 vs 48 reported; 113−36=77
  vs 80 ex-US). No invented "mix" leg or unit-volume figure.
- No buy/sell/allocation call. The verdict speaks only to which leg carries the
  growth and the condition under which it stops.
