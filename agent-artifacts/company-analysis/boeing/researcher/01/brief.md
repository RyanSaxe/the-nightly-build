# Researcher brief — company-analysis/boeing (invocation 01)

## Role
Read and verify the sources for this article, then write the exact evidence
record the writer and editor use. Follow `skills/researcher/SKILL.md`. Drafting
belongs to the writer.

## The question
Does Boeing's cash-flow and delivery record show genuine commercial-aircraft cash
conversion, or is reported operational progress (revenue, deliveries, narrowing
losses, record backlog) outrunning the cash the business generates? The sharp,
answerable version from the primary filing: how much of the reported operating
cash flow is customer prepayment (advances) versus cash converted from delivered
jets, and is free cash flow yet self-funding?

## Source obligations (from commission)
- `consult` FIRST: the SEC filing itself. Lock the exact quarter. Read the latest
  Boeing 10-Q and the matching earnings 8-K/press-release exhibit on EDGAR before
  any secondary coverage.
- min_sources 8. Primary owns the claim (the filing, Boeing's own delivery/order
  data, a party's statement about itself). Secondary = independent reporting for
  context only. Contested figures need the primary. Record kind + locator per
  citation.
- Verify every figure against the filing that owns it. Do not trust a secondary's
  restatement. Confirm every URL resolves (403/paywall is gated, not dead).

## What the writer needs verified (name the owning primary for each)
1. The headline surface: Q2 2026 revenue and YoY; net loss and core loss/share;
   Q2 and H1 commercial deliveries; total and BCA backlog; jets in backlog.
2. The cash spine: operating cash flow and free cash flow (Boeing's definition),
   Q2 and H1 2026 vs year-ago; capital expenditures.
3. The decomposition: the cash-flow-statement working-capital lines for the six
   months — Advances and progress billings, Inventories, and the net-loss and
   operating-cash subtotal — so the writer can show what drove the operating-cash
   swing. Preserve the full series (Q1'25 through Q2'26) so a quarterly free-cash-
   flow chart is possible; note any figure that is derived by subtraction.
4. Segment detail: BCA, BDS, BGS revenue and operating result; any abnormal/
   reach-forward charge (e.g., VC-25B).
5. Balance-sheet context: advances balance at period-end vs prior year-end;
   inventory balance and commercial-program portion; cash and debt.
6. Context (secondary, clearly marked): the FAA 737 monthly production cap and
   its recent increase, as the regulatory ceiling on how fast backlog converts.

## Seek what breaks the angle
Steelman the bull case: rising advances = a healthy, growing order book and a real
production ramp, which is normal and structural for aircraft manufacturing; a
positive-cash quarter and improving YoY half. Record the strongest version so the
writer must weigh it, plus the caution that a single quarter's FCF is noisy and
seasonal.

## Output
`researcher/01/evidence.md` per the SKILL: opening paragraph on what the evidence
supports and where it is thin; then Sources (URL, primary/secondary + reason, what
it establishes, verbatim locators), Contradictions, Numbers (owning primary, exact
reading, unit, denominator, period; full series for the chart), Source assets,
Discarded. Make each claim traceable enough to reopen cold.

## Return
`DONE researcher <evidence-path>`.
