# Writing-coach brief — company-analysis/boeing (invocation 01)

## Role
You are the writing coach. Study how the best writers in this domain actually
write, then hand the writer a practical craft standard for this one article.
Follow `skills/writing-coach/SKILL.md`.

## Domain and genre
Business/markets analysis of a public company. The piece uses Boeing's Q2 2026
SEC filing to answer one question: is Boeing's reported cash-flow recovery real
cash conversion, or is customer prepayment and accounting profit outrunning the
cash the business generates? The register must teach a numerate but non-aerospace
reader how to read a manufacturer's cash-flow statement against its order book.

## Exact inputs
- This brief.
- `editorial-direction.md` (house floor, headline standard, press voice §3
  Mitchell-Hashimoto baseline, template identity, series prompt).
- `commission.md` (assignment, angle, reader, non-repetition list).
Study exemplars via web tools only. Do not read the repository, prior articles,
or the archive for voice or structure.

## What to produce
`writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md`, exactly per the SKILL:
- A concise lead directive: register, reader relationship, and only the moves
  that will change sentences in THIS article (explaining cash-flow mechanics to
  a first-principles reader; grounding every abstraction in a filing number;
  distinguishing reported fact, estimate, synthesis; one earned contrast ceiling;
  no self-grading, no stock-revelation frames).
- End the directive with `Recently used, do not reuse:` carrying the commission's
  negative constraints (the AI-capex trio openers, the "backlog vs. X" framing as
  a template, colon-subtitle headlines, hedged-question deks, recurring section
  shapes).
- Then at least three exemplar blocks in the SKILL's format, each led by a real
  `Source: https://…` line for a writer the field rates, studied firsthand, with
  a short calibration passage. Study genuine markets/finance writers on cash flow
  vs. earnings and disclosure-reading.

## Constraints
Specify how to write, never what to say. No named persona to imitate, no coined
catchphrases, no article-ready lines. Calibration passages stay in the guide.

## Return
`DONE writing-coach <voice-guide-path>`.
