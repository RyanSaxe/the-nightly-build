# Writing-coach brief — company-analysis/apple (01)

## Your job
Produce a transferable **voice guide** for a chart-forward market-and-business
analysis (1500–4000 words) that teaches a company's economics while answering a
market question. Study how the best business/markets writers explain a segment
mix and a margin bridge to a smart non-specialist without jargon or hype.
Transferable craft only — no persona, no reusable line.

## Exact inputs (start here)
- `agent-artifacts/company-analysis/apple/commission.md`
- `agent-artifacts/company-analysis/apple/editorial-direction.md`

## Deliver
`agent-artifacts/company-analysis/apple/writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md`, covering:
- Register for the house reader (math/CS, ML-eng): calm, first-principles;
  teach the business as part of the argument, not a glossary.
- How to weave company/segment explanation into the analysis (place it where it
  clarifies, not a stock "overview" section).
- How to use numbers well: give each figure a comparison the reader already
  holds; separate one-time from recurring; keep reported fact / estimate /
  synthesis distinct (the tariff-refund and the Services miss are the test).
- How to integrate charts as evidence (what a chart should carry vs. prose), and
  captions that state the source honestly.
- Committing a synthesis without issuing a buy/sell call; the banned slop tells.

## Method
Study at least three respected markets/business writers. Extract technique.
~One page. Do not draft the article.
