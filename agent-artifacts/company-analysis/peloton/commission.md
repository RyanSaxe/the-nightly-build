# Commission: company-analysis/peloton

## Assignment

Investigate what Peloton's first full fiscal-year GAAP net profit does and does
not prove about the business, using the just-reported fiscal 2026 results (the
year ended June 30, 2026) as the current record. The market question the company
makes answerable: when a business reaches profitability after years of losses,
how does a reader separate earnings power that will persist from a profit
produced by contraction and cost-cutting? Peloton is the case; the transferable
subject is the quality and durability of a reported profit.

## Why this company now

Peloton reported its first-ever full-year net profit in its fiscal 2026 results
in August 2026, and the stock fell on the report as the outlook disappointed. A
headline profit the market discounted is the record that makes the question
answerable: the disagreement is inside the filing, not in outside opinion.

## Angle and boundaries

- Teach the business from zero: connected-fitness hardware alongside a recurring
  membership subscription, and why the two streams carry very different margins,
  and churn behaves differently for each. Place that explanation where the
  argument needs it, not in a fixed overview paragraph.
- Keep three things the reader must not conflate distinct throughout: revenue
  direction, reported profit, and cash generation. Show where the fiscal 2026
  profit came from and whether the top line is still shrinking underneath it.
- Do not issue a buy, sell, hold, or allocation call. The earned verdict is about
  earnings quality and durability, never price.
- Assume no prior knowledge of the company.

## Evidence spine (the researcher verifies and may correct this)

Read Peloton's own fiscal 2026 filings before any coverage of them: the 10-K if
filed, the Q4 and full-year shareholder letter and 8-K, the cash-flow statement,
and subscription and segment detail. The series consults sec.gov: read the filing
itself, not the write-ups of it. Prior-year figures establish the trend, and
independent reporting supplies market-reaction context. Source floor: the article
template requires at least 8 sources.

## Furniture

Company Analysis is chart-forward. Where a trend or comparison is the argument
(revenue by stream over time, the path to profitability, connected-fitness
subscribers and churn, free cash flow), use a committed chart built only from the
evidence record's verified series, per spec/charts.md. Furniture carries
evidence, not decoration.
