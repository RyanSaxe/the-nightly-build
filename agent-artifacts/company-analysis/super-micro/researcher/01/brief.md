# researcher brief: company-analysis/super-micro (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md — citation standard, series territory, declared reader
- commission.md — the market question, the four things the analysis must
  resolve, and the primary sources to begin from

Output: researcher/01/evidence.md

Read the filing itself before any coverage. The margin jump (about 17.5% from
about 9.9%), the revenue figures, EPS, and the FY2027 guidance are
market-moving, so verify each against the SEC 8-K Exhibit 99.1 and the IR
release, not secondary reporting. Chase the cause of the margin step in the
filing's own language and any accompanying commentary: mix shift, a one-off, or
durable. Pull the cash-flow statement and the inventory and receivables lines so
the writer can compare reported profit with operating cash and working capital.
Preserve full quarterly series (gross margin, revenue) so a chart can be built.
Handle the accounting history (Hindenburg 2024, delayed report, auditor
resignation, near-delisting, refiling) from primary or on-record sources only,
and record what the record does and does not establish. Record contradictions,
especially any gap between the headline margin and the cash picture.
