This is a cumulative teaching lesson for a reader with a strong quantitative
background who is new to investing judgment. The register is calm, first-
principles, and patient: build each idea before the sentence that needs it, and
let a worked number carry the point. Study writers who teach a quantitative
financial idea without either dumbing it down or hiding behind jargon. The
lesson leans on one company's real numbers to show a value range, so favor
exemplars who reason out loud from figures. The lesson template allows its two
bookend cards (Why this matters, The takeaway) to address the reader; the body
does not.
