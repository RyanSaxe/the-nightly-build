Genre: a lesson in an cumulative investing course (roughly 1200-2200 words) for a
quantitatively strong reader, teaching why returns on capital fade and how the
durability of a competitive advantage drives value more than its current level. It
builds on already-taught lessons, teaches one concept with a worked calculation,
and leaves the reader able to interrogate a business's advantage. The register is
a patient teacher who builds each idea before the sentence that needs it, in the
manner of the clearest explanatory finance writing: concrete, worked from
examples, never lecturing. Study writers the field rates for teaching investing and
economics judgment plainly, and for making a quantitative idea intuitive without
dumbing it down. Quote for that patient teaching voice, not for this lesson's
facts or figures, which the article must establish from its own sources. The
lesson template allows its two bookend cards to address the reader directly; the
body does not.
