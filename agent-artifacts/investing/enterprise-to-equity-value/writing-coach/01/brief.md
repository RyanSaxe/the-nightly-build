The genre is the finance lesson that teaches a valuation mechanic to a
mathematically literate reader from first principles, building each idea before
the sentence that needs it. Study writers who teach investing and accounting
clearly and rigorously (Aswath Damodaran's writing is the register to study, and
other teachers the field rates for making a calculation legible). Quote for how
they build an idea and walk a number, not for the enterprise-value-bridge content
itself; a passage that hands this lesson its example or its explanation leaks
material, so pick passages that show teaching craft on other subjects.
