# researcher brief: investing/enterprise-to-equity-value (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, declared reader, series territory
- ../../commission.md — the two-step bridge, the one-company worked example, the transferability requirement

Output: ./evidence.md

Establish, from sources you open:
- The enterprise-value-to-equity bridge as an authority defines it: each claim
  subtracted (net debt, minority/noncontrolling interest, preferred stock,
  material unfunded pension and finance-lease obligations) and each non-operating
  asset added (excess cash, marketable securities, equity stakes), and *why* each
  belongs, from a recognized valuation authority (Damodaran's materials, a CFA/
  corporate-finance reference). Record the reasoning, not just the formula.
- The diluted share count: the treasury-stock method for in-the-money options,
  and how RSUs and convertibles enter the count. Cite the authority and, for the
  worked example, the filing.
- One real company suited to the worked example: material net debt and
  meaningful option/convertible dilution both present, so the bridge clearly
  moves value per share. From its most recent 10-K or 10-Q, record the exact
  lines: total debt, cash and equivalents, marketable securities, any minority
  interest and preferred, basic and diluted share counts, and outstanding
  in-the-money options/RSUs if disclosed. Give locators (statement, page or note).
  Verify every figure against the filing that owns it; do not use a data
  aggregator's derived "enterprise value" as a source for the components.
- A defensible enterprise-value figure for that company to start the bridge from,
  and state plainly how it was derived so the writer can either reuse it or
  compute the bridge from an assumed EV. The lesson's point is the bridge, so the
  EV may be taken as given as long as its basis is stated.

Record in Contradictions any place the bridge is genuinely contested (for
example, treatment of leases or of gross vs. net debt), so the lesson can teach
the judgment rather than assert one convention. Six sources that change the
picture, not padding.
