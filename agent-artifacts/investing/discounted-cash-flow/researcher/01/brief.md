Research questions (prefer primary/authoritative finance sources — e.g. Damodaran,
CFA/curriculum-grade references, and, if a real company is used, its filings):
- The DCF identity and terminal-value mechanics: authoritative statements of
  enterprise value = PV of free cash flows at WACC; the perpetuity-growth
  (Gordon) terminal value TV = FCF*(1+g)/(r-g) and the exit-multiple
  alternative; the enterprise-to-equity bridge (subtract net debt). Cite
  sources that own these formulas.
- Evidence for the lesson's spine — that the terminal value dominates a typical
  DCF and that the output is highly sensitive to r and g: find sourced figures
  or worked demonstrations of terminal value as a share of total DCF value, and
  of sensitivity to small changes in r and g.
- If a concrete company example is chosen (NOT Costco or Apple — those are
  worn), supply real, sourced inputs (FCF, an estimated WACC consistent with
  the earlier cost-of-capital lesson, a defensible g) from the company's own
  filings. Otherwise supply clean illustrative numbers and label them as such.
- Numbers section: give each figure the argument leans on with its owner and
  scope. Preserve any full series useful for a sensitivity chart.
