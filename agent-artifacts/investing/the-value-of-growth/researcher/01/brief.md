# researcher brief: investing/the-value-of-growth (01)

Inputs:
- `agent-artifacts/investing/the-value-of-growth/editorial-direction.md` — citation standard, series territory, declared reader
- `agent-artifacts/investing/the-value-of-growth/commission.md` — the concept, the worked example, and the boundaries

Output: `agent-artifacts/investing/the-value-of-growth/researcher/01/evidence.md`
Source policy: minimum 6 sources.

Research questions the evidence record must answer:

- What is the authoritative statement of the relationship between value, growth,
  return on invested capital, and the cost of capital — that growth adds value
  only when the return on reinvested capital exceeds the cost of capital? Cite
  the recognized valuation treatments and the writers finance rates on this
  point, and record the exact formulation each uses (for example, value as a
  function of growth, ROIC, and WACC, or the reinvestment-rate identity
  g = ROIC × reinvestment rate).
- What is the cleanest way to express, in a form the writer can set as an
  equation, how the value of a growing cash stream depends on the spread between
  return on capital and the cost of capital? Record the formula and its source.
- Provide the ingredients for a two-company worked example: the standard growing-
  perpetuity / value-driver formula and realistic parameter ranges so the writer
  can construct two firms with equal growth and different returns on capital and
  show value diverging. The numbers can be illustrative, but the formula and its
  behavior must be sourced.
- What is the strongest caveat or contested point (for example, when growth is
  value-neutral, the limits of the constant-growth assumption, or empirical
  debate over whether high returns persist)? Record contradictions in full.

Classify each source primary or secondary. Preserve any formula exactly as its
source states it. Source assets are unlikely for a lesson; write `None found`
unless a source's own figure would teach better than prose. Report in your
handoff whether the sourced formula supports the sign-change claim cleanly or
needs qualification.
