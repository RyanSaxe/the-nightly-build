The genre is a cumulative teaching lesson on a quantitative finance concept for a
mathematically fluent reader who is new to investing vocabulary. Study writers
who teach a technical idea by building it one step at a time, grounding each
abstraction in a worked case before generalizing — the house baseline names the
patience of Chris Olah and Lilian Weng for teaching; the best finance explainers
who reason from first principles about capital and returns belong here too.
Prefer their primary work. Choose passages that show how a strong teacher makes a
quantitative idea concrete and keeps the reader oriented, not passages about
growth, return on capital, or the cost of capital, so the writer inherits the
teaching craft and not this lesson's argument. The lesson template allows its
bookend cards to speak to the reader; the guide may note that those cards can be
direct, but the register everywhere else stays the calm, first-principles voice.
