# Editorial review: investing/working-capital (editor/02)

A scoped fresh read of the revision. Editor/01 approved everything except one
substantive break: the AutoZone passage's false causal framing (operating cash
above net income attributed to "growing payables released cash," e.l.f.'s gap
"with the sign reversed," "growth pays for itself"). This read confirms the fix
and checks that nothing settled regressed.

## Skeptic

The correction holds, and it is taught as the right thing: a structural (stock)
inversion, kept distinct from a period flow.

- **The false flow claim is gone.** The consequence paragraph now opens on the
  stock/flow distinction outright ("That negative cycle is a fact about the
  levels on AutoZone's balance sheet, and it is easy to mistake for a claim
  about a single year's cash") and states the FY2025 change in working capital
  as a **use** of about $311 million, "because its inventory grew more in dollars
  than its payables did." Operating cash above net income is attributed to
  non-cash add-backs ("non-cash charges like depreciation added back to profit,
  not working capital handing over cash") — the same mechanism named for e.l.f.
  No sentence claims any AutoZone year's working-capital change was a source of
  cash. The "sign reversed" parallel is removed.

- **Re-verified against researcher/02/evidence.md and the primary (R6).** FY2025
  change in working capital net use $(311,055)K (AR (118,606) + inventory
  (893,908) + AP/accrued +657,408 + income taxes +68,447 + other (24,396));
  balance-sheet inventory rose $870,470K against payables' $669,889K, so
  inventory grew more in dollars — the article's stated reason. OCF $3,117,337K
  over NI $2,498,247K reconciles as 2,498,247 + 930,145 add-backs − 311,055 WC
  use = 3,117,337, the excess coming entirely from the $930,145K of add-backs
  (chiefly D&A $613,199K). Structural facts unchanged and correct: net working
  capital negative $1,178,018K ("about $1.18 billion"), payables 114.2% of
  inventory ("about 114%"), CCC ≈ −33 days (table −33.1). All figures tie.

- **Reverse-cycle opener** now reads as structure, not a flow event: "A negative
  cycle describes the levels a business settles at: it collects each sale before
  it has to pay for the goods behind it, so its suppliers, and not its own cash,
  carry the inventory on its shelves" (s6). The "suppliers, and not its own cash"
  contrast is earned — it corrects the default assumption that a business funds
  its own inventory — not an invented strawman.

- **Body closer** reframed from "pay a retailer to grow" to the structural payoff:
  "arranged so suppliers fund the inventory, leave a retailer's own cash barely
  touched." No growth-releases-cash claim survives.

## Cut

The revision added roughly 106 words (1980 to 2086), all in the AutoZone
consequence paragraph, and they earn their place: the stock/flow distinction is
the reasoning the correction required, not padding. The takeaway's negative-cycle
resolution is now honest and specific: "the company's suppliers carry the
inventory, and it ties up far less of its own cash than a grower like e.l.f.
does, even in a year when those accounts, on balance, still use some." That
closing qualifier does real work — it forecloses the exact misread editor/01
caught. The takeaway's general line ("a use of cash when the balances grow and a
source when payables outrun them") states the mechanism in general terms and
makes no AutoZone-specific source claim, so it stays consistent with the
company-specific correction. No new slop entered; no edge sentence regressed.

## Reader

The revision strengthens the lesson rather than patching it. Teaching AutoZone as
a stock inversion, and naming out loud that its FY2025 change in working capital
was still a use, models the stock/flow distinction a reader needs and removes the
one place the piece contradicted its own method: a reader who computes AutoZone's
FY2025 change now finds a use, exactly as the prose says. The e.l.f. side, the
in-place orientation edit, the reconciliation table, the annotated CCC equation,
and the two-company comparison table are all intact and were re-read; none
regressed. The "Why this matters" card's negative-working-capital phrasing is
aligned to the structural framing ("tie up little of their cash, or none at
all") and no longer implies the accounts hand cash back.
