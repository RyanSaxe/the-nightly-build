Teach so the reader can compute and interpret the change in working capital and
the cash conversion cycle on their own, grounded in the growth example and the
negative-working-capital example from the evidence. Build each idea before its
name. Rely on the earlier course lessons (income statement, balance sheet,
profit-vs-cash, free cash flow) rather than re-deriving them; cite them where
useful. Consider a table or a worked calculation where the numbers carry the
point (documented furniture only). The lesson template's two bookend cards may
address the reader; the body does not.

Framing precision (from the evidence report): the e.l.f. Beauty example shows
growth driving operating cash flow far BELOW net income ($71.2M OCF vs $127.7M
net income in FY2024), not literally negative operating cash flow. State the
mechanism as cash diverging from profit, not as "negative cash," so the lesson
does not overstate. Use the cash-flow-statement working-capital figures for the
cash story (e.l.f.'s balance-sheet deltas are inflated by the Naturium
acquisition), and treat e.l.f.'s ~132-day CCC as directional. AutoZone is the
clean negative-working-capital case (payables exceed inventory; vendor
financing; CCC ≈ −33 days). Use both exactly as the evidence records them.

Recent habits to break (detail in commission): do not open on a single company
and a price, and do not default to a company walkthrough — open on the concept
made concrete; a company illustrates, it does not anchor. Vary the headline from
the recent "Company + number" mold.
