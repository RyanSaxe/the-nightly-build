# Commission: investing/reverse-dcf

## Assignment

The next lesson in the cumulative Investing Course: reverse DCF, or reading the
market-implied expectations already priced into a stock. Instead of computing a
value and comparing it to price, invert the discounted-cash-flow model to ask
what growth and returns the current price must assume, then judge whether those
assumptions are achievable. This turns valuation from producing a number into
interrogating the market's number.

## Where it sits in the syllabus

The course has already taught present value, free cash flow, the discounted
cash-flow model and its terminal value, cost of capital, return on invested
capital, the value of growth, valuation multiples, and margin of safety
(conservative and optimistic values). This lesson uses those directly: it is the
inversion of the DCF the course already built, and it gives the reader a way to
locate a price between the conservative and optimistic scenarios margin of safety
introduced. Rely on the earlier lessons rather than re-deriving them; teach only
the inversion and how to reason about the expectations it exposes.
