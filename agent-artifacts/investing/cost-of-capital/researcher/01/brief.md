return; cost of debt (after-tax, interest deductibility); cost of equity as a

weights are formed. Establish the ROIC-vs-cost-of-capital value-creation test
from an authoritative source.

the owning primary (a filing or the company's own disclosure) and record it in

actually carry the concepts and any figures, not padding. Record honestly what
