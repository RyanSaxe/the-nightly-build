# Researcher brief: investing/cost-of-capital (01)

## Your job
Produce `evidence.md` here: the verified evidence record for a `lesson` on the
cost of capital and the value-creation rule (ROIC vs the hurdle). The writer
teaches only from what you verify.

## Begin with these inputs
- This brief; `../../commission.md`; `../../editorial-direction.md`.

## What to verify
1. **Concept definitions** from an authoritative corporate-finance source:
   cost of capital as opportunity cost / required return; WACC and its weighting;
   cost of debt (after-tax) and cost of equity (residual claim → higher required
   return); the rule that ROIC > cost of capital creates value, growth
   amplifies value above the line and destroys it below. Record exact wording and
   the located source (text section or Damodaran page/dataset). Classify these as
   authoritative secondary (they codify accepted finance), and say why.
2. **Risk-free rate (primary):** a current US Treasury yield (e.g. 10-year) from
   Treasury.gov or FRED, with exact date and value.
3. **Costco (primary):** the operating-income and invested-capital inputs that
   yield its ROIC (reuse the figures the prior `return-on-capital` lesson used;
   confirm them against Costco's 10-K). Enough to state Costco earns well above
   any reasonable hurdle. Record fiscal year and exact readings.
4. **American Electric Power (primary):** its return on invested capital near/
   below the allowed return, AND its regulators' **allowed return on equity**
   (the authorized ROE from a rate case / commission order, or AEP's disclosed
   authorized ROEs). This is the real-world hurdle. Record the exact allowed ROE
   figure(s), the setting body, and date. Verify against a primary regulatory or
   company source, not a summary.
5. **Equity risk premium / cost-of-equity magnitude:** one authoritative,
   dated estimate of the US equity risk premium (e.g. Damodaran's published
   figure) so a worked cost-of-equity number is grounded, not invented.

## Contradictions / Numbers
Record disagreements (e.g. debates over the "right" equity risk premium; whether
book vs market weights belong in WACC; criticisms of WACC as a single hurdle).
In Numbers, capture every figure the lesson will use: risk-free rate, ERP, the
worked cost-of-equity/WACC, Costco ROIC inputs, AEP ROIC and allowed ROE — each
with owning primary, exact reading, unit, and period.

## Constraints
Minimum 6 sources; classify each primary/secondary with a reason. Company/rate
figures verified against filings or regulatory primaries. Every URL resolves
(a paywalled filing: use the SEC EDGAR primary). Do not invent a number to make
a worked example clean; use the real one and round transparently.

Return `DONE researcher <path>`; `BLOCKED researcher <reason>` if the AEP allowed
ROE or a required number cannot be verified; `REQUEST orchestrator <need>` for a
