This is a teaching lesson for a smart reader new to the specific concept but
mathematically literate (the paper's reader has math/CS degrees and an ML
career). Study writers who teach a quantitative finance or economics concept
from first principles, building each idea before the sentence that needs it, and
who make an abstraction (opportunity cost, a required return) concrete with a
worked case. Give the writer craft for pacing a lesson that opens on "Why this
matters" and closes on "The takeaway," for defining terms in the sentence they
first appear, and for headings that trace the argument's steps.
