whether earning it created value; Costco clears its hurdle by 30+ points while
several AEP jurisdictions earn below the return their own regulators authorize";
tested the headline, dek, four subheads, and every worked number; broke: none.

  arithmetic range noted (S1 Table 1). Caveat honored.

  plainly and beta named as the omitted step. Correct.
- AEP blend: $47.3B debt / $31.1B equity = 60/40 (S6/S7/S8); $2.64 + $3.59 =

  points.
- AEP earned-vs-approved ROE table (as of 3/31/2025, S10): APCo/WPCo 9.75/8.2,
  Kentucky Power 9.75/4.4, PSO 9.50/8.0, SWEPCO 9.25-9.50/8.1, I&M 9.85-9.86/10.3,
