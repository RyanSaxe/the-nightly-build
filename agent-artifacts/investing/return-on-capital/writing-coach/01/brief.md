## What to study (at least three respected writers)
Study the best teachers of quantitative finance and the best plain-language
explainers of a numeric idea: e.g. Aswath Damodaran's teaching voice, the
patience of Chris Olah / Lilian Weng building a concept before it is spent, and a
strong finance explainer who works real numbers cleanly (a respected value-
investing writer or a Morgan Housel-grade clarity, without his aphorism habit).
Extract how they: introduce a formula only after the reader needs it; walk real
arithmetic without losing the point; teach a convention honestly as a choice; and
write bookends that set up and resolve a lesson without summarizing the body.
