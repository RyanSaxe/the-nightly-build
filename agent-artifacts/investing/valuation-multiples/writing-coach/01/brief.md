The genre is a teaching lesson for a reader who is quantitatively strong but new
to this subject: the declared reader holds degrees in math and computer science
and a machine-learning career, so the math is welcome and the finance is not
assumed. The article derives a justified multiple and uses it to explain why a
low multiple can be correct pricing. Study writers who teach a technical or
financial idea by building it from first principles, who introduce a formula and
then make it mean something with a real case, and whose patience never tips into
talking down. Investing writers who explain valuation to a smart outsider, and
first-principles technical teachers, both fit.

Prefer exemplars on other financial or quantitative concepts, not on multiples
themselves, so no framing or worked case arrives in the guide as material the
writer inherits.
