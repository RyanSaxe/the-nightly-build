# researcher brief: investing/valuation-multiples (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, series territory, declared reader
- ../../commission.md — the ideas taught and the worked contrast the lesson turns on

Output: evidence.md

Questions the record must answer, each traced to the source that owns it:

- Definitions of the common multiples the lesson will name (P/E, and at least one
  enterprise-value multiple such as EV/EBIT or EV/EBITDA, plus price-to-sales or
  price-to-book if used), from a recognized valuation authority. State exactly
  what each ratio's numerator and denominator are and what a multiple means as a
  price.
- The derivation that a multiple is a discounted-cash-flow value in disguise: the
  justified P/E or justified EV multiple expressed from growth, the payout or
  reinvestment it requires, and the cost of capital (the Gordon-growth form and
  the fundamentals-of-multiples relationship). Record the formula and the source
  that establishes it, with enough of the derivation that the writer can set the
  math honestly.
- Real, current figures for the two or three named companies the worked contrast
  will use (not Costco, not Apple): the current multiple, and the growth,
  margin, and return-on-capital context that explains why each deserves the
  multiple it trades at. Verify every figure against the filing or data source
  that owns it, with the period and denominator recorded. Pick companies whose
  contrast is real: a fast grower and a slow, stable business.
- The standard cautions on relative valuation from the same authorities: what
  comparability requires, and why a screen on low multiples alone misleads.

Source floor is 6; meet it with sources that carry the teaching and the numbers,
not filler. Classify each as primary or secondary and say why (a company filing
that owns a figure is primary for that figure; a textbook is primary for a
definition it establishes). Preserve any full series a chart might use, for
instance multiples against growth across a set of comparables. In Source assets,
name an exact visual only if a cited source contains one worth the space.
