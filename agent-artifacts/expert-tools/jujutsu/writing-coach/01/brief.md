# writing-coach brief: expert-tools/jujutsu (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, slop, headlines, press voice, article-template identity, series prompt
- ../../commission.md — the tool, the one workflow change to prove, the honest-cost requirement, the dek mold to break

Output: ./voice-guide.md

The genre is the expert tool writeup for a machine-learning engineer who lives
in Git: someone who wants the model explained from first principles and the
demonstration to earn its claim. Study the best developer-tool and
version-control writers, people the field rates for making a system's design
legible on a small example (technical bloggers who explain `git` or `jj`
internals well, not tutorial mills). Quote for voice and rhythm, not for the
`jj` findings; a passage that hands this article its demonstration or its verdict
leaks content, so choose passages that show how a good writer builds a concrete
example and states a cost plainly.
