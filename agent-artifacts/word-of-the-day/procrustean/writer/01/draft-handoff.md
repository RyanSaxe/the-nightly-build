# Draft handoff: word-of-the-day/procrustean (01)

## Original work

The article holds the myth's two-bed variant against the century-late 1822
figurative attestation to argue that *procrustean* names a standard that is
arbitrary and self-serving rather than merely strict, then tests that edge on two
legal uses that reject a one-size rule — a synthesis the evidence record holds
only as separate, unconnected facts (the dates, the variants, and the quotations
each sit apart in the record).

Where it is visible: the "adjective arrives twice" section splits the 1647
literal sense from the post-1820s figurative one and uses that gap; the closing
section converts that dating into the working distinction (*strict* vs
*procrustean*) and grounds it in Ker and Krotoszynski.
