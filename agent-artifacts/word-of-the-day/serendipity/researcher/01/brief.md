# Researcher brief: word-of-the-day/serendipity

Required by the commission: an authoritative dictionary for the card
definition, Horace Walpole's 28 January 1754 letter to Horace Mann as the
primary coinage record, Merton & Barber (or comparable scholarship) for
how the sense developed, and cited modern usage. Minimum 4 sources for the
`word` template; every URL must resolve and every claim traces to a
passage actually read.

All four sources below were opened and read directly. Notably, the primary
1754 letter was not reachable as a standalone transcription page (Strawberry
Hill House, the Paris Review, and Boston Magazine's retellings all returned
403 on fetch), so the letter is cited through Merton & Barber's chapter,
which reproduces it verbatim with a full archival citation to Walpole's
Correspondence, vol. 20 — read in full as a PDF (extracted with `pypdf`
after WebFetch returned the file as binary rather than summarized text).
