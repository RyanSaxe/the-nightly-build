# writing-coach brief: word-of-the-day/laconic (01)

Inputs:
- editorial-direction.md — house standard, press voice, Word of the Day series
  direction (the paper's smallest read)
- commission.md — the word, its documented origin, and the shape

Output: writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md

Study writers of the short etymological or lexical essay who make a word's story
entertaining and exact at once, and land its usefulness without padding. This is
550 to 800 words, so the register is compression itself. Choose exemplars whose
brief prose carries a real story and a clear present-day payoff, and avoid the
whimsical filler the form invites.
