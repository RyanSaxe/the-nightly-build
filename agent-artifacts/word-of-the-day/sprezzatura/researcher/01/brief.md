## What the article needs (find and verify)
1. **Definition, pronunciation, part of speech** from an authoritative
   dictionary (OED and/or Merriam-Webster). Record the exact headword entry,

2. **The coinage (primary).** Castiglione, *Il libro del cortegiano* (*The Book
   of the Courtier*, 1528), Book I: the exact passage where Count Lodovico da
   Canossa introduces "una certa sprezzatura." Locate it (book/section) in a
   public-domain edition or reputable translation. Record:
   - Castiglione's own definition of the term (concealing art/effort so that
     what is done appears effortless, "to conceal all art and make whatever is
     done or said appear to be without effort");
   - the contrast with **affettazione** (affectation) that motivates it;
   - the setting: the discussions staged at the court of Urbino (c. 1507),
     published 1528. Give Castiglione's exact identity (Italian courtier,
     diplomat, author; dates 1478-1529) and confirm.
   Verify any quotation against the actual translated text; note translator and
   locator. Distinguish the Italian original from the English translation used.
3. **Classical lineage (context).** The pre-existing rhetorical ideal that art
   should conceal itself, which Castiglione drew on: e.g. Cicero (studied
   plainness/hidden artifice) and/or Ovid, *Ars Amatoria* II, "ars adeo latet
   arte sua" ("so does art conceal art"). Verify the exact Latin and a located
   source before recording; if you cannot verify a specific quotation, say so
   and record only what you can confirm.
4. **Present usefulness (ground it).** At least one real, dated, cited example of
   modern usage in the "effortless mastery / concealed effort" sense (reputable

   general essay). Record the exact sentence and source so the writer can point
   to real use, not assert it.
5. **Meaning development.** Any authoritative note on how the sense traveled from
   courtly ideal to the general modern sense (dictionary history note or
   scholarship). Keep etymology (history) separate from present meaning.
