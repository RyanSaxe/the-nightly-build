# Writing-coach brief: word-of-the-day/sprezzatura (01)

## Your job
Produce `voice-guide.md` (in this folder) for a ~550-800 word Word of the Day
piece on **sprezzatura**: an entertaining, exact short read that gives a
definition, tells the documented origin, and lands the word's transferable use.

## Begin with these inputs
- This brief.
- `../../commission.md` (angle, reader, obligations).
- `../../editorial-direction.md` (house floor, headline standard, press voice,
  template identity, series prompt) for register only. Do not restate its rules.

## Domain and genre
The genre is the short lexical essay / etymological micro-feature: a precise,
story-driven word portrait for a general but demanding reader. Study at least
three exemplars the field rates for this kind of writing, e.g. the best
etymology and word-history writing (an authoritative dictionary's usage/history
notes; a respected word columnist or lexicographer; a stylist who writes short,
exact pieces about a single idea). Prefer primary pieces over commentary. Do not
study The Nightly Build's prior Word of the Day pieces for voice.

## What the voice guide must settle for this writer
- Register and pace for a 4-minute read: how to be entertaining without slop,
  precise without dryness.
- How to open a word portrait without the "X means Y" or "X coined it in YEAR"
  formulas the commission bans.
- How to weave a 500-year-old origin, a classical lineage, and a modern use into
  one continuous short read rather than a stack of facts.
- How to handle a foreign-language coinage and a translated quotation gracefully.
- Diction: concrete over abstract, one name kept for one thing.

## Constraints
- Specify how to write, never what to say. No coined catchphrases, no
  article-ready lines, no named-persona imitation.
- End the directive with `Recently used, do not reuse:` listing the habits the
  commission flags (definition-as-claim openers, "coined in YEAR for" openers,
  who/what/when deks, eponym-correction shapes, closing on a reading list).

## Output
Write only `voice-guide.md` here. Return `DONE writing-coach <path>`.
Use `nb` and web tools for focused work; request missing context from the
correspondent rather than touring the repo.
