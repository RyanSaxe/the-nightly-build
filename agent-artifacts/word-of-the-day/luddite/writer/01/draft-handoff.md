# Draft handoff: word-of-the-day/luddite (writer 01)

## Original work
This piece joins four sources that never meet — a dictionary's flattened
definition, a labour history of the wide frame and the shearing frame, the
documented state response, and Merchant's corrective — into one telling that
reconstructs the precise object of Luddite opposition (the *use* of machinery to
cut wages and deskill a trade) and sets it against the insult the word became, so
the reader leaves able to wield "Luddite" exactly.
