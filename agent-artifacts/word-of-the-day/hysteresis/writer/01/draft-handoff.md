# Draft handoff: word-of-the-day/hysteresis (writer/01)

## Original work

The piece fixes Ewing's 1881 coinage precisely to the twisted-wire
polarisation experiment he actually named it in, keeps that separate from
the magnetic B-H loop that made the word famous in his 1885 paper four years
later, and uses that gap to earn the one distinction the word preserves — a
system that fails to retrace its own path, not merely a delay — then shows
that distinction doing real work in Blanchard and Summers's 1986 account of
European unemployment, where the "normal" rate itself relocates rather than
the shock simply fading.
