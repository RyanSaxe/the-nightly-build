## Care taken with the contested material

- Literal electrical sense (verb entered English ~1801-1802, Etymonline/MW) is
  kept distinct from the figurative "rouse to action" sense (first recorded

- The Galvani-Volta dispute is told as two real rival explanations (Galvani:

  two dissimilar metals, the frog as detector). Volta's coinage of "galvanism"
