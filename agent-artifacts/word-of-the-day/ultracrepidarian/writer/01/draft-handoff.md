the sandal and wrong about the leg — to fix the exact line the word draws

## Accuracy traps honored

- Coinage vs. first record: the article says 1819 "fixes the word's first
  appearance in the printed record, not the moment anyone invented it," reads
  Hazlitt's *well called* as an already-circulating epithet, and flags the
  Charles Lamb attribution. Never "Hazlitt coined it."
- Latin form: the proverb note prints Pliny's *supra crepidam*; the pivot names
  the later *ultra crepidam* as the reshaped tag English took its prefix from.
- Dictionaries: cited Dictionary.com; did not cite Merriam-Webster (404s).
- Modern use: grounded in the confirmed *Salon* (2023) line as it appears in
  Dictionary.com's example panel; Baptist News (403s) not printed.
