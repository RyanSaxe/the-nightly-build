# Researcher brief — word-of-the-day/bowdlerize (invocation 01)

## Inputs (begin here)
- `editorial-direction.md`, `commission.md` (do not edit).
- This brief.

## Research questions
1. **Definition / pronunciation / part of speech.** The precise present-day
   dictionary sense of *bowdlerize*, its pronunciation, and part of speech, from an
   authoritative dictionary.
2. **Etymology and first attestation.** When and how the surname became a verb; the
   documented dating; any disagreement between authorities on the first-use year.
3. **The documented origin.** Thomas Bowdler (1754–1825) and his sister Henrietta
   "Harriet" Bowdler (1750–1830); *The Family Shakespeare* — the 1807 anonymous first
   edition (how many plays, how many volumes, where published) and the 1818 expanded
   edition (plays, volumes, publisher, attribution). The exact wording of the
   edition's stated aim and its title-page promise about what was omitted.
4. **The scholarly attribution to Harriet.** Evidence that Harriet did the original
   expurgation and why she was uncredited.
5. **Present usage, quoted and cited.** At least two real, cited examples grounding
   the present sense — ideally one use of the word itself in reputable prose and one
   documented modern act that fits the precise sense (sanitizing a text while still
   presenting it as the author's own work).
6. **The distinction.** Material to support bowdlerize vs. abridge / censor / redact.

## Source obligations
- min_sources 4. Cite authoritative dictionaries for definition/etymology and original

  etymology (history) distinct from the present sense (a separate claim).
- Every recorded URL must resolve (404/410 or DNS-dead is dead; a 403/paywall is
  gated, not dead). Classify each source primary/secondary with a reason.
