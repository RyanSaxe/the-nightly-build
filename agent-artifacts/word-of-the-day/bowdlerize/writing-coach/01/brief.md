## Task
Produce `writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md` for a single short article on the word
**bowdlerize**. Study how respected writers of short lexical / etymology essays
actually write, and turn that into a practical craft standard the writer can apply
sentence by sentence.

## Domain and genre
The word-history / etymology short essay: a single unusual word given a documented
origin story and a precise present sense. This paper's benchmark is the *sphexish*
piece — story plus present usefulness, not a bare etymology. Register is a serious
paper (Mitchell Hashimoto baseline: calm, precise, argued in plain sentences).

## What the guide must do
- Lead with a concise directive: register, reader relationship, and only the moves
  that will change sentences in THIS piece (a 550–800 word read that opens on a word
  card, tells the Bowdler history, and fixes one precise present sense).
- Study at least three exemplars by writers the field rates. Skip SEO/influencer
  content. Prefer the primary essay over commentary.
- For each exemplar record craft on the skill's axes plus the important move the axes
  miss, and keep one short calibration passage (texture only, never to echo into the
  article). Each exemplar entry MUST begin `Source: https://…` with a real, resolving
  URL to a real piece.
- End the directive with `Recently used, do not reuse:` carrying the commission's
  negative constraints (no colon-subtitle headline; no "X is not Y; it is Z" reflex;
  vary opener/closer from the recent word-of-the-day run; do not retell it as a
  disgrace-eponym the way *quisling* was told).

## Constraints
- Specify HOW to write, never WHAT to say. Do not restate the subject, the sources,
  or template rules. Do not coin catchphrases or reusable lines.
- Watch the hard, tight word band (550–800). The craft advice must serve compression.

## Output
- `writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md` only.
