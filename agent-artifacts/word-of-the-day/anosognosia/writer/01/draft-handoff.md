# Draft handoff: word-of-the-day/anosognosia (writer 01)

## Original work

The piece draws one sourced line from Babinski's 1914 hemiplegia sign to the
present psychiatric use and, along it, separates the word from its two
look-alikes on a cited property of each: denial is a psychological defense in
which the deficit is registered and minimized, and the Dunning-Kruger effect is
metacognitive miscalibration in cognitively healthy people, while anosognosia is
an organic unawareness in which the injury that removes a function also removes
the capacity to detect it is gone. That is the act the evidence does not perform
itself, and it is what the piece argues the word is worth keeping for.
