The genre is the short lexical essay: one word, its documented story, and the
distinction it preserves, told for a well-read technical reader who enjoys a
precise history. Study writers who make word-and-origin histories genuinely fun
to read without slackening the scholarship. Choose exemplars whose passages show
the writing rather than hand this article its subject; an exemplar about
"maverick" itself would leak findings, so quote for voice, not for content.
