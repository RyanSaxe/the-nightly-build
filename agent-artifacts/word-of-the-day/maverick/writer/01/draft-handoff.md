## Original work

The evidence record lays out the sources and the contradictions among them;
this draft's own work is sequencing that record into an argument the
folklore doesn't make on its own — opening on the one documented,
named-actor cause of the unbranded herd (Jack, left alone with the cattle),
then showing that the humane-motive legend traces to a single unsourced 2008
broadcast while three independent scholarly accounts that examine Maverick's
motives in depth never repeat it, then surfacing the second, deeper
disagreement the record itself flags almost in passing (the sources don't
even agree how Maverick got the cattle), and finally using the word's own
1924 first-person attestation to state precisely what "maverick" preserves
that "rebel" and "nonconformist" don't: not an act of leaving, but a
condition of never having been claimed.
