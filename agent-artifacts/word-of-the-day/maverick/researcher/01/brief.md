# researcher brief: word-of-the-day/maverick (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — citation standard, declared reader, series territory
- ../../commission.md — the word, the origin-vs-modern-sense tension, the required sense distinction

Output: ./evidence.md

Establish, from sources you open:
- The dictionary definition and recorded senses of "maverick," and the first
  attested figurative use, from an authoritative dictionary (OED preferred;
  Merriam-Webster acceptable as secondary). Record dates and the exact senses.
- The documented record on Samuel Augustus Maverick and his cattle: who he was,
  what the record actually says about why his cattle went unbranded, and how his
  name became a ranching term for an unbranded stray. Separate the documented
  record from the humane-motive folklore; find where the folk story is asserted
  and where it is contradicted or unsupported (a historical society, a scholarly
  etymology, or a biography carries more weight than a dictionary aside).
- The path from "unbranded stray anyone may claim" to "admirable independent,"
  with at least one dated, quoted real use for each attested sense, from a
  primary or period source, not a modern gloss.
- The precise sense the word preserves against near-synonyms (rebel,
  nonconformist, independent): what the unbranded-stray image adds.

Record contradictions between the folk origin and the documented record in the
Contradictions section; that tension is the article's spine, so it must be
sourced on both sides or the angle is not safe.
