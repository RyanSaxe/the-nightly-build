Output: researcher/01/evidence.md

Focus: read the primary evidence itself. Establish what the systematic-review
evidence shows on fluoride toothpaste for caries prevention in young children:
the Cochrane review(s) of fluoride toothpaste (effect size versus placebo, the
concentration-response finding, and what the trials do and do not establish below
age one), and the fluoride-varnish evidence. Establish the basis and exact wording
of current guidance for the relevant age: the "smear/rice-grain of fluoride
toothpaste from eruption of the first tooth" recommendation and the fluoride
supplementation schedule that depends on drinking-water fluoride level, from the
AAP, the ADA, and the US Preventive Services Task Force recommendation on caries
prevention in children. Establish the fluorosis-risk evidence tied to toothpaste
amount and swallowing in infants and toddlers, with figures. Record where each
conclusion is bounded by the age range actually tested, study design, or water
status. Note, briefly and only as context, the current US water-fluoridation
policy dispute, without letting it become the subject. Verify every figure against
its owning primary; record denominators and periods. Search for and record
evidence that complicates the guidance (for example weak or absent trial data in
the youngest infants).
