# Draft handoff: parenting-research/teething (01)

## Original work

This piece sorts every symptom Macknin's cohort tracked into what tooth
eruption actually moves versus what merely coincides with it, grounds the
coincidence in the cohort's own base rate (475 eruptions across 19,422
child-days, so a tooth is almost always "in window"), and then converts the
FDA's benzocaine and belladonna case record into one calibrated household rule
for a six-month-old — cold and pressure for the tooth, and a named red-flag
list that marks the signs which mean "not teething, call the pediatrician." No
single source performs that fusion of attribution, base rate, and drug-safety
record into a decision.

## Evidence-flag compliance

- **Fever** stated as the defensible claim: eruption is tied to at most a small
  temperature rise, not a true or high fever, and a real fever is not teething.
  The contrary evidence is steelmanned and weighed, not hidden — Nemezio's
  rectal-subgroup OR 2.82 (against the null overall OR 1.32) and Ramos-Jorge's
  measured day-of-eruption rise (P=.004, max 36.8 C, within normal range), plus
  the note that Ramos-Jorge also ties diarrhea/rash to eruption against the
  larger Macknin cohort and the AAP.
- **Belladonna** rests only on the two FDA letters and the FDA-posted recall
  announcement actually opened (Class I requested recall, "serious health
  hazard," "no known safe dose or toxic dose of belladonna in children"). The
  non-resolving FDA press-announcement URL is not cited, and the ~99 FAERS /
  infant-death / seizure counts are omitted (no openable primary or secondary
  in the evidence record carries them).
- **Acetaminophen** is framed as weight-based and clinician/label-directed, kept
  occasional; no numeric dose is printed (the AAP dosing table was not opened).
- **Benzocaine** is used firmly and carries the piece's one hard safety
  imperative: 119 methemoglobinemia cases, four deaths including a teething
  infant, no demonstrated benefit (FDA, May 23 2018).

## Recent habits broken

Opens on the coincidence mechanism, not a trial's bottom line. Dek avoids the
hedged-contrast / "does one thing not the other" / comma-triad molds. No
"where AAP and ESPGHAN part ways" (nb-position) section and no "back to the
[object]" closer.

## Open questions

- **Evidence (belladonna magnitude):** the piece deliberately carries no body
  count for belladonna. If the editor judges the argument needs one, it requires
  a new researcher artifact sourcing the counts to an openable primary or an
  attributed secondary; I did not expand the claim set to fill the gap.
- **Voice:** none material. The calibration budget is spent on a single
  benzocaine imperative, with belladonna reported firmly through the FDA record
  and the null/effect-size findings stated flat.
