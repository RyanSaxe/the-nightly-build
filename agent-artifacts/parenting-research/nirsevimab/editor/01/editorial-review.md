# Editorial review — parenting-research/nirsevimab (editor, invocation 01)

Production note: run in single context (no child-agent isolation available); the

## Skeptic: thesis "For a healthy term infant, nirsevimab's large relative-risk reduction is a small absolute one; it is one of two either/or routes; the concrete timing is a pediatrician's call."; tested 5 claims; broke: none.
- **MELODY** (healthy term). MA RSV LRTI 25/496 (5.0%) vs 12/994 (1.2%), RRR
  74.5%. Recomputed ARR 3.8 pp; NNT 100/3.8 = 26. Matches article and the
  registered results (NCT03979313). Hospitalization gap ~1 pp, NNT ~100, and the
  article correctly reports MELODY's hospitalization endpoint as **not
  significant** (p=0.07) and attributes the hospitalization evidence to HARMONIE
  and surveillance. This is the claim most popular coverage gets wrong; it is
  right here.
- **Griffin** (preterm). 9.5% vs 2.6%, 70.1%; ARR 6.9 pp, NNT 14.5 → 15.
  Verified. The preterm/term baseline contrast is the article's load-bearing
  move and it holds.
- **HARMONIE.** 64/4019 (1.6%) vs 11/4038 (0.3%), ~84%. The article writes "near
  84%" (registry 84.0%); the NEJM primary paper reports 83.2% from a slightly
  different analysis set. The hedge is accurate and the discrepancy is
  immaterial. Accepted as a knowing choice, not an error.
- **Real-world.** 90% (75–96), median 45 days, with the CDC's own waning caveat —
  reported faithfully. Population reductions 43% / 28% (0–7 mo) verified.
- **Either/or.** The CDC quote ("...but both are not needed for most infants") is
  reproduced accurately; AAP concurrence verified against HealthyChildren.
- **data-nb-kind audit.** All ten sources are labeled primary and each genuinely
  owns its number: trial registries for the trial counts, CDC/ACIP MMWRs for the
  recommendations and effectiveness, CDC for burden/supply, AAP for its own
  parent guidance. No secondary is masquerading as primary; no independent source
  is missing behind a wrong label.
- **Headline.** "four in a hundred" = the MELODY MA-RSV ARR (3.8 ≈ 4/100);
  defended in the body. "three-quarters" = 74.5%. Both stand.
