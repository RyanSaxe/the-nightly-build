# Editor review-brief — parenting-research/nirsevimab (invocation 01)

## Inputs (begin here)
- `editorial-direction.md`; the exact writer `brief.md` (writer/01) for
  prompt-leakage detection; `writing-coach/01/voice-guide.md`;
  `researcher/01/evidence.md`; `writer/01/draft-handoff.md`; the article HTML;
  `.nb-context/` template contract.

## Reads to perform (in order)
1. **Skeptic.** State the thesis and the 2–4 claims it rests on. Try to break
   each. Reopen the evidence record and, where needed, the ClinicalTrials.gov /
   MMWR primaries. Recompute every ARR and NNT from the counts. Confirm MELODY's
   hospitalization endpoint is reported as non-significant. Audit every
   `data-nb-kind` (all claim "primary"): is each source genuinely the owner of
   its number? Verify the either/or maternal-vaccine framing against the CDC
   wording. Check the headline's "four in a hundred" against the MELODY ARR.
2. **Cut.** Delete sentences carrying no fact/claim/reasoning. Hunt prompt
   leakage against the writer brief (planning labels, "this desk fulfilled its
   assignment," selection rules). Check for banned tells, em-dash reflex, dek
   molds, colon-subtitle/Betteridge headline. Assess each furniture piece for a
   clear purpose (stat strip, trials table, chart, holds-up grid + one Verdict
   note, two plain notes): remove any that reads as decoration.
3. **Reader.** As the house reader: what does this give beyond the sources?
   Compare with the writer's original-work sentence. Judge voice against the
   coach exemplars vs a median summary. Retest the headline as the largest claim.

## Standards to apply
- House floor: sourced claims, teach-don't-summarize, concrete numbers with a
  comparison, no fluff/slop/run-ons, plainest punctuation.
- Health specifics (commission): absolute vs relative always paired; NNT shown;
  the either/or choice explicit; the desk does not replace individual care; any
  risk-factor infant is a clinician's call.
- Inspect the chart: compare chart-1.py data against the evidence record and
  read the image for honest axes/scale/legend.

## Routing
- Cuts and small prose fixes: make directly. Past a word/clause of new writing →
  writer. Evidence gaps → researcher. Record the decision line.
- Proof to confirm after any change: the writer's `nb check` command to BLOCK 0.
