## Questions the evidence must answer (verify every number against its owner)

   hospitalization primary endpoint: counts, percents, efficacy + CI; very
   severe RSV LRTI.
4. **Absolute risk reduction and NNT** for each headline relative figure,
   computed from the counts, separately for healthy-term vs preterm groups.
5. **Baseline season risk** for a healthy term infant (trial control arms; CDC

6. **Guidance:** ACIP nirsevimab recommendation (who, dose by weight); ACIP
   maternal RSVpreF/Abrysvo recommendation and the "one or the other, not both"
   framing; AAP position.
7. **Maternal vaccine (Abrysvo) efficacy** against severe MA RSV LRTI (MATISSE),
   and the preterm-birth safety signal.
8. **Real-world effectiveness** (2023–24 MMWR; 2024–25 population impact) and how
   it compares to RCT conditions.
9. **Limits:** mortality not demonstrable in high-income settings; long-term
   outcomes; supply/access/cost history.
