This is an evidence review a household can act on, written for a well-read reader
who is a new parent, not a clinician. The register is calm and exact: it reports
what the studies support, how strongly, and what they cannot settle, and it does
not soothe or alarm. A safety threshold has to read as a clear line without
tipping into fear. Study writers who explain medical evidence honestly to a lay
reader, holding uncertainty and reassurance in the same paragraph. Choose
passages that show how to state a limit or a risk plainly; leave out any that
lean on a clinician's authority the piece cannot borrow.
