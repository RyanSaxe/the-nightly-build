# Researcher brief: parenting-research/infant-iron (01)

## Your job
Produce `evidence.md` here: the verified evidence record for an `article` on
infant iron in the second half of the first year. The writer weighs only what you
verify; the editor will reopen the trials.

## Begin with these inputs
- This brief; `../../commission.md`; `../../editorial-direction.md`.

## What to read and verify (open the studies, not summaries)
1. **Physiology / why six months:** an authoritative source on fetal iron stores
   depleting ~4-6 months, breast milk's low iron, and formula fortification (AAP
   clinical report on iron; WHO; a review). Record exact ages and mechanisms.
2. **Delayed cord clamping:** a primary trial/review on its effect on infant iron
   status (e.g. Andersson et al.). Record the effect on ferritin/anemia and age.
3. **Association evidence (development):** Lozoff et al. (iron deficiency in
   infancy and long-term neurodevelopment). Record what it shows, the design
   (observational/longitudinal), and the causation caveat.
4. **Trial evidence (the tension):** Cochrane review(s) of iron supplementation
   in infants; a landmark RCT (e.g. the Chilean trial of iron supplementation and
   long-term outcomes) — record population, dose, design, effect sizes with CIs,
   AND the key finding that supplementing iron-replete/non-anemic infants shows
   no clear developmental benefit and possible harm. This contradiction is the
   article's spine; get it exactly right.
5. **Guidance (primary for each body's recommendation):** AAP (complementary
   iron-rich foods from ~6 months; screening/supplementation history), WHO,
   CDC, USDA Dietary Guidelines (0-24 months), USPSTF evidence report/
   recommendation on screening for iron-deficiency anemia in young children.
   Record each body's exact recommendation and where they diverge.
6. **Prevalence:** a current, dated US figure for iron deficiency / IDA in infants
   or toddlers (CDC/NHANES) with denominator and age band.

## Contradictions / Numbers
The Contradictions section is central here: association vs RCT evidence;
screening recommendations that disagree; the harm-in-replete-infants signal.
Record them fully. In Numbers, capture every figure the writer may cite
(prevalence %, ages, doses in mg/kg/day, RR/OR/mean differences with CIs) with
owning primary, unit, denominator, and period.

## Constraints
Minimum 8 sources; classify each primary/secondary with a reason (a trial/review
owns its result; a guideline is primary for that body's recommendation; news is
secondary). Verify every number against the owning study. Every URL resolves;
prefer PubMed/PMC or the journal primary; a paywall is gated, find the free
primary. Do not overstate causation the evidence does not support.
