Task: read the evidence on infant iron in the 6-12 month window for a healthy
term infant. Gather and read the passages in:

- AAP guidance on iron requirements/supplementation in infants (and the
  screening recommendation), and WHO guidance on infant iron / complementary
  feeding.
- Primary data on iron content of human milk and infant iron requirements, and
  on the depletion of fetal iron stores around six months.
- A systematic review (Cochrane or equivalent) of iron supplementation in
  infancy and its outcomes.
- The landmark evidence linking infant iron status to neurodevelopment: the
  key cohort work (e.g. Lozoff and colleagues) and randomized trials of iron
  supplementation, including where trials did *not* find long-term benefit.
- Prevalence data for iron deficiency / iron-deficiency anemia in infants.

For each source, record kind (primary/secondary and why), what it establishes,
precise paraphrase, and locators. In Numbers, record effect sizes with their
denominators and periods, and distinguish anemia from deficiency without anemia.
Critically: surface the contradiction between the consistent observational
association (iron-deficiency anemia ↔ worse development) and the mixed RCT
evidence on whether supplementation changes long-term outcomes — this is the
article's central honesty. Confirm every URL resolves. Meet the eight-source
floor with sources that ground or complicate the claim, not padding.
