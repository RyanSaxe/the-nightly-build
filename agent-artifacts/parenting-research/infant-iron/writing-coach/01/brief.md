This is an evidence-based parenting-research piece for a scientifically literate
parent: it must convey uncertainty precisely without either alarmism or false
reassurance. Study writers who communicate medical/epidemiological evidence to a
smart general reader — distinguishing association from causation, relative from
absolute risk, and RCT from cohort — with calm authority. Give the writer craft
for stating what is established vs uncertain, for grounding numbers in
comparisons a reader holds, and for the clinician-boundary passages that stay
useful rather than boilerplate. Headings should trace the evidence, not fill
scaffolding slots.
