## Contradictions

- s8 body-content consequence (must reach the writer). researcher/01 attached to
  s8 the claim that "more hours in early care were associated with more
  risk-taking and impulsivity at 15." That behavior finding is NOT what the
  resolving 2016 paper reports. At end of high school this paper found no
  significant association between hours/quantity of early care and risk-taking,
  impulsivity, or externalizing; its one significant quantity finding runs the
  other way on a different outcome ("Fewer hours in child care was related to
  admission to more selective colleges (d = -.10, p < .05)"). When s8 is repointed
  to the 2016 EOHS paper, the writer must not print the "more risk-taking and
  impulsivity" hours-effect against this source. The quality magnitudes
  (d = .08 to .16) and the correlational caveat do transfer intact; the
  quantity-of-care behavior claim does not, and should be dropped or re-sourced to
  the age-15 paper if the article intends to keep it.
- The body's "At age 15 both threads persisted" is inconsistent with the
  corrected s8 (end of high school, mean age 18.3). The writer must change the age
  to end of high school (~age 18) to match the source the link opens.
