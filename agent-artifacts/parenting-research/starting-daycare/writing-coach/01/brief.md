# writing-coach brief: parenting-research/starting-daycare (01)

Inputs:
- ../../editorial-direction.md — house standard, press voice, the article template
  identity, and the series prompt
- ../../commission.md — the decision, the evidence tiers, and the desk close

Output: voice-guide.md

The genre is an evidence-appraisal article for a specific reader: a parent who is
also quantitatively literate and wants the strength of the evidence, not
reassurance. The piece weighs firm and contested findings, marks what study
design can and cannot establish, and closes on what one family might change and
where a clinician takes over. Study writers who read medical and social-science
evidence honestly for a general reader: science and health writers who state an
effect size and its uncertainty in the same breath, who steelman a contested
claim before weighing it, and who never let a caveat turn into hedging mush.

Prefer exemplars on other health or child-development questions, not on daycare
itself, so no finding or framing arrives in the guide as material the writer
inherits. The reader is a parent making a real decision; the register is calm and
exact, never anxious and never soothing.
