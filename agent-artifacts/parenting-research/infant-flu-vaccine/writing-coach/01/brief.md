Genre: an evidence-appraisal article for parents, written for a technically
literate reader who wants effect sizes, uncertainty, and what a study design can
and cannot establish — not reassurance and not advocacy. The register is calm and
exact, careful to separate reported fact, estimate, and synthesis, and honest
about what is unknown. Study writers who explain medical evidence to a lay-expert
audience with that discipline. Avoid exemplars written to persuade about
vaccination in either direction; quote for the appraising voice, not for a stance.
