Read the evidence itself. Establish from owning primaries: the current CDC/ACIP
influenza recommendation for the 2026-27 season (ages eligible from six months;
the two-dose rule and >=4-week interval for first-time vaccinees under nine
years), the Cochrane review of influenza vaccine in children (with its efficacy
and effectiveness estimates and its GRADE/quality caveats, distinguishing
laboratory-confirmed influenza from influenza-like illness), the pivotal
pediatric efficacy trials underpinning under-2 estimates, the evidence on
maternal antenatal influenza vaccination protecting infants under six months
(the randomized trials), and vaccine-safety evidence at 6-23 months (including
the febrile-seizure signal with concomitant PCV13/DTaP and current egg-allergy
guidance). Verify every number against its owner: efficacy/effectiveness ranges
and their confidence intervals, the age thresholds, the dose interval, absolute
versus relative measures. Search hard for what bounds or contradicts the angle:
season-to-season match dependence, weaker and less precise estimates in the
youngest children, null or heterogeneous trial results. Record contradictions in
full. For Source assets, note any figure or forest plot that could carry an
effect-size point better than prose. Confirm every URL resolves to the source's
own page.
