Use only the evidence record's claims and sources. The honest through-line the
evidence supports: a six-month-old's flu protection is modest and season-dependent
when direct (inactivated vaccine only at this age; RR ~0.36 against
laboratory-confirmed influenza, carried mostly by children over two; the pivotal
6-24-month trial swung 66% to -7% across two seasons), and strongest but fading
when borrowed from maternal antenatal vaccination (pooled ~35%, CI 19-47, waning to
a CI-crossing ~19% by 4-6 months) — the two mechanisms do not overlap cleanly in
time. Keep absolute and relative measures distinct. Present cocooning as an ACIP
recommendation without attaching an efficacy number (no trial quantifies the infant
benefit). Handle the febrile-seizure signal precisely (no signal for inactivated
vaccine alone; ~tripled short-term rate with concomitant PCV13/DTaP; small absolute
excess). If the 2026-27 ACIP MMWR is not citable as its own page, carry the season
rule via the standing ACIP documents and the AAP 2026-27 page, as the evidence
records. End on what the evidence might change at home and where it is a matter for
the pediatrician; do not stand in for medical care. Do NOT open on the child's
six-month milestone (the last parenting piece did, near-verbatim). Fill nb-meta
tags and harness/model (writer model: Claude Opus).
