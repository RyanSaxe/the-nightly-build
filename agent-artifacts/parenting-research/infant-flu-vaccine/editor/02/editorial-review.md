# Editorial review: parenting-research/infant-flu-vaccine (editor/02)

## Skeptic

This is a fresh read after the routed correction. Editor/01 verified every
figure, interval, denominator, href, chart, and table and approved everything
except one broken central claim: the article explained the Hoberman
66%-to-(-7%) two-season efficacy swing as a change in antigenic match between
the circulating strain and the vaccine. The owning primary says the opposite.
My read confirms that correction is now fully carried and rests it on the
primary rather than the superseded record.

The thesis is unchanged and holds: for a healthy six-month-old the actionable
flu protection is thin and season-dependent from the child's own inactivated
shot, and the better-measured protection borrowed from maternal pregnancy
vaccination has faded by the age the child becomes eligible, with the two
timelines never overlapping. The load-bearing claims (LAIV's RR 0.22 out of
scope below age two; the applicable inactivated RR 0.36 resting mostly on older
children; the one in-band trial's swing; maternal 35% waning to a
zero-crossing 19% by four-to-six months) are the ones editor/01 recomputed and
cleared, and the redraft did not disturb them.

I focused on the correction and its three-place ripple, recomputed the new
figures against researcher/02's evidence, and opened the two hrefs the redraft
now leans on.

- **Hoberman (s3), href as printed** (jamanetwork.com/.../197348) resolves to
  the paper's own page and confirms, in the paper's words, "Circulating
  influenza strains were well matched with vaccine strains in the 2 respiratory
  seasons" — both seasons, including 2000-01. It confirms 66% (CI 34-82) then
  -7% (CI -247 to 67), the placebo attack rate falling 22/138 = 15.9% to
  4/123 = 3.3%, and the second-season null attributed to influenza occurring
  infrequently with few cases, not to mismatch. 22/138 recomputes to 15.9% and
  4/123 to 3.3%; the four second-season placebo cases are what the wide,
  zero-crossing CI rests on.
- **CDC effectiveness (s12), href as printed** (cdc.gov/flu-vaccines-work/
  effectiveness/index.html) resolves to CDC's own page and owns the general
  statement the closer cites: effectiveness "may vary based on how similar or
  different the vaccine virus is to circulating influenza viruses," lower when
  vaccine viruses are less similar to the circulating ones. This is a general
  US public-health statement, correctly detached from the Hoberman example.

The correction holds in all three places. Section 2 now reads "In both winters

The added s12 entry carries `data-nb-kind="primary"`, which matches the

No claim broke against the primary. No figure, name, date, or citation was

## Cut

I ran the slop pass over the three rewritten passages specifically and re-walked
the edges. The rewritten prose is clean: each new sentence carries a fact or a
reasoning step and fails the placeholder test in the right direction (the nouns
— four cases, 15.9 to 3.3 percent, the -247-to-67 interval — are what carry it).
"Four cases cannot fix a number" reads punchy but depends entirely on the
four-case count and delivers the CI it names, so it survives. The verdict's
recast sentence and the closer's "Both are known only in hindsight" both state
earned conclusions, not empty ones.

One edge sentence failed the delete test. The section-2 closer ended "...when a
season's influenza is scarce, where the trials are themselves few and small."
The trailing "where the trials are themselves few and small" is a loose clause
with a fuzzy referent, and the fact it carries (in-band trials are few) is
already established in the orientation ("Cochrane found very few randomized
trials below the age of two"). Removing it loses no fact and leaves a clean,
earned section close resting on the paragraph's own incidence argument. Cut.

Formula check against the recent-pattern notes: the piece still does not open on
the six-month-milestone construction, and the dek makes a licensing/measurement
claim rather than the flagged large-relative/small-absolute or "settles X and
little else" mold. The redraft touched neither. Headings are unchanged and vary
in construction. The verdict block earns its place as deliberate emphasis and
was already cleared; only its final sentence changed, and it now coheres with
the reframed section.

## Reader

Read straight through as the desk's parent, the piece still gives what the
sources alone do not: the two protective timelines set against each other so the
reader sees the child's own shot comes due exactly as the borrowed maternal
protection reaches a CI that no longer excludes zero, with cocooning
unquantified in the gap. That assembly is the article's own work and matches the
draft-handoff's original-work sentence. The correction strengthens rather than
weakens it: the swing now honestly shows how imprecisely direct efficacy is
pinned down in this age band, which is the reason the family cannot count on the
child's own shot for a given season — the same epistemic limit the closer lands
on. The prose sits closer to the voice-guide exemplars than to a median summary;
it states what each figure measured before giving the number, in the register
the guide describes. The headline reads as the largest claim and holds.
