# unbiased

An unbiased article presents the strongest evidence-backed case for each of
two genuinely contested answers to one question. The paper does not choose
between them.

Define the positions from the actual disagreement, not from default political
teams. Name them in terms their serious holders would recognize. Apply the same
standards of relevance, evidence, and scrutiny to both.

Fairness does not require false symmetry. Each position receives the strongest
support the record allows, and neither receives support the record does not.
Use primary statements from people or institutions that genuinely hold the
position when they are available.

The question and evidence determine the structure within each position. The
template requires no repeated sequence of claims or labels and no house
conclusion. Head every section for this particular disagreement.
