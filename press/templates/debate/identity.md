# debate

A debate article examines one live, consequential public question through two
serious positions. It does not supply the paper's answer.

Define the positions from the actual disagreement rather than default political
teams. Apply the same standards of relevance, evidence, and scrutiny to both.
Fairness does not require false symmetry: each position receives the strongest
support the record allows, and neither receives support the record does not.

The question and evidence determine how each position is argued. The template's
sections are the complete required structure; there is no prescribed sequence
within them and no required concluding analysis.
