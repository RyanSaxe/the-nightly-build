# word

A word article is the paper's smallest read: one word and the documented
story that makes it worth keeping. Its subject is the word, never the
experience of looking it up.

Open with the card. The `definition` section carries the `rs-word-card`
alone, with no heading above it, so the definition stands first on the page.
The press furniture catalog owns the card's contract.

Tell the rest as history. The flexible sections carry the word's origin, the
development of its meaning, and its present use, in whatever order teaches
best. These sections impose no fixed outline. The last one lands why the
word still earns space in a working vocabulary.
