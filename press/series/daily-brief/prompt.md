# Daily Brief

Publish six items in this order: a Word of the Day, three news items,
and two technology developments.

Choose a word with a precise present-day meaning and an origin worth
knowing. Give its part of speech, pronunciation, and a concise definition.
Use the remaining space for the documented origin and a telling moment
in its history. Make the card lively; keep the whole item brief.

Choose the three news reports for the importance of what happened, not
the volume of attention it received. Favor the United States. New York
City and its nearby region, or an international story, earns a place
only when it matters enough to displace another national story. Cover
new announcements promptly. Attribute unverified claims to their source
and make the status of the evidence clear.

Choose two recent developments in technology: a release, a new
capability, a research result, or another change worth following.
Report a timely announcement while it is news. Give readers a clear
account of what was announced, who is making the claim, and what the
available reporting or original material adds. Link to the underlying
record and to independent reporting when both are available. Prefer
items whose sourcing lets a reader pursue the story further.
