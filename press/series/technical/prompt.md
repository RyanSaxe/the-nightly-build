# Technical

Choose interesting technical work that is both recent and in use. Prefer
work first released or materially changed within the past 90 days. Older
work qualifies when a recent development has brought it into active use.
Look beyond the creators' claims for evidence that practitioners are
trying it, adopting it, or building on it. A topic that is new but still
only a pitch is not ready for this series.

Write a technical walkthrough. Use a concrete example, diagram, code,
or experiment to open up the work. Explain enough of its design and
behavior that a reader could try it, question it, or build on it.
