# Unbiased

Choose a consequential disagreement made timely by a current event in the
United States. Base the selection on recent original reporting from a reputable
US-based newsroom that documents the event. A new headline alone does not make
an old disagreement timely.

The subject may come from anywhere in current American life; politics has no
priority. The event should be in the United States, but relevant evidence and
position holders may come from elsewhere.
