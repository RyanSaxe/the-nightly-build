Cutting Lp(a) as far as one genetic study required did not reduce cardiovascular events

Novartis's pelacarsen cut Lp(a) by an estimated 66 to 84 mg/dL, and the Lp(a)HORIZON trial ruled out the benefit genetic studies tie to a fall that size.

5 min read2026-11-08

A 171 nmol/L fall and a hazard ratio of 1.02

Pelacarsen, Novartis's antisense drug for lipoprotein(a), lowered Lp(a) by an estimated 66 to 84 mg/dL. Two genetic studies put the fall needed for a meaningful coronary benefit at 65.7 and 101.5 mg/dL. Lp(a)HORIZON, presented on 7 November 2026, found no change in major cardiovascular events over a median 4.7 years.12

Those studies use Mendelian randomization, which treats inherited gene variants as a natural randomized experiment. Burgess and colleagues found that a 101.5 mg/dL lower Lp(a) matched the coronary effect of a 38.67 mg/dL (1 mmol/L) lower LDL cholesterol.3 Lamina and Kronenberg found 65.7 mg/dL.4 The trial's primary endpoint was the first cardiovascular death, heart attack, stroke or urgent coronary revascularization. A hazard ratio of 1.00 means equal event rates. Trial figures come from Cleveland Clinic's summary of the published paper.2

Three conversions put the fall at 66 to 84 mg/dL

Mass in mg/dL and particle count in nmol/L do not convert by a fixed factor. The trial's investigators found that the fixed factor of 2.5 matched measured nmol/L worse than a Copenhagen General Population Study formula.5 The pelacarsen arm's baseline medians come in both units, 108.4 mg/dL and 235.6 nmol/L, but later values only in nmol/L.2 Table 1 derives the fall three ways. One applies a reported percentage to the mg/dL baseline. Two scale a nmol/L fall by the ratio of those medians, 0.460 mg/dL per nmol/L, which is not a patient-level conversion.

The range reaches Lamina and Kronenberg's 65.7 mg/dL and lies inside their interval. It falls 17 to 35 percent short of Burgess's 101.5, and two of its three values sit inside his interval.

The trial's interval excludes the benefit the genetic estimates predict

Burgess found coronary risk fell in proportion to the absolute change in Lp(a): 5.8% lower per 10 mg/dL, an odds ratio of 0.942.3 Over 66 to 84 mg/dL that compounds to a lifelong odds ratio of 0.67 to 0.60. Both papers equate their required fall with the clinical benefit of cutting LDL cholesterol by 1 mmol/L,34 which in 26 statin trials over a median of about five years was a rate ratio of 0.78 for major vascular events.7 Burgess expects benefit to scale with the absolute fall, so 66 to 84 mg/dL, 65 to 83 percent of his 101.5, predicts ratios of 0.85 to 0.81 (derived here). Lamina and Kronenberg's requirement, which the fall met, corresponds to 0.78.

The primary interval excludes both bands and any reduction beyond about 10%. The trial was set to stop at 993 first events, and the design abstract does not state the effect it was powered to detect.9

No gradient appears by size of fall: landmark hazard ratios were 1.09 at or above the median fall of 170 nmol/L and 1.10 at or below the median achieved level of 69.5 nmol/L.8 Because 35.1% of pelacarsen and 18.3% of placebo patients stopped early, an on-treatment analysis counted events only on the drug and for 112 days after, and gave 0.98.82 Heart attack alone, the nearest match to the genetics' coronary outcome, had 0.99.10

Nissen's lifelong explanation needs Lp(a) to act more slowly than LDL

The statin trials behind the 0.78 also ran about five years, close to HORIZON's 4.7. No source measures how fast the benefit of lowering Lp(a) arrives. Nissen also cited intensive background therapy and a smaller fall than the small interfering RNA drugs now in trials.2

Every pending trial enters patients above HORIZON's line of about 148 nmol/L, whose median patient had 236.1 No document opened gives HORIZON's share above 175 or 200 nmol/L. Phase 2 placebo-adjusted falls were 94% (lepodisiran, 400 mg) and 97% (olpasiran, 75 mg) from medians of 254 and 260 nmol/L,1617 against pelacarsen's 77.7% at 12 months, though assays, populations and time points differ. With follow-up near HORIZON's 4.7 years, a miss at that depth would point to duration or the genetics, and a hit to the size of the fall.
