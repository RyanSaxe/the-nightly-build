Word of the Day
dord noun /dôrd/

A ghost word: a dictionary entry for a noun nobody ever used, made when an abbreviation for "density" was misread.12

Austin M. Patterson, the chemistry editor of Webster's New International Dictionary, Second Edition (1934), wrote a slip to record "D" as an abbreviation for "density." A later handler read the "or" between its spaced terms as part of one word, underlined the four letters as a headword and made it a lowercase noun, with no etymology and no usage examples. An editor's 1939 slip marked "imperative! urgent!" asked for a correction in the next printing, which was not made until 1947. Merriam-Webster calls dord its only ghost word and says no one was fired.1

News

UN climate talks run two days over, then fail

COP31 closed in Antalya, Türkiye, on Sunday 22 November, two days late, when its Turkish co-president, Murat Kurum, told delegates, "I understand that there is no agreement on this text. For that reason, these decisions have not been adopted."3 The Australian and Turkish co-presidents' draft never named fossil fuels, so the EU, through Irish climate minister Darragh O'Brien, would not endorse it, while Russia and Saudi Arabia supported leaving them out. ABC counts it the first failure to adopt an agreed deal at UN climate talks since Copenhagen in 2009.4

News

Suspected sea-drone strike capsizes a Russian corvette in Benghazi

At dawn on Sunday 22 November a suspected sea drone hit a ship moored in Benghazi's civil port. The satellite-imagery company Vantor identifies it as the Russian Navy corvette Merkury, and Sunday's images show it mostly capsized with only its bow above water. Nobody has claimed the strike, though AP, citing unnamed sources, says Ukrainian sea drones were launched from a base run by Ukrainian forces in western Libya.5 Eastern Libya's prime minister, Osama Hammad, has asked the UN General Assembly and Security Council for an international investigation.5

News

Virginia reports its first measles death of 2026

The Virginia Department of Health said on 20 November that a child aged 0 to 4 who tested positive for measles has died, and gave no further detail, citing the family.6 The CDC's count through 19 November is 5,838 confirmed US cases and six deaths in 2026,7 two fewer deaths than Pennsylvania alone has reported this year.8

Technology

Signal blocks screenshots to keep OS-level AI assistants out of chats

Signal's Meredith Whittaker wrote on 20 November that the app now blocks screenshots on iOS 27, under a setting named "Block Siri AI," and on Android turns on "Screen security" automatically when it detects an AI assistant trying to read the screen. It cannot reliably block screenshots on macOS. "Our remedy is also incomplete," Whittaker writes, and she asks Apple, Google and Microsoft for a documented way for apps to opt out of AI access to screens, files, notifications and APIs.9

Technology

BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury

The ESA-JAXA probe entered orbit on Saturday 21 November, eight years after its 2018 launch, and is the first spacecraft in Mercury orbit since NASA's MESSENGER ended in 2015, WRAL reports. ESA Operations posted that Flight Dynamics "already has confirmed it achieved orbital capture around Mercury!"10 The Qatar News Agency says ESA announced the insertion.11 The two orbiters separate on 9 December, and the science phase starts on 6 April 2027.10
