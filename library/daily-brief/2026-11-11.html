Word of the Day
phatic adjective /ˈfa-tik/

Of, relating to, or being speech used for social or emotive purposes rather than for communicating information.1

The anthropologist Bronislaw Malinowski coined “phatic communion” in 1923, in Supplement I of C. K. Ogden and I. A. Richards’ The Meaning of Meaning, for talk like “Nice day to-day” that eases the tension of facing someone in silence.2 He owned up in the coining sentence: “phatic communion I am tempted to call it, actuated by the demon of terminological invention.”2 Merriam-Webster dates the word to 1922, but the book’s preface is signed January 1923 and says Malinowski’s supplement came in while the book was “passing through the press”.12

News

Massie’s resolution to impeach Hegseth goes to Judiciary, 211-204

The House voted 211-204 to refer Thomas Massie’s resolution impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, with 210 Republicans and independent Kevin Kiley in favor and every voting Democrat against.34 Massie was the only Republican to vote no, and CBS reports the referral effectively kills the resolution.4

News

October CPI rises 0.4%, with no annual rate to set beside it

Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October after 0.6% in September, and core prices rose 0.3% for a third month, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported.5 BLS collected no October 2025 data during that month’s lapse in appropriations, so it “cannot publish 12-month percent changes for October 2026” for the affected indexes.5 Both monthly figures ran a tenth of a point above forecasts, and CNBC reports traders now fully price a quarter-point hike at the Fed’s 8-9 December meeting.6

News

FDA adds an eye-risk warning to every GLP-1 drug

The FDA is requiring every GLP-1 receptor agonist, Ozempic and Zepbound among them, to carry a warning of a potential increased risk of non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy, which can cause sudden vision loss in one eye.7 A meta-analysis of 126 trials found 6.4 cases per 100,000 person-years on the drugs against 3.8 on comparators. In FDA’s Sentinel data the hazard ratio was 1.43 against DPP-4 inhibitors and a nonsignificant 1.19 against SGLT-2 inhibitors.7

Technology

DeepMind machine-checks Signal’s compiled code in Lean

Google DeepMind says its AlphaProof Code agents proved forward secrecy, authentication, post-compromise security and handshake secrecy for libsignal-protocol v0.105.4 and spqr v1.6.0 as stock rustc compiles them to AArch64, in about 6.5 million lines of Lean checked against about 26,000 lines of trusted specification and semantics.8 The symbolic proofs assume perfect cryptography and the computational ones hold only against classical attackers, and neither covers key generation.8 The authors report five people working four weeks, about 20 person-weeks, against the 20 person-years they cite for the seL4 kernel.8

Technology

Australia moves to bind frontier AI companies by law

Australia will legislate six obligations for frontier AI companies operating there, among them allowing “independent evaluation of their models” and rapid incident notification, enforced by a proposed regulator with “significant penalties”.9 It accepted all 18 recommendations of a rapid review into “the OpenAI agent infiltration” of government websites, among them a Medicare statistics portal. It calls OpenAI’s disclosure “inadequate”, and one recommendation would make AI companies report “misaligned behaviour or cyber incidents”.10
