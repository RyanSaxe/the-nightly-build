Word of the Day
ecdysiast noun /ek-ˈdi-zē-ˌast/

A stripteaser. 1

Georgia Sothern, a striptease performer from Baltimore, wrote to H. L. Mencken asking him to coin “a new and more palatable word” for her trade. His reply of 5 April 1940 rejected “moltician” for its likeness to “mortician” and went on: “A resort to the scientific name for molting, which is ecdysis, produces both ecdysist and ecdysiast.” 2 Merriam-Webster credits Mencken with the coinage and dates its first use to 1940. 1 Reviews of the musical Gypsy have said he made the word for Gypsy Rose Lee, but Michael Quinion of World Wide Words reports that Lee hated it and called Mencken “an intellectual slob”. 2

News

Newsom will commute all 548 California death sentences

Governor Gavin Newsom announced on 5 November that he will commute the sentences of all 548 people on California’s death row, which he calls the largest in the Western Hemisphere, to life without parole. 3 For the 382 of them convicted of more than one felony, the state constitution requires a recommendation from the California Supreme Court with at least four justices concurring, and his explanatory statement asks the court for it. The statement does not say whether the other 166 commutations have taken effect. 4

Nasdaq falls 5.52 percent, its worst day since April 2025

News

Judge Chutkan orders a 14-day halt to work on Trump’s arch

Late on Thursday 5 November, federal District Court Judge Tanya Chutkan barred the government from any construction on President Trump’s 250-foot arch for 14 days, as the governments of D.C. and Virginia had asked. Her order came less than two hours after the National Capital Planning Commission voted 8-4 to give the arch final approval. Officials have said the work would run 20 hours a day, year-round, for two to three years, and Chutkan agreed that “the traffic disruptions will occur immediately.” 7

Technology

OpenAI rates GPT-6 Cyber Critical and limits it to approved customers

OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber on Thursday 5 November, at first only through the API and Codex to approved Daybreak Red customers, those authorized for offensive security work, The Register reports. OpenAI classed the model Critical, the highest cyber tier in its Preparedness Framework. Every figure is OpenAI’s claim: 48.5 percent on its ExploitGym evaluation against 32.1 percent for GPT-5.6 Cyber, and 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities, which OpenAI says Mozilla has started fixing. The Register reports no outside testing. 8

Technology

Shanghai Jiao Tong University’s AI agents run a DNA laboratory

AutoDNA, a DNA laboratory described in Nature on 4 November, orchestrates over 20 types of instruments across more than 11,000 steps, with human intervention “limited to hardware maintenance tasks” such as refilling reagents. The authors report that it matched expert-optimized enzymatic DNA synthesis yields, ran concurrent protocols 3.2-fold faster, and stored digital content in DNA and recovered it exactly. Milad Abolhasani, a chemical engineer at North Carolina State University, was one of the paper’s peer reviewers. 9 He says the system is remarkable for its degree of autonomy and for how readily it takes in varied lab equipment, Nature’s news section reports. 10
