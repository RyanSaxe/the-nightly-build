Word of the Day
eggcorn noun /ˈɛɡˌkɔːrn/

A word or phrase that sounds like another and is mistakenly used in a seemingly logical or plausible way in its place. 1

On 23 September 2003 the linguist Mark Liberman wrote on Language Log about a woman who had typed “egg corns” for “acorns.” Geoffrey K. Pullum then suggested a name for such slips, following the pattern of “mondegreen,” and Liberman’s update of 30 September carried it; the Oxford English Dictionary cites that update as its first evidence. The coinage is an instance of its own meaning, since “eggcorn” is an eggcorn for “acorn.” 12

News

Two federal courts rule against DOJ’s California ballot challenge in a single day

U.S. District Judge Trina Thompson ordered on 30 October that Justice Department and Homeland Security officials may not threaten, intimidate or coerce election officials into halting a voter’s ballot solely because the administration calls the voter ineligible. The order runs through 16 November. 3 The Ninth Circuit ruled 2-1 the same day against the department’s request that California set aside ballots from more than 20,000 people it alleges are noncitizens. 4 CBS reported that the department also issued grand-jury subpoenas to Los Angeles County election officials between 21 and 24 October. 5

News

Third-quarter growth hits 3.4% as September prices run 3.6% above last year

The Bureau of Economic Analysis put third-quarter real GDP growth at a 3.4 percent annual rate on 29 October, up from 2.2 percent in the second quarter. 6 Its release the same day showed the PCE price index for September 3.6 percent above a year earlier. 7 The GDP figure is an advance estimate that BEA will revise, and both numbers came five days before the midterm elections.

News

Veterans pay $150 more a month after VA ends loan-modification program, NPR finds

NPR’s analysis of VA data finds that more than 15,000 veterans with modified loans now pay at least $150 more a month since the department ended its VA Servicing Purchase program on 1 May 2025. 8 NPR reports that 63 percent of VA modifications raised the payment, against 2 percent of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac modifications. Congress has since passed a replacement, which lenders have until 28 November to implement.

Technology

Anthropic engineer’s draft would let robots.txt opt a site out of AI training

Kevin Kelley of Anthropic submitted an Internet-Draft on 30 October proposing a __usage_ai-training token that sites name in a robots.txt User-Agent line, where Disallow expresses a preference against training use. 9 It is an individual draft with no IETF standing, and a wildcard User-Agent: * group does not count as an opt-out. Google says it aims to support such a shared control in 2027. 10

Technology

Mistral publishes weights for a trillion-parameter Large 4

Mistral released the open weights of Large 4, a model of about 1 trillion parameters, on 29 October. 11 The license is a modified MIT license, and businesses running it as a hosted service need a separate commercial license from Mistral. Its reported 41 percent on Terminal-Bench 4.0 is Mistral’s own figure. 12
