Word of the Day
eucatastrophe noun /ˌjuːkəˈtæstɹəfi/

A plot-resolving catastrophe that ends in the protagonist's well-being.1

J. R. R. Tolkien coined it as "the good catastrophe", the sudden upward turn that reverses a story's downward one, set against the catastrophe of Greek drama.2 It belongs to "On Fairy-Stories", which grew from the Andrew Lang Lecture he gave at St Andrews on 8 March 1939 and was printed, revised and expanded, in 1947.3 Wiktionary dates the coinage to a 1944 letter, but the letter's "I coined the word" does not say when.1 The essay's epilogue applies it to the Gospels: "The Birth of Christ is the eucatastrophe of Man's history."3

News

Jeffries is "a hard yes" on impeaching Hegseth

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries told ABC's This Week on Sunday that he is "a hard yes" on impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, whom he accused of abusing power and undermining national security. Hegseth has called the eight articles brought by Rep. Thomas Massie, an outgoing Kentucky Republican, a "sham", and President Trump said, "I have every intention to keep him." Massie plans to reintroduce the resolution as privileged as early as Monday, and once he raises it on the floor, leaders must schedule a vote within two legislative days.4

News

Nvidia's board investigates Jensen Huang

Nvidia's board said on Sunday that it has opened an investigation, overseen by a committee of independent directors, into reports that CEO Jensen Huang "has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort", and that it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised.5 Huang admitted an affair in an email to staff, and the New York Times and the Financial Times report that US intelligence agencies believe the woman, Alice Huang, passed information to Beijing.67 She is linked to Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer accused of helping restricted Chinese firms obtain Nvidia chips.7

News

Herzog gives Eisenkot the first try at forming Israel's government

Israeli President Isaac Herzog tasked Gadi Eisenkot, who chairs the Yashar party, with forming a government on Sunday, after 67 of the Knesset's 120 members recommended him and 46 recommended Benjamin Netanyahu. Eisenkot has 28 days, extendable by up to 14, and has ruled out partnering with Netanyahu. His own change bloc holds 48 seats, 13 short of a majority, so he depends on parties outside it that recommended him.8

Technology

Thinking Machines holds Inkling 2 Small's weights for safety testing

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Small on 5 November, a mixture-of-experts model with 594B total and 17B active parameters, on the lab's Tinker service with closed weights. The lab said it would publish the weights under Apache 2.0 "next week", pending safety evaluations "by us and our partners", and conceded the model "still trails closed-weights models" on its alignment evaluations.9

Technology

Ricursive ships a 65B dense model trained from scratch

Ricursive Intelligence released Ripple-65B Preview on 6 November, an open-weight 65B dense Transformer pretrained from scratch on 12T tokens. Ricursive chose a dense design over mixture-of-experts for simpler training and serving, and reports 80.52 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, which it calls the best score for a US open-weight model. It puts the full research and development cost at $32M.10
