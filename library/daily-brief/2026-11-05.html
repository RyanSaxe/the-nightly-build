Word of the Day
sphexish adjective /ˈsfɛksɪʃ/

Of behaviour: following a fixed, preprogrammed routine that looks intelligent and runs unchanged when the situation changes.12

Douglas Hofstadter coined it in 1982, in a Scientific American column later collected in Metamagical Themas (1985), for the digger wasp Sphex.12 In Dean Wooldridge’s telling, which Hofstadter quoted, the wasp leaves its paralysed cricket on the threshold to inspect the burrow. Move the cricket a few inches and the wasp drags it back and inspects again, “forty times, always with the same result.”2 Jean-Henri Fabre’s 1879 original also describes a colony that caught on after two or three tries, and Wooldridge’s own source recorded an American Sphex that did the same.2

News

Vance pins the midterm losses on the war as Brent drops below $95

Vice President JD Vance wrote on X on Wednesday, “The reason we lost is the war,” and dared anyone who thought “losing the Senate for the next 6 years was worth it” to say so. Trump told the Wall Street Journal that “Iran was a piece of it” and that he “would have done it again.”3 By early afternoon Brent crude futures were down 8.6% at $94.66 a barrel, a drop OilPrice.com put down to traders pricing an end to the war.4

News

Four people on Johnson’s staff call say he will not run for House Republican leader

Speaker Mike Johnson told staff on a Wednesday call that he will not run when House Republicans choose a leader on 10 November, four people on the call told NBC News.5 Two people familiar with his decision told AP the same, and he has made no on-record statement. AP expects him to keep his seat as a rank-and-file member.6 Majority Leader Steve Scalise and Judiciary Committee Chairman Jim Jordan are running.5

News

CVS Health plans to split its pharmacies from Aetna and Caremark

About 9,000 CVS pharmacies, the mail pharmacy and MinuteClinic will become a separate public company, CVS Pharmacy, led by Group President Prem Shah, CVS Health said in an SEC filing. Aetna, CVS Caremark, CVS Specialty, Oak Street Health and Signify Health stay with the healthcare company under Chairman and CEO David Joyner. CVS expects to finish in the first half of 2028, pending final board approval, an effective Form 10 and confirmation of tax-free treatment.7

Technology

Attackers exploit a WordPress flaw that opens only mid-upgrade

CISA added CVE-2026-119977 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 3 November. It is a time-of-check time-of-use race in WordPress core that lets an unauthenticated attacker execute code through “predictable, web-accessible staging paths” during a full-package core upgrade. CISA names no attacker.8 WordPress fixed it in 7.1.4 on 28 October, with backports to every branch back to 4.7 under way, and sites on automatic background updates get the fix on their own.9

Technology

Google makes schools opt in to Gemini and Search for students

Schools that want Google Search for K-12 students will have to take it with its AI features. Gemini products and Search will be “entirely off for K-12 student accounts until school administrators explicitly turn them on,” Google said.10 Chris Phillips, its VP and general manager for education, told the New York Times that “Search and AI are deeply integrated.”11 Google later said schools can turn on Search without Gemini, though Search still carries AI summaries.12
