Word of the Day
palimpsest noun /ˈpæləmpˌsɛst/ (PAL-imp-sest)

A manuscript page reused after its earlier writing was erased or obscured; also, something bearing visible layers from its past.1

Greek palimpsēstos joined palin (“again”) to psēstos (“rubbed smooth”), a name for writing scraped away so a page could serve again.2 The British Library describes a tenth-century Syriac manuscript whose reused pages preserve two older Latin texts, including fragments of the historian Granius Licinianus.3

News

The Supreme Court lets third-country removals resume

On September 29, the Court stayed a lower-court order requiring advance notice and a chance to raise fear-of-persecution or torture claims before removal to a third country.456 It also granted the government’s petition for review.4 The emergency order keeps the policy operating during litigation. It does not decide legality on the merits.4 Reuters says human-rights groups count more than 25,000 removals to 29 countries. That is an advocacy tally, not a Court finding.56

Consumer confidence falls to 81.9 in September

The Conference Board’s index fell 6.7 points to 81.9 from August’s 88.6.78 Present Situation fell 7.9 points to 109.3. Expectations fell 5.9 points to 63.6, its third monthly decline.7 The index uses a 1985 average of 100 and records survey responses, not purchases or a forecast.7 AP says write-in responses were collected September 1–23.89

Six technology firms sign a voluntary White House AI accord

Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI and Nvidia signed the accord.1011 It calls for internal model monitoring, an internal review team, independent external evaluation and board review of reports.10 The text names no penalty, enforcement process, public-reporting duty or deadline.1012 It says, “Over time, it may make sense to codify these steps into laws and regulations.” That describes a possible future, not a current legal obligation.10

DeepSeek releases code for Huawei Ascend chips

Technology

Microsoft makes WSL containers generally available

On September 29, Microsoft moved WSL containers from preview to general availability.1516 Developers can update WSL and use wslc.exe or the container.exe alias to build, run and deploy Linux containers. Microsoft also documents an API for Windows apps.15 The release adds Intune policy controls and Defender for Endpoint visibility.15 Microsoft claims Windows-file access can be “up to 2x” faster without stating test conditions. Help Net Security says Docker Compose remains a requested gap.1517
