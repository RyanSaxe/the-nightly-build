Word of the Day
panjandrum noun /pan-ˈjan-drəm/

A powerful personage or pretentious official.1

Its source reached print in 1825 as a run of gibberish ending “the grand Panjandrum himself, with the little round button at top” that a father recites in Maria Edgeworth’s Harry and Lucy Concluded.2 Edgeworth credits it to “Mr. Foote”, who wrote it to test “a man, who boasted that he could learn any thing by rote, on once hearing it.”2 Merriam-Webster, citing the Oxford English Dictionary, names the playwright Samuel Foote and makes the boaster the actor Charles Macklin, but Word Histories traces that version no further back than an 1854 printing.13 By 1840 it named a person: The Essex Standard reported that John Thorogood “was dubbed the great Panjandrum, or Chairman.”3

News

United Fidelity Bank of Evansville fails

The Comptroller of the Currency closed United Fidelity Bank of Evansville, Indiana, on 27 November, and the FDIC sold substantially all its deposits and assets to First-Citizens Bank & Trust, at an estimated cost of $1.83 billion to the Deposit Insurance Fund.4 It is the seventh US bank failure of 2026, and at about $5.9 billion in assets it is eight times the size of the next largest, Nano Banc, at $736 million.456 Its 22 branches reopen as First Citizens branches.4

News

Reuters reports a breach of the Air Force’s Cloud One platform

Hackers breached Cloud One, an Air Force cloud computing platform, Reuters reported on 27 November, citing three people familiar with the matter.7 The platform hosts the North American air-defense command-and-control system and a munitions tracker used at more than 200 Air Force locations.7 Reuters could not determine who the intruders were or whether they took data, and the Air Force declined to comment.7

News

Euro-area leaders choose Pablo Hernández de Cos to run the ECB

Euro-area leaders on 26 November backed Pablo Hernández de Cos, the former Bank of Spain governor who now runs the Bank for International Settlements, to succeed Christine Lagarde, who leaves on 7 February 2027, as European Central Bank president for a single eight-year term.8 The leaders also nominated Laurence Boone and Markus Brunnermeier to the ECB’s Executive Board.9 The European Parliament and the ECB Governing Council, neither with a veto, must be consulted before the appointment is formal.89

Technology

1Password traces a backend breach to an employee’s GitHub account

1Password said on 25 November that an attacker used an employee’s GitHub account to push malicious code to its backend.10 The code downloaded names, email addresses, public keys, Secure Remote Password (SRP) parameters and some invitation and recovery tokens for a subset of users, and 1Password says no vault contents were exposed.10 The Cloud Security Alliance notes that the disclosure does not say whether SRP verifiers, which allow offline password guessing, were taken, though 1Password’s 128-bit Secret Key would make that guessing infeasible for accounts protected by one.11

Technology

Hera finds DART flattened a whole face of Dimorphos

ESA’s Hera spacecraft has returned its first close images of the asteroid Dimorphos, showing that NASA’s 2022 DART impact flattened the struck face into a crater “the size of a sports stadium” spanning the 151-metre moonlet’s entire width.12 The rest of the surface looks as DART saw it, but Dimorphos now tumbles in its orbit, and hundreds of previously unknown boulders circle it and its parent asteroid, Didymos.12
