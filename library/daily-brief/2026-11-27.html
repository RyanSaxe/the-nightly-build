Word of the Day
smellfungus noun \ˈsmel-ˌfəŋ-gəs\

A captious critic, or faultfinder. 1

Laurence Sterne coined it in A Sentimental Journey through France and Italy (1768) for “the learned Smelfungus,” a traveler who set out “with the spleen and jaundice, and every object he passed by was discoloured or distorted.” 2 Sterne aimed the character at Tobias Smollett and his Travels through France and Italy (1766), 1 and Smollett is “immortalized, however unhappily for him,” as the learned Smelfungus. 3 When Smelfungus vows at Turin to tell the world his miseries, the narrator answers: “You had better tell it, said I, to your physician.” 2

News

USTR proposes tariffs of up to 35 percent on 14 economies

US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer found on 25 November that 14 economies’ practices on structural excess capacity in manufacturing burden or restrict US commerce and are actionable under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974. He proposed tariffs from 15 percent on the European Union, Japan, Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan to up to 35 percent on Vietnam, with China at up to 20 percent net of an existing forced-labor duty. 4 Mexico and Norway, also investigated since 11 March, got no determination. 4 5 Comments are due 5 January 2027, and hearings open 25 January. 4

News

A judge halts the FCC’s early review of ABC’s station licenses

On 25 November Judge Loren L. AliKhan of the federal district court in Washington preliminarily enjoined the FCC’s early license renewal proceedings against ABC’s eight stations, none due for renewal before 2028. The FCC opened them on 28 April, the day after President Trump posted that “Jimmy Kimmel should be immediately fired.” AliKhan found that Chairman Brendan Carr “has taken President Trump’s rhetoric as his marching orders.” She declined a broader order against coercion and set ABC’s bond at $1. 6

News

Software price revisions help lift the third-quarter growth estimate to 4.3 percent

The Bureau of Economic Analysis’s second estimate, released 25 November, puts third-quarter real GDP growth at a 4.3 percent annual rate, up from 3.4 percent in the advance estimate. Investment and consumer spending drove the revision. Software led the investment revision, mainly because the Bureau of Labor Statistics revised its producer price index for software publishing. 7

Technology

The FCC licenses radio links for SpaceX’s million-satellite data-center constellation

The FCC’s Space Bureau on 25 November authorized SpaceX to deploy Starmind, 1,000,000 satellites operating as data centers at altitudes of 500 to 1,002 km, 8 which the agency calls the first such US authorization. 9 The grant’s legal scope is radio links for backup and emergency telemetry, tracking and command, supplementing Starmind’s optical links. The bureau deferred SpaceX’s request to waive the rule requiring disposal by atmospheric re-entry, saying SpaceX “has not provided sufficient information,” and the authorization lapses unless one satellite is operating by 25 November 2033. 8

Technology

OpenAI claims the matrix multiplication exponent is exactly 9/4

In a preprint dated 24 November, OpenAI claims the exponent of square matrix multiplication is 9/4, or 2.25, over every field, where schoolbook multiplication gives 3. It also claims formal arithmetic circuits need at least ⌈n9/4/2⌉ nonscalar multiplication or division gates at every side length n ≥ 2. Its Lean formalization covers the 9/4 exponent only for division-free circuits computing formal polynomial identities, and the preprint has not been peer reviewed. 10
